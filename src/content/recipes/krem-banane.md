---
slug: krem-banane
title:
  sr: Krem banane
  en: Homemade Cream Bananas
category: kremovi-sladoledi
tags:
- banane
- cokolada
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 800 g šećera
  - 2 dcl vode
  - 4 lista (30-40 g) želatina
  - 1 dcl hladne vode
  - ekstrakt od banane
  - 200 g čokolade za glazuru
  en:
  - 800 g sugar
  - 2 dl water
  - 4 sheets (30–40 g) gelatine
  - 1 dl cold water
  - banana extract
  - 200 g chocolate for coating
steps:
  sr:
  - Šećer i vodu kuvati 5 minuta da vri.
  - Želatin rastopiti u hladnoj vodi.
  - Kuvani šećer mutiti mikserom čim se skloni sa šporeta pa dodati želatin.
  - Mutiti dok se ne stegne i pobeli pa dodati ekstrakt banane.
  - Na foliju u plehu razliti pola čokolade pa fil pa ponovo čokoladu.
  en:
  - Boil the sugar and water for 5 minutes.
  - Dissolve the gelatine in the cold water.
  - Beat the hot syrup with a mixer as soon as it's off the heat then add the gelatine.
  - Beat until thick and white then add the banana extract.
  - Spread half the chocolate on foil in a tin then the filling then chocolate again.
