title:
  sr: Posni kolač s pomorandžom i rozen koricama
  en: Lenten Orange Wafer Cake
category: bez-pecenja
tags:

ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 400 g šećera
  - 3 dl vode
  - 1 posni margarin
  - 200 g čokolade
  - 200 g mlevenog keksa
  - 200 g mlevenih oraha
  - 1 smrznuta pomorandža (samlevena)
  - rozen korice
  - glazura od čokolade
  en:
  - 400 g sugar
  - 3 dl water
  - 1 vegan margarine block
  - 200 g chocolate
  - 200 g ground biscuits
  - 200 g ground walnuts
  - 1 frozen orange (minced)
  - thin wafer sheets
  - chocolate glaze
steps:
  sr:
  - Šećer i vodu da provri pa skloniti i dodati margarin i čokoladu.
  - Dodati keks, orahe i pomorandžu.
  - Kad se fil prohladi filovati korice (ako je redak dodati keksa).
  - Odozgo glazura od čokolade.
  en:
  - Bring the sugar and water to a boil then remove and add the margarine and chocolate.
  - Add the biscuits, walnuts and orange.
  - When it cools fill the wafer sheets (add more biscuits if runny).
  - Finish with chocolate glaze.
