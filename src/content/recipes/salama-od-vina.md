  en: Wine Walnut Log
category: bez-pecenja
tags:

ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 200 g šećera
  - 1 kafena šoljica vina
  - 200 g mlevenih oraha
  - 50 g seckanih oraha
  - glazura od čokolade
  en:
  - 200 g sugar
  - 1 coffee cup wine
  - 200 g ground walnuts
  - 50 g chopped walnuts
  - chocolate glaze
steps:
  sr:
  - Šećer i vino da provri.
  - Dodati mlevene i seckane orahe i oblikovati salamu.
  - Odozgo glazura od čokolade.
  en:
  - Bring the sugar and wine to a boil.
  - Add the ground and chopped walnuts and shape a log.
  - Cover with chocolate glaze.
