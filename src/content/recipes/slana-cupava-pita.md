ingredients:
- group:

  sr:
  - 4 jaja (odvojena)
  - malo soli
  - 1 jogurt
  - 1 prašak za pecivo
  - 12 kašika brašna
  - 3 kašike ajvara
  en:
  - 4 eggs (separated)
  - a little salt
  - 1 tub yogurt
  - 1 baking powder
  - 12 tbsp flour
  - 3 tbsp ajvar
- group:
    sr: Preko
    en: Topping
  sr:
  - 6 kašika majoneza
  - 3 kuvana jaja
  - 150 g šunke ili pečenice
  - 3 krastavčića
  - sir
  en:
  - 6 tbsp mayonnaise
  - 3 boiled eggs
  - 150 g ham
  - 3 gherkins
  - cheese
steps:
  sr:
  - Posebno umutiti belanca i žumanca pa sjediniti sa solju jogurtom praškom brašnom i ajvarom; ispeći.
  - Ohlađenu koru premazati sa 3 kašike majoneza.
  - Narendati jaja šunku i krastavčiće pa opet 3 kašike majoneza.
  - Pred služenje narendati sir.
  en:
  - Whisk the whites and yolks separately then combine with the salt yogurt baking powder flour and ajvar; bake.
  - Spread 3 tbsp mayonnaise over the cooled base.
  - Grate over the eggs ham and gherkins then another 3 tbsp mayonnaise.
  - Grate cheese on top just before serving.
