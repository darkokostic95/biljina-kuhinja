title:
  sr: Sunce pogača
  en: Sunflower Bread
category: peciva
tags:

sources:
- IMG_5645

ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Testo
    en: Dough
  sr:
  - 250 g mleka
  - 250 g vode
  - 3 kašičice šećera
  - 2 kašike sirćeta
  - 2 kašičice soli
  - kvasac
  - 2 jaja (odvojiti žumance)
  - 1 kg brašna
  - zejtin
  - susam
  en:
  - 250 g milk
  - 250 g water
  - 3 tsp sugar
  - 2 tbsp vinegar
  - 2 tsp salt
  - yeast
  - 2 eggs (keep one yolk)
  - 1 kg flour
  - oil
  - sesame
steps:
  sr:
  - Zamesiti testo i posle 10 minuta premesiti; ostaviti da nadođe.
  - Napraviti 3 veće i 3 manje lopte.
  - Veće lopte razviti u kore veličine tepsije — prve dve premazati zejtinom a treću ne.
  - Složiti kore podeliti na 8 delova i uviti kiflice sa vrhom ka sredini tepsije.
  - Male lopte razviti premazati zejtinom uviti u rolate i od tri rolata isplesti pletenicu za sredinu.
  - Premazati žumancetom posuti susamom i kad nadođe ispeći (tepsija 32 cm).
  en:
  - Knead the dough knock it back after 10 minutes and let rise.
  - Make 3 large and 3 small balls.
  - Roll the large balls to the size of the tin — oil the first two but not the third.
  - Stack them cut into 8 and roll crescents pointing to the centre of the tin.
  - Roll the small balls oil them roll up and braid the three ropes for the centre.
  - Brush with yolk sprinkle with sesame and bake once risen (32 cm tin).
