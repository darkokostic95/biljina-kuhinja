title:
  sr: Kokos štangle
  en: Coconut Bars
category: bez-pecenja
tags:
- bez-pecenja
- kokos
- keks
- orasi
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Tamni deo
    en: Dark layer
  sr:
  - 1 jogurtska čaša vode
  - 1 jogurtska čaša šećera
  - 300 g mlevenog keksa
  - 200 g mlevenih oraha
  - 1/4 margarina
  - 2 kašike kakaa
  en:
  - 1 yogurt glass water
  - 1 yogurt glass sugar
  - 300 g ground biscuits
  - 200 g ground walnuts
  - 1/4 margarine block
  - 2 tbsp cocoa
- group:
    sr: Beli deo
    en: White layer
  sr:
  - 1 jogurtska čaša vode
  - 1 jogurtska čaša šećera
  - 5-6 kašika griza
  - 150 g kokosa
  - 1/4 margarina
  - kokos za posipanje
  en:
  - 1 yogurt glass water
  - 1 yogurt glass sugar
  - 5-6 tbsp semolina
  - 150 g coconut
  - 1/4 margarine block
  - coconut for sprinkling
steps:
  sr:
  - Vodu i šećer da provri pa dodati keks, orahe, margarin i kakao.
  - Za beli deo skuvati vodu, šećer i griz pa dodati kokos i margarin.
  - Prvo rastanjiti tamni deo pa preko beli i posuti kokosom.
  en:
  - Bring the water and sugar to a boil then add the biscuits, walnuts, margarine and cocoa.
  - For the white layer cook the water, sugar and semolina then add the coconut and margarine.
  - Spread the dark layer then the white one on top and sprinkle with coconut.
notes:
  sr: 'Druga verzija (IMG_5697): 300 g šećera i 1 šoljica vode ukuvati, dodati 125 g putera, 200 g mlevenog keksa i 100 g kokosa.'
  en: 'Another version (IMG_5697): boil 300 g sugar with 1 small cup water, add 125 g butter, 200 g ground biscuits and 100 g coconut.'
