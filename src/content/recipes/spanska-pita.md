    sr: Sastojci

  sr:
  - 4 jaja
  - 1 i po čaša brašna
  - 1/2 čaše zejtina
  - 1 čaša mleka
  - 1 prašak za pecivo
  - 150 g salame
  - 150 g kačkavalja
  - 150 g krastavčića
  en:
  - 4 eggs
  - 1.5 glasses flour
  - 1/2 glass oil
  - 1 glass milk
  - 1 baking powder
  - 150 g salami
  - 150 g kashkaval cheese
  - 150 g gherkins
steps:
  sr:
  - Jaja umutiti mikserom.
  - Dodati brašno zejtin mleko i prašak.
  - Umešati seckanu salamu kačkavalj i krastavčiće.
  - Sipati u pleh i ispeći.
  en:
  - Beat the eggs with a mixer.
  - Add the flour oil milk and baking powder.
  - Fold in the chopped salami cheese and gherkins.
  - Pour into a tin and bake.

  en: 'Cornmeal version (IMG_5673): 2 eggs, 1 yogurt, 1 small cup oil, 1 tbsp salt, 2.5 small cups each of cornmeal and wheat flour, 1 baking powder, 2.5 small cups milk, a lump of cheese, chopped salami and 2 tbsp ajvar.'
