title:
  sr: Bela Milka torta
  en: White Milka Cake
category: torte
tags:
- cokolada
sources:
- IMG_5557
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Korice (×4)
    en: Sponges (×4)
  sr:
  - 4 belanca
  - 4 kašike šećera
  - 1 kašičica limunovog soka
  - 1 kašika brašna
  - 50 g bele čokolade
  en:
  - 4 egg whites
  - 4 tbsp sugar
  - 1 tsp lemon juice
  - 1 tbsp flour
  - 50 g white chocolate
- group:
    sr: Fil
    en: Filling
  sr:
  - 1 l mleka
  - 16 žumanaca
  - 12 kašika brašna
  - 24 kašike šećera
  - 300 g bele čokolade
  - 2 margarina
  - 300 g šlag kreme
  en:
  - 1 l milk
  - 16 egg yolks
  - 12 tbsp flour
  - 24 tbsp sugar
  - 300 g white chocolate
  - 2 margarine blocks
  - 300 g whipped topping
steps:
  sr:
  - Umutiti belanca sa šećerom pa dodati limun i brašno i narendati belu čokoladu; ispeći 4 korice.
  - U mleku skuvati žumanca, brašno i šećer; u vreo fil dodati belu čokoladu.
  - Kad se potpuno ohladi dodati posebno umućene margarine.
  - 'Filovati: kora, fil, šlag — ponoviti — odozgo šlag.'
  en:
  - Whisk the whites with the sugar then add the lemon and flour and grate in the white chocolate; bake 4 sponges.
  - Cook the yolks, flour and sugar in the milk; stir the white chocolate into the hot filling.
  - When completely cool add the separately creamed margarine.
  - 'Layer: sponge, filling, whipped cream — repeat — cream on top.'
