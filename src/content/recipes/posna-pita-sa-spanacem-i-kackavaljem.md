ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 600 g brašna
  - 1 kašika soli
  - pola šoljice zejtina
  - malo sirćeta
  - voda po potrebi
  - margarin za rendanje
  - kačkavalj (posni)
  - blitva ili spanać
  en:
  - 600 g flour
  - 1 tbsp salt
  - half a small cup oil
  - a little vinegar
  - water as needed
  - margarine for grating
  - cheese (vegan for Lent)
  - chard or spinach
steps:
  sr:
  - Zamesiti testo i razvući koru pa narendati margarin.
  - Preklopiti i ostaviti pola sata; posle na svakih pola sata ponovo preklopiti bez margarina.
  - Testo podeliti na 2 dela — veći deo staviti u tepsiju i po zidovima.
  - Ređati kačkavalj pa blitvu ili spanać pa opet kačkavalj.
  - Pokriti drugim delom testa i ivice preklopiti preko prvog.
  - Iseći na kocke i zamrznuti; peći kao žužu po potrebi.
  en:
  - Knead the dough, roll it out and grate margarine over it.
  - Fold and rest half an hour; then fold again every half hour without margarine.
  - Divide in 2 — line the tin and its sides with the larger piece.
  - Layer cheese then chard or spinach then cheese again.
  - Cover with the second piece and fold the edges over the first.
  - Cut into squares and freeze; bake as needed.
