slug: stanglice-u-oblandi
title:
  sr: Štanglice u oblandi
  en: Fruit and Coconut Wafer Bars
category: bez-pecenja
tags:
- bez-pecenja
- oblanda
- kokos
- keks
- orasi
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 200 g mleka
  - 250 g šećera
  - 1 margarin
  - 300 g mlevenog keksa
  - 200 g suvog grožđa
  - 100 g kokosa
  - 2 kašike seckanih oraha
  - 2 lista oblande
  en:
  - 200 g milk
  - 250 g sugar
  - 1 margarine block
  - 300 g ground biscuits
  - 200 g raisins
  - 100 g coconut
  - 2 tbsp chopped walnuts
  - 2 wafer sheets
steps:
  sr:
  - Mleko i šećer kuvati pa dodati margarin.
  - Kad malo provri dodati keks, grožđe, kokos i orahe.
  - Dok je masa topla rasporediti po oblandi i pokriti drugim listom.
  - Ostaviti da odstoji pa seći.
  en:
  - Heat the milk and sugar then add the margarine.
  - After a short boil add the biscuits, raisins, coconut and walnuts.
  - Spread while warm on a wafer sheet and cover with the second sheet.
  - Leave to set then cut.
