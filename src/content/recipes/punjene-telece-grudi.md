ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci

  sr:
  - 1 i po kg telećih grudi bez kostiju
  - 200 g šampinjona
  - debela kriška belog hleba
  - 2 glavice crnog luka
  - 6 kašika mleka
  - 1 žumance
  - 1 čaša kisele pavlake
  - peršun
  - ulje
  - so
  - biber
  en:
  - 1.5 kg boneless veal breast
  - 200 g mushrooms
  - a thick slice of white bread
  - 2 onions
  - 6 tbsp milk
  - 1 egg yolk
  - 1 glass sour cream
  - parsley
  - oil
  - salt
  - pepper
steps:
  sr:
  - Na ulju prodinstati luk i šampinjone pa dodati hleb natopljen mlekom so biber i peršun.
  - Smesu staviti na meso uviti u rolat i uvezati.
  - Peći u rerni na 200 °C oko 2 sata.
  - U sok od pečenja umešati pavlaku sa žumancetom i preliti meso.
  en:
  - Sauté the onion and mushrooms then add the milk-soaked bread salt pepper and parsley.
  - Spread over the meat roll up and tie.
  - Roast at 200 °C for about 2 hours.
  - Stir the sour cream and yolk into the pan juices and pour over the meat.
