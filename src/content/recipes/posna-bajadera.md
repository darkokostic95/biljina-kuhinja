  sr: Posna bajadera
  en: Lenten Bajadera
category: kolaci
tags:
- posno
- orasi
- keks
- cokolada
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Kora
    en: Base
  sr:
  - 2 šoljice ulja
  - 8 šoljica brašna
  - 6 šoljica šećera
  - 4 šoljice kisele vode
  - 4 kašičice kakaa
  - 1 prašak za pecivo
  - 2 vanilin šećera
  - 10 kašika mlevenih oraha
  - 10 kašika seckanih oraha
  en:
  - 2 small cups oil
  - 8 small cups flour
  - 6 small cups sugar
  - 4 small cups sparkling water
  - 4 tsp cocoa
  - 1 baking powder
  - 2 vanilla sugar sachets
  - 10 tbsp ground walnuts
  - 10 tbsp chopped walnuts
- group:
    sr: Fil
    en: Filling
  sr:
  - 250 g šećera u prahu
  - 1 posni margarin
  - 300 g mlevenog posnog keksa
  - 300 g sojinog mleka
  - 200 g mlevenih oraha
  - 200 g hladnog sojinog mleka
  en:
  - 250 g powdered sugar
  - 1 vegan margarine block
  - 300 g ground vegan biscuits
  - 300 g soy milk
  - 200 g ground walnuts
  - 200 g cold soy milk
- group:
    sr: Preliv
    en: Glaze
  sr:
  - 200 g čokolade
  - 100 g vode
  - 2 kašike šećera
  en:
  - 200 g chocolate
  - 100 g water
  - 2 tbsp sugar
steps:
  sr:
  - Ispeći koru i preseći je na pola.
  - Umutiti šećer u prahu sa margarinom.
  - Keks pomešati sa sojinim mlekom; orahe pomešati sa hladnim sojinim mlekom.
  - Sjediniti sva tri fila i filovati koru.
  - Preliv kratko prokuvati pa ohladiti i preliti kolač.
  en:
  - Bake the base and cut it in half.
  - Beat the powdered sugar with the margarine.
  - Mix the biscuits with soy milk; mix the walnuts with cold soy milk.
  - Combine the three fillings and sandwich the halves.
  - Bring the glaze to a boil then cool and pour over the cake.
notes:
  sr: Sojino mleko — 6 kašika (ili 135 g) sojinog mleka u prahu na 1 l tople vode.
  en: Soy milk — 6 tbsp (or 135 g) soy milk powder per 1 l warm water.
