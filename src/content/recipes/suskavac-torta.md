ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Kora
    en: Meringue
  sr:
  - 8 belanaca
  - 550 g šećera
  - 1 kašika sirćeta
  en:
  - 8 egg whites
  - 550 g sugar
  - 1 tbsp vinegar
- group:
    sr: Fil
    en: Filling
  sr:
  - 8 žumanaca
  - 6-8 kašika šećera
  - 3 kašike gustina
  - 1 puding od vanile
  - 500-750 ml mleka
  - 1 margarin
  - 200 g šećera u prahu
  - 300 g mlevenih oraha
  - 4 šlag kreme
  en:
  - 8 egg yolks
  - 6-8 tbsp sugar
  - 3 tbsp cornstarch
  - 1 vanilla pudding mix
  - 500-750 ml milk
  - 1 margarine block
  - 200 g powdered sugar
  - 300 g ground walnuts
  - 4 sachets whipped topping
steps:
  sr:
  - Belanca umutiti sa šećerom; na pola mućenja dodati sirće i dobro umutiti.
  - Sipati u pleh obložen papirom i poravnati.
  - Staviti u rernu zagrejanu na 300 °C pa odmah smanjiti na 100 °C i sušiti 2 i po-3 sata.
  - Žumanca, šećer, gustin i puding skuvati u mleku.
  - Kad se prohladi dodati orahe; kad se skroz ohladi dodati margarin umućen sa šećerom u prahu.
  - Šlag umutiti sa hladnom vodom.
  - 'Filovati: kora, pola fila, šlag, kora, fil, šlag.'
  en:
  - Whisk the whites with the sugar; halfway through add the vinegar and whisk until stiff.
  - Spread on a lined tray.
  - Put into an oven preheated to 300 °C then immediately lower to 100 °C and dry for 2.5–3 hours.
  - Cook the yolks, sugar, cornstarch and pudding in the milk.
  - When warm add the walnuts; when completely cool add the margarine creamed with powdered sugar.
  - Whip the topping with cold water.
  - 'Layer: meringue, half the filling, cream, meringue, filling, cream.'
