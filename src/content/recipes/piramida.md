- group:
    sr: Sastojci

  - 13 kašika šećera
  - 150 g mlevenih oraha
  - 150 g keksa
  - 1 čokolada
  - malo manje od 1 margarina
  en:
  - 15 tbsp water
  - 13 tbsp sugar
  - 150 g ground walnuts
  - 150 g biscuits
  - 1 bar of chocolate
  - a little less than 1 margarine block
steps:
  sr:
  - Vodu i šećer prokuvati.
  - Dodati margarin i čokoladu da se istope.
  - Umešati orahe i keks i oblikovati piramidu.
  en:
  - Boil the water and sugar.
  - Add the margarine and chocolate and let them melt.
  - Stir in the walnuts and biscuits and shape into a pyramid.
