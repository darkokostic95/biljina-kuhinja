ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Preliv za kore
    en: Batter for the sheets
  sr:
  - 7 kašika brašna
  - 1 suvi kvasac (sa malo vode i šećera)
  - 2 šolje mleka
  - 1 šolja ulja
  - so
  - 1-2 jaja
  en:
  - 7 tbsp flour
  - 1 sachet dry yeast (with a little water and sugar)
  - 2 cups milk
  - 1 cup oil
  - salt
  - 1–2 eggs
- group:
    sr: Fil
    en: Filling
  sr:
  - 300-400 g sira
  - 3 jaja
  - malo jogurta
  - malo zejtina
  - kore za pitu
  en:
  - 300–400 g white cheese
  - 3 eggs
  - a little yogurt
  - a little oil
  - filo sheets
steps:
  sr:
  - Kvasac aktivirati sa malo vode i šećera pa umutiti sa ostalim sastojcima preliva.
  - Svaku koru preliti prelivom; na svaku treću staviti fil od sira i jaja.
  - Slagati u pleh, ostaviti sat i po pa ispeći.
  en:
  - Activate the yeast with a little water and sugar then whisk with the other batter ingredients.
  - Brush each sheet with the batter; on every third sheet spread the cheese and egg filling.
  - Layer in a tin, leave for an hour and a half, then bake.
notes:

  en: 'Similar version (IMG_5678): a thin pancake-like batter of 2 glasses warm water, yeast, flour, salt, sugar and oil; filling of eggs, baking powder and cheese (spinach optional).'
