---
slug: ljutenica-od-feferona
title:
  sr: Ljutenica od feferona
  en: Hot Pepper Relish
category: zimnica
tags:
- povrce
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 2,5 kg feferona (mogu mešane)
  - 2 glavice belog luka
  - 1 jogurtska čaša zejtina
  - pola šoljice esencije
  - so po ukusu
  - pola konzervansa
  en:
  - 2.5 kg hot peppers (mixed is fine)
  - 2 heads garlic
  - 1 yogurt glass oil
  - half a small cup vinegar essence
  - salt to taste
  - half a sachet preservative
steps:
  sr:
  - Feferone zagrejati u rerni i samleti.
  - Dodati samleven beli luk.
  - Zejtin zagrejati i njime popariti papriku pa pomešati sa esencijom i solju.
  - Sipati u tegle.
  en:
  - Warm the peppers in the oven and mince them.
  - Add the minced garlic.
  - Heat the oil, scald the peppers with it, then mix with the essence and salt.
  - Fill into jars.
---
