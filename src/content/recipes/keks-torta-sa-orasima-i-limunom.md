  sr: Keks torta sa orasima i limunom
  en: Walnut Lemon Biscuit Cake
category: torte

ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 1 margarin
  - 250 g šećera u prahu
  - 2 jaja
  - 4 kašike šećera
  - 250-300 g oraha
  - sok i kora od 1/2 limuna ili malo ruma
  - keks
  - jaka crna kafa ili mleko
  en:
  - 1 margarine block
  - 250 g powdered sugar
  - 2 eggs
  - 4 tbsp sugar
  - 250–300 g walnuts
  - juice and zest of 1/2 lemon or a little rum
  - biscuits
  - strong black coffee or milk
steps:
  sr:
  - Margarin dobro umutiti sa šećerom u prahu.
  - Posebno umutiti jaja sa šećerom i postepeno dodavati u margarin uz stalno mućenje.
  - Dodati orahe i limun ili rum.
  - Keks umakati u kafu ili mleko i slagati sa filom; odozgo orasi.
  en:
  - Cream the margarine well with the powdered sugar.
  - Separately whisk the eggs with the sugar and gradually beat into the margarine.
  - Add the walnuts and lemon or rum.
  - Dip the biscuits in coffee or milk and layer with the filling; walnuts on top.
