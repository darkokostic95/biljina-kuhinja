title:
  sr: Grčka baklava (posna)
  en: Lenten Greek Baklava
category: pite
tags:
- posno
- orasi
- keks
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 1 pakovanje kora za pitu
  - 1/2 kg mlevenog keksa
  - 250 g oraha
  - 100 g suvog grožđa
  - ulje
  en:
  - 1 pack filo sheets
  - 1/2 kg ground biscuits
  - 250 g walnuts
  - 100 g raisins
  - oil
- group:
    sr: Šerbet
    en: Syrup
  sr:
  - 1/2 kg šećera
  - 1/2 l vode
  en:
  - 1/2 kg sugar
  - 1/2 l water
steps:
  sr:
  - Orahe malo nakvasiti i pomešati sa keksom i grožđem.
  - Slagati kore sa filom i ispeći.
  - Ukuvati šerbet i ohladiti.
  - Hladnim šerbetom preliti vruću baklavu.
  en:
  - Moisten the walnuts slightly and mix with the biscuits and raisins.
  - Layer the filo with the filling and bake.
  - Boil the syrup and let it cool.
  - Pour the cold syrup over the hot baklava.
