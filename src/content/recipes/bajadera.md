ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 400 g šećera
  - 2 šoljice vode
  - 150 g mlevenog keksa
  - 150 g mlevenih oraha
  - 150 g margarina
  - 1 kašika kakaa
  - glazura od čokolade
  en:
  - 400 g sugar
  - 2 small cups water
  - 150 g ground biscuits
  - 150 g ground walnuts
  - 150 g margarine
  - 1 tbsp cocoa
  - chocolate glaze
steps:
  sr:
  - Šećer i vodu da provri.
  - Dodati keks orahe i margarin i izmešati; podeliti na 2 dela.
  - U jednu polovinu dodati kakao i staviti u podmazan pleh.
  - Preko staviti drugu polovinu i kad se ohladi preliti glazurom.
  en:
  - Bring the sugar and water to a boil.
  - Add the biscuits walnuts and margarine and mix; divide in 2.
  - Add cocoa to one half and press into a greased tin.
  - Spread the other half on top and glaze when cool.
