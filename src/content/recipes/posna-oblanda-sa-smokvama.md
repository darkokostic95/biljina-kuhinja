  sr: Posna oblanda sa smokvama
  en: Lenten Fig and Walnut Wafer Cake

tags:

sources:

ingredients:
- group:
    sr: I list
    en: Layer I
  sr:
  - 200 g šećera
  - 100 g vode
  - parče margarina
  - 200 g mlevenih oraha
  en:
  - 200 g sugar
  - 100 g water
  - a knob of margarine
  - 200 g ground walnuts
- group:
    sr: II list
    en: Layer II
  sr:
  - 200 g smokava
  - 150 g suvog grožđa
  - 1 smrznuta pomorandža
  - 200 g šećera u prahu
  en:
  - 200 g dried figs
  - 150 g raisins
  - 1 frozen orange
  - 200 g powdered sugar
- group:
    sr: III list
    en: Layer III
  sr:
  - 200 g margarina
  - 200 g šećera u prahu
  - sok od 1 limuna
  - 4 lista oblande
  en:
  - 200 g margarine
  - 200 g powdered sugar
  - juice of 1 lemon
  - 4 wafer sheets
steps:
  sr:
  - Ukuvati šećer i vodu pa dodati margarin i orahe; premazati prvi list.
  - Samleti smokve, grožđe i pomorandžu pa dodati šećer u prahu; premazati drugi list.
  - Umutiti margarin sa šećerom u prahu i limunovim sokom; premazati treći list.
  - Pokriti četvrtim listom oblande.
  en:
  - Boil the sugar and water then add the margarine and walnuts; spread on the first sheet.
  - Mince the figs, raisins and orange then add the powdered sugar; spread on the second sheet.
  - Beat the margarine with powdered sugar and lemon juice; spread on the third sheet.
  - Cover with the fourth wafer sheet.
notes:
  sr: 'Voćna (neposna) verzija sa IMG_5667: u treći fil dodati 1 žumance, a odozgo glazura pola crne a pola bele čokolade.'
  en: 'Non-Lenten version (IMG_5667): add 1 egg yolk to the third layer and finish with a half dark half white chocolate glaze.'
