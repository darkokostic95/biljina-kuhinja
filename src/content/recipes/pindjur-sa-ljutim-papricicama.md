---
slug: pindjur-sa-ljutim-papricicama
title:
  sr: Pinđur sa ljutim papričicama
  en: Pindjur with Hot Cherry Peppers
category: zimnica
tags:
- povrce
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 100 pečenih oljuštenih paprika
  - 3 kg pečenog oljuštenog paradajza
  - 2 jogurtske čaše zejtina
  - 2 čaše šećera
  - malo manje od 1 čaše sirćeta
  - so
  - 1 kg ljutih okruglih papričica
  - konzervans
  en:
  - 100 roasted peeled peppers
  - 3 kg roasted peeled tomatoes
  - 2 yogurt glasses oil
  - 2 glasses sugar
  - just under 1 glass vinegar
  - salt
  - 1 kg round hot peppers
  - preservative
steps:
  sr:
  - Papriku samleti pa sa paradajzom prokuvati.
  - U drugoj šerpi pomešati zejtin šećer sirće i so pa sjediniti sa paprikom i prokuvati.
  - U to obariti ljute papričice i sve zajedno prokuvati.
  - Dodati so i konzervans i sipati u tegle.
  en:
  - Mince the peppers and cook with the tomatoes.
  - In another pot mix the oil sugar vinegar and salt then combine with the peppers and boil.
  - Blanch the hot peppers in it and boil everything together.
  - Add salt and preservative and fill the jars.
