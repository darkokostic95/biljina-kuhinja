slug: turska-torta
title:
  sr: Turska torta
  en: Turkish Caramel Biscuit Cake
category: torte
tags:
- keks
- bez-pecenja
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 300 g keksa
  - 1 i po l mleka
  - 4 pudinga od karamele
  - 12 kašika šećera
  - 1 margarin
  - 1,5 mlinče šećera u prahu
  - 1/2 kg šlaga sa ukusom pavlake
  en:
  - 300 g biscuits
  - 1.5 l milk
  - 4 caramel pudding mixes
  - 12 tbsp sugar
  - 1 margarine block
  - 1.5 measures (mlinče) powdered sugar
  - 1/2 kg cream-flavoured whipped topping
steps:
  sr:
  - Pudinge skuvati u mleku sa šećerom.
  - Kad se ohladi dodati margarin umućen sa šećerom u prahu.
  - 'Ređati: keks, fil, šlag — ponoviti.'
  en:
  - Cook the puddings in the milk with the sugar.
  - When cool add the margarine creamed with powdered sugar.
  - 'Layer: biscuits, filling, whipped cream — repeat.'
