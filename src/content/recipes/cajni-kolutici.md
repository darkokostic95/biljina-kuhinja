- group:
    sr: Sastojci

  sr:
  - 2 jaja
  - 16 kašika šećera
  - 16 kašika mleka (ili pola jogurta)
  - 8 kašika rastopljene masti ili margarina
  - 1 prašak za pecivo
  - 1 vanilin šećer
  - brašno po potrebi
  - ulje za prženje
  - šećer u prahu
  en:
  - 2 eggs
  - 16 tbsp sugar
  - 16 tbsp milk (or half yogurt)
  - 8 tbsp melted lard or margarine
  - 1 baking powder
  - 1 vanilla sugar sachet
  - flour as needed
  - oil for frying
  - powdered sugar
steps:
  sr:
  - Umutiti jaja sa šećerom pa dodati mleko, mast, prašak i vanilu.
  - Dodati brašna koliko treba za testo kao za krofne.
  - Razvaljati i vaditi kolutiće šoljom.
  - Pržiti u ulju i vruće valjati u šećeru u prahu.
  en:
  - Whisk the eggs with the sugar then add the milk, fat, baking powder and vanilla.
  - Add flour as needed for a doughnut-like dough.
  - Roll out and cut rings with a cup.
  - Fry in oil and roll in powdered sugar while hot.
