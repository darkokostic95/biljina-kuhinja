slug: torta-sa-piskotama-i-dva-fila
title:
  sr: Torta sa piškotama i dva fila
  en: Ladyfinger Cake with Two Fillings
category: torte
tags:
- plazma
- orasi
- cokolada
- lesnik
- bez-pecenja
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Tamni fil
    en: Dark filling
  sr:
  - 600 ml mleka
  - 2 pudinga od čokolade
  - 100 g čokolade
  - 150 g šećera u prahu
  - pola margarina
  - 150 g oraha
  en:
  - 600 ml milk
  - 2 chocolate pudding mixes
  - 100 g chocolate
  - 150 g powdered sugar
  - half a margarine block
  - 150 g walnuts
- group:
    sr: Beli fil
    en: White filling
  sr:
  - 500 ml slatke pavlake
  - 250 g šećera u prahu
  - 600 g kisele pavlake
  - 100 g otopljene bele čokolade
  - 300 g plazme
  en:
  - 500 ml whipping cream
  - 250 g powdered sugar
  - 600 g sour cream
  - 100 g melted white chocolate
  - 300 g Plazma
- group:
    sr: Piškote
    en: Ladyfingers
  sr:
  - 300 ml mleka
  - 2 kesice karamela sa lešnikom
  - piškote
  en:
  - 300 ml milk
  - 2 bags hazelnut caramels
  - ladyfingers
steps:
  sr:
  - Skuvati pudinge u mleku i dodati čokoladu; u prohlađen dodati margarin umućen sa šećerom u prahu i orahe.
  - Slatku pavlaku umutiti sa šećerom u prahu pa dodati kiselu pavlaku, belu čokoladu i plazmu (malo odvojiti za vrh).
  - Karamele rastopiti u mleku i u to umakati piškote.
  - 'Ređati: piškote, tamni fil, piškote, beli fil; odozgo ostatak belog fila.'
  en:
  - Cook the puddings in the milk and add the chocolate; when cool add the margarine creamed with powdered sugar and the walnuts.
  - Whip the cream with the powdered sugar then add the sour cream, white chocolate and Plazma (keep some for the top).
  - Melt the caramels in the milk and dip the ladyfingers.
  - 'Layer: ladyfingers, dark filling, ladyfingers, white filling; finish with the rest of the white filling.'
notes:
  sr: Bilja je ovde stavila samo jedan red piškota i označila recept kao svoj.
  en: Bilja used only one layer of ladyfingers and marked this recipe as her own.
---
