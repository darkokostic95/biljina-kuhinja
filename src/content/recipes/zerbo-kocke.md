  sr:

  - Isto ponoviti sa drugom korom.
  - Treću koru staviti odozgo ostaviti da naraste 30 minuta i peći na 200 °C 30 minuta.
  - Vruć kolač preliti glazurom.
  en:
  - Knead the dough and divide into 3.
  - Roll the first into a greased tin spread with jam and sprinkle with walnuts and sugar.
  - Repeat with the second layer.
  - Put the third on top let rise 30 minutes and bake at 200 °C for 30 minutes.
  - Glaze the hot cake.
