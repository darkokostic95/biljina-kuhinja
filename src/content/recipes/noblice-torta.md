slug: noblice-torta
title:
  sr: Noblice torta
  en: Noblice Biscuit Cake
category: torte
tags:
- keks
- cokolada
sources:
- IMG_5555
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 1 kg noblica (keks sa filom)
  - 2 pudinga od vanile
  - 1 l mleka
  - 10 kašika šećera
  - 4 jaja (odvojena)
  - 1 margarin
  - 250 g šećera u prahu
  - 200 g otopljene čokolade
  - mleko za potapanje
  en:
  - 1 kg Noblice sandwich biscuits
  - 2 vanilla pudding mixes
  - 1 l milk
  - 10 tbsp sugar
  - 4 eggs (separated)
  - 1 margarine block
  - 250 g powdered sugar
  - 200 g melted chocolate
  - milk for dipping
steps:
  sr:
  - Skuvati pudinge u mleku sa šećerom i ohladiti.
  - Umutiti žumanca sa margarinom; posebno umutiti belanca sa šećerom u prahu pa sve sjediniti.
  - Uz mućenje dodavati hladan puding kašiku po kašiku i na kraju otopljenu čokoladu.
  - Ređati noblice natopljene u mleko, fil, noblice, fil i odozgo šlag.
  en:
  - Cook the puddings in the milk with the sugar and cool.
  - Beat the yolks with the margarine; separately whisk the whites with the powdered sugar then combine.
  - While beating add the cold pudding a spoonful at a time and finally the melted chocolate.
  - Layer milk-dipped biscuits, filling, biscuits, filling and finish with whipped cream.
