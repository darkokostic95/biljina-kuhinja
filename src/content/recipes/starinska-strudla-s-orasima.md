ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Testo
    en: Dough
  sr:
  - 300 g maslaca
  - 2 kašike šećera
  - prstohvat soli
  - 6 žumanaca
  - 3 dl mlakog mleka
  - 1 kesica kvasca
  - 1,5 kg griz brašna
  en:
  - 300 g butter
  - 2 tbsp sugar
  - a pinch of salt
  - 6 egg yolks
  - 3 dl lukewarm milk
  - 1 sachet yeast
  - 1.5 kg semolina flour
- group:
    sr: Fil
    en: Filling
  sr:
  - 0,5 kg šećera
  - 0,5 kg oraha
  - 4 dl vrućeg mleka
  - 0,5 kg mlevenih urmi ili smokava
  - 80 g suvog grožđa
  - 2 šipke strugane čokolade
  - 2 kašike pekmeza od kajsije
  - 2 rendane kisele jabuke
  - 4 belanceta
  en:
  - 0.5 kg sugar
  - 0.5 kg walnuts
  - 4 dl hot milk
  - 0.5 kg minced dates or figs
  - 80 g raisins
  - 2 bars grated chocolate
  - 2 tbsp apricot jam
  - 2 grated sour apples
  - 4 egg whites
steps:
  sr:
  - Maslac penasto umutiti sa šećerom i solju pa dodavati jedno po jedno žumance.
  - Dodati mleko sa razmućenim kvascem i postepeno brašno.
  - Mesiti pa tri puta razvući na dasci uz odmaranje.
  - Za fil pomešati sve sastojke a belanca dodati na kraju.
  - Razvući koru debljine oko 1 cm premazati filom i uviti.
  - Peći na umerenoj temperaturi dok testo ne bude pečeno.
  en:
  - Cream the butter with the sugar and salt then add the yolks one at a time.
  - Add the milk with the dissolved yeast and gradually the flour.
  - Knead then stretch on the board three times resting in between.
  - Mix the filling adding the egg whites last.
  - Roll the dough about 1 cm thick spread with filling and roll up.
  - Bake at a moderate temperature until cooked through.
notes:
