slug: lesnik-stangle
title:
  sr: Lešnik štangle
  en: Hazelnut Caramel Bars
category: bez-pecenja
tags:
- bez-pecenja
- lesnik
- keks
- cokolada
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 3 jaja
  - 300 g šećera
  - 100 g lešnik karamela (bombona)
  - 1 margarin
  - 300 g mlevenog keksa
  - čokolada za rendanje
  en:
  - 3 eggs
  - 300 g sugar
  - 100 g hazelnut caramel sweets
  - 1 margarine block
  - 300 g ground biscuits
  - chocolate for grating
steps:
  sr:
  - Jaja i šećer skuvati na pari.
  - Dodati karamele i margarin da se istope.
  - Dodati keks i dok je još vruće rasteniti u pleh.
  - Preko narendati čokoladu i seći na štangle.
  en:
  - Cook the eggs and sugar over steam.
  - Add the caramels and margarine to melt.
  - Add the biscuits and spread in a tin while still hot.
  - Grate chocolate over and cut into bars.
