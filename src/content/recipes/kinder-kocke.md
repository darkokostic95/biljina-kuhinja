  en: Kinder Squares
category: bez-pecenja
tags:

- cokolada
sources:
- IMG_5596
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Tamni deo
    en: Dark layer
  sr:
  - 2 dl vode
  - 300 g šećera
  - 1 margarin
  - 200 g mlevenih oraha
  - 500 g mlevenog keksa
  - 100 g čokolade
  en:
  - 2 dl water
  - 300 g sugar
  - 1 margarine block
  - 200 g ground walnuts
  - 500 g ground biscuits
  - 100 g chocolate
- group:
    sr: Beli fil
    en: White layer
  sr:
  - 1 dl vode
  - 400 g šećera
  - 1 margarin
  - 300 g mleka u prahu
  - orasi kokos ili glazura za vrh
  en:
  - 1 dl water
  - 400 g sugar
  - 1 margarine block
  - 300 g milk powder
  - walnuts coconut or glaze for the top
steps:
  sr:
  - Vodu i šećer da provri pa dodati margarin, orahe, keks i čokoladu; razviti u koru.
  - Za fil prokuvati vodu i šećer, dodati margarin, skloniti sa vatre i dodati mleko u prahu.
  - Fil umutiti mikserom da se ujednači i staviti preko prve mase.
  - Posuti orasima kokosom ili preliti glazurom.
  en:
  - Bring the water and sugar to a boil then add the margarine, walnuts, biscuits and chocolate; press into a layer.
  - For the white layer boil the water and sugar, add the margarine, take off the heat and add the milk powder.
  - Beat smooth with a mixer and spread over the first layer.
  - Top with walnuts coconut or glaze.
