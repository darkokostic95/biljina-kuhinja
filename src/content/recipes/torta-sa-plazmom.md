---
slug: torta-sa-plazmom
title:
  sr: Torta sa plazmom
  en: Plazma Layer Cake
category: torte
tags:
- plazma
- orasi
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Korice (×3)
    en: Sponges (×3)
  sr:
  - 5 belanaca
  - 5 kašika šećera
  - 4 kašike oraha
  - 4 kašike plazme
  - 1 kašika brašna
  - malo praška za pecivo
  en:
  - 5 egg whites
  - 5 tbsp sugar
  - 4 tbsp walnuts
  - 4 tbsp Plazma
  - 1 tbsp flour
  - a little baking powder
- group:
    sr: Žuti fil
    en: Yellow filling
  sr:
  - 15 žumanaca
  - 1/2 kg šećera
  - 1 puding od vanile
  - 7 kašika gustina
  - 1 l mleka
  - 1 margarin
  - 150 g mlevene plazme
  en:
  - 15 egg yolks
  - 1/2 kg sugar
  - 1 vanilla pudding mix
  - 7 tbsp cornstarch
  - 1 l milk
  - 1 margarine block
  - 150 g ground Plazma
- group:
    sr: Beli fil
    en: White filling
  sr:
  - 400 g kisele pavlake
  - 200 g šećera u prahu
  - 200 g čvrsto umućenog šlaga
  - 150 g mlevene plazme
  en:
  - 400 g sour cream
  - 200 g powdered sugar
  - 200 g stiffly whipped cream
  - 150 g ground Plazma
steps:
  sr:
  - Ispeći 3 korice na 200 °C po 14-15 minuta.
  - Skuvati žuti fil u mleku; umutiti margarin penasto i sjediniti sa filom pa dodati plazmu.
  - Za beli fil izmešati pavlaku, šećer u prahu, šlag i plazmu.
  - Filovati korice naizmenično žutim i belim filom.
  en:
  - Bake 3 sponges at 200 °C for 14–15 minutes each.
  - Cook the yellow filling in the milk; cream the margarine and combine then add the Plazma.
  - For the white filling mix the sour cream, powdered sugar, whipped cream and Plazma.
  - Fill the sponges alternating yellow and white filling.
