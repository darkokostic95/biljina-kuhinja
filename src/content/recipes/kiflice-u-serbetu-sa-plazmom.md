title:
  sr: Kiflice u šerbetu sa plazmom
  en: Syrup-Dipped Crescents with Plazma
category: peciva
tags:
- kvasac
- plazma
sources:
- IMG_5576
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Testo
    en: Dough
  sr:
  - 1 kg brašna
  - 1 kašičica soli
  - 1 kašika šećera
  - 2 umućena jaja
  - 1 čaša zejtina
  - 1 čaša jogurta
  - 1 čaša kisele vode
  - kvasac
  - krem za punjenje
  en:
  - 1 kg flour
  - 1 tsp salt
  - 1 tbsp sugar
  - 2 beaten eggs
  - 1 glass oil
  - 1 glass yogurt
  - 1 glass sparkling water
  - yeast
  - cream spread for filling
- group:
    sr: Šerbet
    en: Syrup
  sr:
  - 250 g šećera
  - 250 ml vode
  - mlevena plazma za valjanje
  en:
  - 250 g sugar
  - 250 ml water
  - ground Plazma biscuits for rolling
steps:
  sr:
  - Tečnost malo smlačiti, dodati kvasac i zamesiti testo.
  - Napraviti 5 lopti; svaku podeliti na kiflice, staviti krem i uviti.
  - Rernu zagrejati na 180 °C; kad ubaciš kiflice smanjiti na 150 °C i peći 20 minuta (da ostanu bele).
  - Mlake kiflice provući kroz hladan šerbet pa uvaljati u plazmu.
  en:
  - Warm the liquids slightly, add the yeast and knead the dough.
  - Make 5 balls; cut each into crescents, add the cream and roll up.
  - Preheat to 180 °C; when the rolls go in lower to 150 °C and bake 20 minutes (they should stay pale).
  - Dip the lukewarm crescents in cold syrup then roll in Plazma.
