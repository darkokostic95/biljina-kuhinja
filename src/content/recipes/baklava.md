  en: Walnut Baklava Rolls
category: pite
tags:

aliases:
  sr:
  - baklava sa orasima
  en:
  - walnut baklava
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 1/2 kg kora za pitu
  - 150 g margarina
  - 300 g mlevenih oraha
  - 100 g šećera
  - 1 dcl mleka
  - 3 kašike ulja
  - zejtin za premazivanje
  en:
  - 1/2 kg filo sheets
  - 150 g margarine
  - 300 g ground walnuts
  - 100 g sugar
  - 1 dl milk
  - 3 tbsp oil
  - oil for brushing
- group:
    sr: Sirup
    en: Syrup
  sr:
  - 2 dcl vode
  - 500 g šećera
  - 1 isečen limun
  - 1 vanilin šećer
  en:
  - 2 dl water
  - 500 g sugar
  - 1 sliced lemon
  - 1 vanilla sugar sachet
steps:
  sr:
  - Margarin orahe šećer mleko i ulje umutiti mikserom.
  - Tri kore premazati zejtinom a na četvrtu staviti fil.
  - Uviti u rolat i staviti u pleh — ponavljati dok ima kora.
  - Peći na 200 °C.
  - Prokuvati sirup i preliti pečenu baklavu.
  en:
  - Beat the margarine walnuts sugar milk and oil with a mixer.
  - Brush three sheets with oil and spread the filling on the fourth.
  - Roll up and place in the tin — repeat with the remaining sheets.
  - Bake at 200 °C.
  - Boil the syrup and pour over the baked baklava.
