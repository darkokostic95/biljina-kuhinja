slug: domaci-sladoled
title:
  sr: Domaći sladoled (dobar)
  en: Homemade Ice Cream
category: kremovi-sladoledi
tags:
- bez-pecenja
aliases:
  sr:
  - sladoled
  en:
  - ice cream
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 1 l hladnog mleka
  - 2 jogurtske čaše šećera
  - 1 limontus (limunska kiselina)
  - 2 šlag kreme
  - malo manje od 1 jogurtske čaše mleka
  - čokolada jagode ili banane po želji
  en:
  - 1 l cold milk
  - 2 yogurt glasses sugar
  - 1 sachet citric acid
  - 2 sachets whipped topping
  - just under 1 yogurt glass milk
  - chocolate strawberries or bananas as you like
steps:
  sr:
  - U hladno mleko staviti šećer i limuntus i mešati dok se šećer ne otopi.
  - Staviti u zamrzivač 12 sati.
  - Izvaditi da odstoji 5-10 minuta.
  - Umutiti šlag sa mlekom i ubacivati smrznutu masu kašiku po kašiku.
  - Podeliti na 2-3 dela i dodati šta volite (čokoladu jagode banane) pa ponovo zamrznuti.
  en:
  - Stir the sugar and citric acid into the cold milk until dissolved.
  - Freeze for 12 hours.
  - Take out and leave 5–10 minutes.
  - Whip the topping with the milk and beat in the frozen mixture spoon by spoon.
  - Divide into 2–3 and add what you like (chocolate strawberries bananas) then freeze again.
