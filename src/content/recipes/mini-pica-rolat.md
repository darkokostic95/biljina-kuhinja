  sr:

  en:
  - Knead the dough and rest 1 hour.
  - Beat the margarine sour cream yolks and flour.
  - Roll out spread with the mixture and add the salami.
  - Roll up and slice; top with ketchup.
  - Bake adding the grated cheese halfway.
