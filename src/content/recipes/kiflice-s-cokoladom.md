steps:
  sr:
  - Zamesiti testo i odmah raditi — ne čekati da naraste.
  - Podeliti na 4 lopte a svaku na 6 delova; razvući i narendati margarin između.
  - Veliku koru podeliti na 16 delova a na širi deo staviti pola kocke čokolade.
  - Uviti kiflice ređati u podmazan pleh i ostaviti 2 sata pa ispeći.
  - Vruće kiflice premazati medom (po kašiku na kiflu).
  en:
  - Knead the dough and work it straight away — don't wait for it to rise.
  - Divide into 4 balls and each into 6 pieces; roll out with grated margarine between them.
  - Cut the large sheet into 16 and put half a square of chocolate on the wide end.
  - Roll up arrange in a greased tin rest 2 hours then bake.
  - Brush the hot rolls with honey (a spoonful each).
