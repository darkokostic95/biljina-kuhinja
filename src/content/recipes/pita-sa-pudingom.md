---
slug: pita-sa-pudingom
title:
  sr: Pita sa pudingom
  en: Custard Filo Pie
category: pite
tags: []
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Preliv za kore
    en: Egg mixture
  sr:
  - 5 jaja
  - 5 kašika šećera
  - 2 šolje zejtina
  - 1 prašak za pecivo
  - 1 pakovanje kora za pitu
  en:
  - 5 eggs
  - 5 tbsp sugar
  - 2 cups oil
  - 1 baking powder
  - 1 pack filo sheets
- group:
    sr: Fil
    en: Filling
  sr:
  - 1 l mleka
  - 3 pudinga od vanile
  en:
  - 1 l milk
  - 3 vanilla pudding mixes
- group:
    sr: Sirup
    en: Syrup
  sr:
  - 3 malo veće šoljice šećera
  - 2 šoljice vode
  en:
  - 3 generous small cups sugar
  - 2 small cups water
steps:
  sr:
  - Jaja umutiti sa šećerom pa dodati zejtin i prašak.
  - Skuvati pudinge u mleku i ohladiti.
  - Kore podeliti po 4 — prve tri premazati filom od jaja a na četvrtu na početak staviti puding i uviti u rolat.
  - Ponavljati dok se ne potroše kore; pre pečenja malo zaseći.
  - Ispeći i preliti sirupom (jedno vruće a drugo hladno).
  - Kad se ohladi opet malo zaseći da bolje upije.
  en:
  - Whisk the eggs with the sugar then add the oil and baking powder.
  - Cook the puddings in the milk and cool.
  - Take the sheets in fours — brush three with the egg mixture and put pudding along the edge of the fourth then roll up.
  - Repeat until the sheets are used up; score lightly before baking.
  - Bake and pour over the syrup (one hot and the other cold).
  - When cool score again so it soaks better.
