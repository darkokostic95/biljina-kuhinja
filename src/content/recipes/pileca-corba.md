ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - pileće belo meso
  - veza zeleni za supu
  - 2 šargarepe
  - 100 g pirinča
  - 2 kašike kisele pavlake
  - 1 kašika majoneza
  - 1 kašika senfa
  - 2 kašike ulja
  - pola lovorovog lista
  - 10 zrna bibera
  - 1 glavica crnog luka
  - so
  - peršun
  en:
  - chicken breast
  - a bunch of soup greens
  - 2 carrots
  - 100 g rice
  - 2 tbsp sour cream
  - 1 tbsp mayonnaise
  - 1 tbsp mustard
  - 2 tbsp oil
  - half a bay leaf
  - 10 peppercorns
  - 1 onion
  - salt
  - parsley
steps:
  sr:
  - Zelen i šargarepu očistiti i iseći na kolutove.
  - Na ulju kratko propržiti zelen i pirinač pa uliti 1,5 l vode.
  - Dodati meso biber i lovor i kuvati dok ne omekša.
  - Posoliti po ukusu i dodati isečen crni luk zapečen na ringli; kuvati još 10 minuta pa luk izvaditi i baciti.
  - Meso izvaditi, iseckati i vratiti.
  - Skinuti sa vatre i umešati pavlaku umućenu sa majonezom i senfom; posuti peršunom.
  en:
  - Clean and slice the soup greens and carrots.
  - Briefly fry the greens and rice in oil then add 1.5 l water.
  - Add the chicken pepper and bay and cook until tender.
  - Season and add the halved onion charred on the hob; cook 10 more minutes then remove and discard the onion.
  - Take out the meat, chop it and return it.
  - Take off the heat and stir in the sour cream whisked with mayonnaise and mustard; sprinkle with parsley.
