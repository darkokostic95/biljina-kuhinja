  - 4 pudinga od vanile

  - 1 margarin

  - 300 g mlevene plazme

  en:
  - 4 vanilla pudding mixes
  - 1.5 l milk
  - 1 margarine block
  - 250 g powdered sugar
  - 300 g ground Plazma
  - 8–10 cream banana sweets
  - ready-made meringue layers
  - whipped cream
steps:
  sr:
  - Skuvati pudinge u mleku i ohladiti.
  - Dodati margarin umućen sa šećerom u prahu i podeliti na 2 dela.
  - U I deo dodati plazmu a u II sitno seckane krem banane.
  - Filovati gotove kore i odozgo staviti šlag.
  en:
  - Cook the puddings in the milk and cool.
  - Add the margarine creamed with powdered sugar and divide in 2.
  - Add the Plazma to one half and finely chopped cream bananas to the other.
  - Fill the ready meringue layers and top with whipped cream.
