title:
  sr: Gastronomski sote
  en: Mixed Meat Sauté
category: glavna-jela
tags:
- meso
sources:
- IMG_5620
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 1 glavica crnog luka
  - 500 g teletine i svinjetine na kocke
  - malo mlevenog mesa (začinjenog kao za pljeskavice)
  - šampinjoni
  - biber
  - lovor
  - vegeta
  - voda
  - 1 čaša belog vina
  - 1 kašika brašna
  en:
  - 1 onion
  - 500 g veal and pork in cubes
  - a little minced meat (seasoned as for burgers)
  - mushrooms
  - pepper
  - bay leaf
  - Vegeta seasoning
  - water
  - 1 glass white wine
  - 1 tbsp flour
steps:
  sr:
  - Ispržiti luk pa dodati meso na kocke.
  - Od mlevenog mesa praviti male ćufte i posebno ih ispržiti.
  - Kad je meso polupriženo dodati šampinjone i ćufte.
  - Dodati biber lovor i vegetu i stalno dolivati vodu.
  - Na kraju dodati vino i brašno i dinstati još malo na tihoj vatri.
  en:
  - Fry the onion then add the cubed meat.
  - Shape the minced meat into little meatballs and fry them separately.
  - When the meat is half done add the mushrooms and meatballs.
  - Season with pepper bay and Vegeta adding water as it cooks.
  - Finish with the wine and flour and simmer a little longer.
