title:
  sr: Ljubavno gnezdo
  en: Love Nest Cake
category: torte
tags:

sources:

ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Kora
    en: Meringue
  sr:
  - 5 belanaca
  - 300 g šećera
  - 200 g seckanih oraha
  - džem od kajsije
  en:
  - 5 egg whites
  - 300 g sugar
  - 200 g chopped walnuts
  - apricot jam
- group:
    sr: Fil
    en: Filling
  sr:
  - 1/2 l mleka
  - 6 žumanaca
  - 6 kašika šećera
  - 6 kašika brašna
  - 250 g margarina
  - malo šećera u prahu
  - 2 šlaga
  en:
  - 1/2 l milk
  - 6 egg yolks
  - 6 tbsp sugar
  - 6 tbsp flour
  - 250 g margarine
  - a little powdered sugar
  - 2 sachets whipped topping
steps:
  sr:
  - Umutiti belanca sa šećerom.
  - U podmazan pleh staviti orahe a preko umućena belanca; sušiti na 150 °C.
  - Pečenu koru okrenuti i premazati džemom.
  - Skuvati fil; u ohlađen dodati margarin umućen sa šećerom u prahu.
  - Preko kore staviti žuti krem a odozgo 2 šlaga.
  en:
  - Whisk the whites with the sugar.
  - Scatter walnuts in a greased tin and spread the meringue over; dry at 150 °C.
  - Turn the baked layer over and spread with jam.
  - Cook the custard; when cool beat in margarine creamed with powdered sugar.
  - Spread the yellow cream over and finish with whipped cream.
