---
slug: kiflice-sa-margarinom
title:
  sr: Lisnate kiflice sa kvascem
  en: Flaky Yeast Crescents
category: peciva
tags:
- kvasac
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 750 g brašna
  - 2 žumanca
  - 1 i po šolja ulja
  - 2 kašičice soli
  - parče kvasca
  - 1 jogurtska čaša mleka
  - 1 čaša vode
  - 4 ravne kašičice šećera
  - margarin za rendanje
  en:
  - 750 g flour
  - 2 egg yolks
  - 1.5 cups oil
  - 2 tsp salt
  - a piece of fresh yeast
  - 1 yogurt glass milk
  - 1 glass water
  - 4 level tsp sugar
  - margarine for grating
steps:
  sr:
  - Zamesiti testo i ostaviti 1 sat.
  - Podeliti na 6 lopti razviti svaku i narendati margarin.
  - Kore slagati jednu na drugu (na poslednju bez margarina).
  - Razvući u jednu veliku koru i praviti kiflice (prazne ili sa filom).
  - Odmah peći u zagrejanoj rerni — ne treba da stoje u plehu.
  en:
  - Knead the dough and rest 1 hour.
  - Divide into 6 balls roll each out and grate margarine over.
  - Stack the sheets (no margarine on the last).
  - Roll into one large sheet and shape crescents (plain or filled).
  - Bake straight away in a hot oven — don't let them stand.
