title:
  sr: Štangle sa želatinom
  en: Marshmallow Wafer Bars
category: bez-pecenja
tags:
- bez-pecenja
- oblanda
sources:
- IMG_5708
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 1 kesica želatina (10 g)
  - 2 šoljice vode
  - 300 g šećera u prahu
  - žele po želji
  - listovi oblande
  en:
  - 1 sachet gelatine (10 g)
  - 2 small cups water
  - 300 g powdered sugar
  - jelly (optional)
  - wafer sheets
steps:
  sr:
  - Želatin staviti u vodu i ostaviti pola sata na kraju šporeta.
  - Preliti u vanglicu i dodati šećer u prahu.
  - Mutiti dok ne postane gusta bela masa.
  - Po želji dodati žele i sve staviti u oblandu; seći na štangle.
  en:
  - Soak the gelatine in the water and leave it at the side of the stove for half an hour.
  - Pour into a bowl and add the powdered sugar.
  - Beat until thick and white.
  - Add jelly if you like spread between wafer sheets and cut into bars.
