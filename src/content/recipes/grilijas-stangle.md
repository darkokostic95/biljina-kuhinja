ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 300 g šećera
  - 2 jaja
  - 250 g šećera u prahu
  - 1 margarin
  - 100 g seckanih oraha
  - 100 g suvog grožđa
  - 3 male oblande
  en:
  - 300 g sugar
  - 2 eggs
  - 250 g powdered sugar
  - 1 margarine block
  - 100 g chopped walnuts
  - 100 g raisins
  - 3 small wafer sheets
steps:
  sr:
  - Šećer otopiti na suvo (grilijaš).
  - Jaja i šećer u prahu kuvati na pari i dodati margarin.
  - Kad se skuva dodati u vreo grilijaš zajedno sa orasima i grožđem.
  - Promešati još malo na tihoj vatri pa lepiti sa 3 male oblande.
  en:
  - Melt the sugar dry to a caramel.
  - Cook the eggs and powdered sugar over steam and add the margarine.
  - Stir into the hot caramel together with the walnuts and raisins.
  - Stir a little longer over low heat then sandwich between 3 small wafer sheets.
