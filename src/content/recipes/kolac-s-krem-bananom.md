title:
  sr: Kolač s krem bananom
  en: Cream Banana Biscuit Pyramid
category: bez-pecenja
tags:
- bez-pecenja
- keks
- orasi
- cokolada
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 2 jaja
  - 200 g šećera
  - 1/2 margarina
  - 1 čokolada
  - 200 g mlevenog keksa
  - 50 g seckanih oraha
  - 10 krem banana (bombone)
  en:
  - 2 eggs
  - 200 g sugar
  - 1/2 margarine block
  - 1 bar chocolate
  - 200 g ground biscuits
  - 50 g chopped walnuts
  - 10 cream banana sweets
steps:
  sr:
  - Umutiti jaja šećer margarin i čokoladu pa staviti na mlaku ringlu da se rastopi.
  - Dodati keks i orahe.
  - Rastanjiti koru i poređati krem banane.
  - Preklopiti i oblikovati kao piramidu.
  en:
  - Beat the eggs sugar margarine and chocolate then melt on a warm hob.
  - Add the biscuits and walnuts.
  - Spread into a sheet and lay the cream bananas along it.
  - Fold over and shape into a pyramid.
