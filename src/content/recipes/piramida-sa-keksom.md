  en: Walnut Biscuit Pyramid

ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 200 g šećera
  - 1/2 šolje vode
  - 200 g mlevenih oraha
  - 2 štanglice čokolade
  - više od pola margarina
  - 1 tanjir izlomljenog keksa
  - 1 jaje
  en:
  - 200 g sugar
  - 1/2 cup water
  - 200 g ground walnuts
  - 2 small bars chocolate
  - more than half a margarine block
  - 1 plateful broken biscuits
  - 1 egg
steps:
  sr:
  - Šećer i vodu ukuvati (ušpilovati).
  - Dodati orahe čokoladu i margarin pa keks.
  - Kad se fil malo prohladi dodati jaje.
  - Oblikovati piramidu.
  en:
  - Boil the sugar and water to a syrup.
  - Add the walnuts chocolate and margarine then the biscuits.
  - When it cools a little add the egg.
  - Shape into a pyramid.
