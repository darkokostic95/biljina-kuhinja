ingredients:
- group:
    sr: I deo
    en: Layer I
  sr:
  - 2 jaja
  - 200 g šećera u prahu
  - 1 margarin
  - 300 g mlevenog keksa
  - 100 g pečenog i mlevenog kikirikija
  en:
  - 2 eggs
  - 200 g powdered sugar
  - 1 margarine block
  - 300 g ground biscuits
  - 100 g roasted ground peanuts
- group:
    sr: II i III deo
    en: Layers II and III
  sr:
  - 200 g mlevenog kikirikija
  - 1 jaje
  - 2 kašike šećera
  - 70 g margarina
  - 100 g čokolade
  en:
  - 200 g ground peanuts
  - 1 egg
  - 2 tbsp sugar
  - 70 g margarine
  - 100 g chocolate
steps:
  sr:
  - Jaja i šećer u prahu kuvati na pari pa dodati margarin keks i kikiriki; rasporediti u podmazan pleh debljine 1 cm.
  - Posuti sa 100 g mlevenog kikirikija.
  - Jaje šećer i margarin rastopiti na tihoj vatri i dodati čokoladu; preliti preko kikirikija.
  - Odozgo posuti još 100 g kikirikija.
  en:
  - Cook the eggs and powdered sugar over steam then add the margarine biscuits and peanuts; spread 1 cm thick in a greased tin.
  - Sprinkle with 100 g ground peanuts.
  - Melt the egg sugar and margarine over low heat add the chocolate; pour over the peanuts.
  - Finish with another 100 g peanuts.
