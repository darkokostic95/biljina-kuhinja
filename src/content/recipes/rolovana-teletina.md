  - barena jaja

  en:
  - a large piece of veal (shoulder or breast)
  - salt
  - mustard
  - 1.5 coffee cups rice
  - boiled eggs
  - cured meat
  - kashkaval cheese
  - carrot
  - peas
  - gherkins
  - a little margarine
  - parsley
  - pepper
  - oil
  - 1 wine glass white wine

steps:
  sr:
  - Meso istanjiti posoliti i namazati senfom.
  - Pirinač obariti u slanoj vodi i u hladan dodati seckana jaja suhomesnato kačkavalj povrće margarin i začine.
  - Nadev staviti na meso uviti uvezati kanapom.
  - Peći u plehu sa uljem i vinom pokriveno folijom uz povremeno prelivanje.
  - Pri kraju skinuti foliju da porumeni; u sosu ispeći krompir.
  en:
  - Flatten the meat season and brush with mustard.
  - Cook the rice in salted water and when cold add chopped egg cured meat cheese vegetables margarine and seasoning.
  - Spread the stuffing on the meat roll up and tie with string.
  - Roast in a tin with oil and wine covered with foil basting occasionally.
  - Uncover at the end to brown; roast potatoes in the juices.
