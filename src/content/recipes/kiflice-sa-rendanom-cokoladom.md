---
slug: kiflice-sa-rendanom-cokoladom
title:
  sr: Kiflice sa rendanom čokoladom
  en: Grated Chocolate Crescent Rolls
category: peciva
tags:
- kvasac
- cokolada
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 1/2 l mleka
  - 1 kocka kvasca
  - pola jogurtske čaše šećera
  - 1 pavlaka
  - 1 jogurtska čaša zejtina
  - 2 vanilin šećera
  - prstohvat soli
  - brašno po potrebi
  - rendana čokolada ili džem
  - 1 belance
  en:
  - 1/2 l milk
  - 1 cube fresh yeast
  - half a yogurt glass sugar
  - 1 tub sour cream
  - 1 yogurt glass oil
  - 2 vanilla sugar sachets
  - pinch of salt
  - flour as needed
  - grated chocolate or jam
  - 1 egg white
steps:
  sr:
  - U mleku rastopiti kvasac da nadođe pa dodati ostale sastojke i umesiti testo.
  - Kad testo nadođe razvući koru i narendati čokoladu.
  - Uviti u rolat i iseći na 4 dela.
  - Svaki deo razvući i praviti kiflice (može kašičica čokolade ili džema).
  - Premazati belancetom i ispeći.
  en:
  - Dissolve the yeast in the milk to rise then add the rest and knead a dough.
  - When risen roll out and grate chocolate over it.
  - Roll up and cut into 4 pieces.
  - Roll out each piece and shape crescents (a little chocolate or jam inside).
  - Brush with egg white and bake.
