ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Kore (×3)
    en: Layers (×3)
  sr:
  - 4 belanca
  - 130 g šećera
  - 130 g prepečenih mlevenih oraha
  - 1 i po kašika ulja
  en:
  - 4 egg whites
  - 130 g sugar
  - 130 g toasted ground walnuts
  - 1.5 tbsp oil
- group:
    sr: Fil
    en: Filling
  sr:
  - 2 žumanca
  - 4 kašike šećera
  - 2 kašike gustina
  - 250 ml mleka
  - 12 žumanaca
  - 12 kašika šećera
  - 2 štangle čokolade
  - 5 vanilin šećera
  - 350 g margarina
  en:
  - 2 egg yolks
  - 4 tbsp sugar
  - 2 tbsp cornstarch
  - 250 ml milk
  - 12 egg yolks
  - 12 tbsp sugar
  - 2 bars chocolate
  - 5 vanilla sugar sachets
  - 350 g margarine
- group:
    sr: Grilijaš
    en: Hazelnut brittle
  sr:
  - 250 g šećera
  - 200 g pečenih mlevenih lešnika
  - šlag
  en:
  - 250 g sugar
  - 200 g roasted ground hazelnuts
  - whipped cream
steps:
  sr:
  - Umutiti belanca uz postepeno dodavanje šećera pa dodati orahe i ulje; ispeći 3 kore na 200 °C u dobro podmazanom plehu ili na papiru za pečenje.
  - I fil (2 žumanca šećer gustin mleko) skuvati na pari.
  - II fil (12 žumanaca i šećer) kuvati na pari a pred kraj dodati čokoladu i vanilu.
  - Ohlađene filove sjediniti i dodati umućen margarin.
  - Šećer rastopiti dodati lešnik izručiti u podmazan pleh da se stegne pa izlomiti i samleti.
  - 'Filovati: kora, fil, mleveni grilijaš — na kraju šlag.'
  en:
  - Whisk the whites adding the sugar gradually then the walnuts and oil; bake 3 layers at 200 °C in a well-greased tin or on baking paper.
  - Cook filling I (2 yolks sugar cornstarch milk) over steam.
  - Cook filling II (12 yolks and sugar) over steam adding the chocolate and vanilla at the end.
  - Combine the cooled fillings and beat in the margarine.
  - Melt the sugar add the hazelnuts pour onto a greased tray to set then break up and grind.
  - 'Layer: sponge, filling, ground brittle — finish with whipped cream.'
