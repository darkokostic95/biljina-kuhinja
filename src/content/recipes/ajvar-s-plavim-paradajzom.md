title:
  sr: Ajvar s plavim paradajzom
  en: Ajvar with Eggplant
category: zimnica
tags:
- povrce
aliases:
  sr:
  - ajvar sa patlidžanom
  en:
  - eggplant ajvar
  - aubergine
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 8-10 kg pečene samlevene paprike
  - 3,5 kg plavog paradajza (patlidžana)
  - 3 l vode
  - šoljica sirćeta
  - šoljica zejtina
  - 2 kašike soli
  - 2 kašike šećera
  - 2 konzervansa
  en:
  - 8–10 kg roasted minced peppers
  - 3.5 kg eggplant
  - 3 l water
  - small cup vinegar
  - small cup oil
  - 2 tbsp salt
  - 2 tbsp sugar
  - 2 sachets preservative
steps:
  sr:
  - Patlidžan oljuštiti, obariti u 3 l vode i kad se ohladi samleti.
  - Sirće, zejtin, so i šećer da provri.
  - U rastvor sipati papriku i patlidžan i dodati zejtina i soli po ukusu.
  - Na kraju dodati konzervans i sipati u tegle.
  en:
  - Peel the eggplant, boil in 3 l water and mince when cool.
  - Bring the vinegar, oil, salt and sugar to a boil.
  - Add the peppers and eggplant and adjust oil and salt to taste.
  - Finally add the preservative and fill the jars.
