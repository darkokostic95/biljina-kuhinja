title:
  sr: Posna salata sa posnim majonezom
  en: Lenten Salad with Vegan Mayonnaise
category: salate-predjela
tags:
- posno
- povrce
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Posni majonez
    en: Vegan mayonnaise
  sr:
  - 2 kašike sojinog brašna
  - 10 kašika hladne vode
  - 200 g zejtina
  - 1 i po kašičica senfa
  - malo soli
  - malo limunovog soka
  en:
  - 2 tbsp soy flour
  - 10 tbsp cold water
  - 200 g oil
  - 1.5 tsp mustard
  - a little salt
  - a little lemon juice
- group:
    sr: Salata
    en: Salad
  sr:
  - grašak
  - krompir
  - šargarepa
  - krastavčići
  - tunjevina
  en:
  - peas
  - potatoes
  - carrots
  - gherkins
  - tuna
steps:
  sr:
  - Sojino brašno razmutiti u vodi i skuvati pa ohladiti.
  - Mutiti mikserom uz postepeno dodavanje zejtina.
  - Na pola mućenja dodati senf so i limun pa ostatak zejtina.
  - Pomešati sa povrćem i tunjevinom.
  en:
  - Mix the soy flour with the water cook and cool.
  - Beat with a mixer adding the oil gradually.
  - Halfway add the mustard salt and lemon then the rest of the oil.
  - Mix with the vegetables and tuna.
