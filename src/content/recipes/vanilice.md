  - 1 jaje

  en:
  - 300 g lard
  - 7 tbsp sugar
  - 2 egg yolks
  - 1 egg
  - 600 g flour
  - zest of 1 lemon
  - jam
  - powdered sugar
steps:
  sr:
  - Umutiti mast i šećer pa dodati žumanca i jaje.
  - Dodati brašno i koru od limuna i zamesiti.
  - Rastanjiti i vaditi čašicom; ispeći.
  - Pečene spajati džemom i valjati u šećer u prahu.
  en:
  - Beat the lard and sugar then add the yolks and egg.
  - Add the flour and lemon zest and knead.
  - Roll out and cut with a small glass; bake.
  - Sandwich with jam and roll in powdered sugar.
