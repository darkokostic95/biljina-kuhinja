  en: Turkish Biscuit Cake

ingredients:
- group:
    sr: I fil
    en: Filling I
  sr:
  - 600 g mleka
  - 2 pudinga od čokolade
  - 1 čokolada
  - šećer po ukusu
  - pola margarina
  - šećer u prahu
  - 2 šake mlevenih oraha
  en:
  - 600 g milk
  - 2 chocolate pudding mixes
  - 1 bar chocolate
  - sugar to taste
  - half a margarine block
  - powdered sugar
  - 2 handfuls ground walnuts
- group:
    sr: II fil
    en: Filling II
  sr:
  - 1 margarin
  - 250 g šećera u prahu
  - 300 g plazme
  - 1 otopljena čokolada
  - 2 kisele pavlake
  - 2 šlag kreme od čokolade
  en:
  - 1 margarine block
  - 250 g powdered sugar
  - 300 g Plazma biscuits
  - 1 bar melted chocolate
  - 2 tubs sour cream
  - 2 sachets chocolate whipped topping
- group:
    sr: Za slaganje
    en: For layering
  sr:
  - turski keks
  en:
  - Turkish (petit-beurre) biscuits
steps:
  sr:
  - Pudinge skuvati u mleku sa čokoladom i šećerom.
  - Pola margarina umutiti sa šećerom u prahu i sjediniti sa pudingom pa dodati orahe.
  - Za II fil umutiti margarin sa šećerom u prahu i dodati plazmu, čokoladu, pavlaku i šlag.
  - Ređati keks i filove naizmenično.
  en:
  - Cook the puddings in the milk with the chocolate and sugar.
  - Cream half a margarine block with powdered sugar and combine with the pudding then add the walnuts.
  - For filling II cream the margarine with powdered sugar and add the Plazma, chocolate, sour cream and whipped topping.
  - Layer biscuits and the two fillings alternately.
