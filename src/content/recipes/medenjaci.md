ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Testo
    en: Dough
  sr:
  - 1 kg brašna
  - 2 žumanca
  - 1 celo jaje
  - 2 kašike masti
  - pola šoljice zejtina
  - 1 kašičica sode bikarbone
  - 1 kašičica cimeta
  - 1 prašak za pecivo
  - 200 g šećera
  - 2 dcl vode
  en:
  - 1 kg flour
  - 2 egg yolks
  - 1 whole egg
  - 2 tbsp lard
  - half a small cup oil
  - 1 tsp baking soda
  - 1 tsp cinnamon
  - 1 baking powder
  - 200 g sugar
  - 2 dl water
- group:
    sr: Glazura
    en: Glaze
  sr:
  - 2 belanca
  - 200 g šećera u prahu
  en:
  - 2 egg whites
  - 200 g powdered sugar
steps:
  sr:
  - Šećer pregoreti (karamelizovati) i dodati vodu; ohladiti.
  - Sa ovim zamesiti testo od ostalih sastojaka.
  - Praviti medenjake i ispeći.
  - Umutiti belanca sa šećerom u prahu.
  - Vruće medenjake valjati u šne od belanaca i ostaviti da se suše.
  en:
  - Caramelise the sugar and add the water; cool.
  - Use it to knead the dough with the rest.
  - Shape the cookies and bake.
  - Whisk the whites with the powdered sugar.
  - Toss the hot cookies in the meringue and leave to dry.
