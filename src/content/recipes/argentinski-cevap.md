  en: Argentine Breaded Kebabs

ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 500 g mlevenog mesa
  - 3 kuvana krompira
  - 1 crni luk
  - beli luk po ukusu
  - 1 kašičica soli
  - 1 vegeta
  - 1 kašičica sode bikarbone
  - malo bibera
  - 1 jaje
  - prezle
  - jaje za pohovanje
  - ulje
  en:
  - 500 g minced meat
  - 3 boiled potatoes
  - 1 onion
  - garlic to taste
  - 1 tsp salt
  - 1 Vegeta
  - 1 tsp baking soda
  - a little pepper
  - 1 egg
  - breadcrumbs
  - egg for coating
  - oil
steps:
  sr:
  - Krompir izgnječiti i pomešati sa mesom lukom začinima i jajetom.
  - Oblikovati ćevape.
  - Uvaljati u prezle pa u jaje pa ponovo u prezle.
  - Pržiti na ulju.
  en:
  - Mash the potatoes and mix with the meat onion seasoning and egg.
  - Shape into kebabs.
  - Roll in breadcrumbs then egg then breadcrumbs again.
  - Fry in oil.
