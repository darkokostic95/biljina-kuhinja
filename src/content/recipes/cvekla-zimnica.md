---
slug: cvekla-zimnica
title:
  sr: Cvekla (zimnica)
  en: Pickled Beetroot
category: zimnica
tags:
- povrce
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Rastvor
    en: Brine
  sr:
  - cvekla
  - voda
  - 2,5% soli
  - 2,5% esencije
  en:
  - beetroot
  - water
  - 2.5% salt
  - 2.5% vinegar essence
steps:
  sr:
  - Cveklu kuvati na pari (voda do pola cvekle i poklopljeno).
  - Kuvanu cveklu očistiti iseći i staviti u tegle.
  - Naliti vrelim rastvorom i zatvoriti.
  - Tegle kuvati u vodi na 80-90 °C 35 minuta pa postepeno hladiti.
  en:
  - Steam the beetroot (water halfway up and covered).
  - Peel slice and pack into jars.
  - Fill with the hot brine and close.
  - Process the jars in water at 80–90 °C for 35 minutes then cool gradually.
notes:
  sr: Isto se sprema i kornišon samo se ne bari nego svež ide u tegle.
  en: Gherkins are made the same way except they go into the jars raw.
---
