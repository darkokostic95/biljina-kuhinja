title:
  sr: Salata sa susamom i sirom
  en: Sesame and Cheese Spread
category: salate-predjela
tags:
- susam
- sir
- brzo
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 200 g susama
  - 1/2 kg sira
  - 1 pavlaka
  - 1 kesica majoneza
  en:
  - 200 g sesame
  - 1/2 kg white cheese
  - 1 tub sour cream
  - 1 pack mayonnaise
steps:
  sr:
  - Propržiti susam da porumeni.
  - Isitniti sir i dodati pavlaku i majonez.
  - Sve izmešati i služiti.
  en:
  - Toast the sesame until golden.
  - Crumble the cheese and add the sour cream and mayonnaise.
  - Mix and serve.
