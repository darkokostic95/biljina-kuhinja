---
slug: pecena-paprika-zimnica
title:
  sr: Cepkana paprika (zimnica)
  en: Chopped Roasted Peppers in Jars
category: zimnica
tags:
- povrce
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 100 pečenih oljuštenih paprika
  - zejtin
  - mala šoljica šećera
  - so po ukusu
  - 1 kašičica vinskog sirćeta
  en:
  - 100 roasted peeled peppers
  - oil
  - a small cup sugar
  - salt to taste
  - 1 tsp wine vinegar
steps:
  sr:
  - Paprike iseckati; staviti zejtin da pokrije dno šerpe i smlačiti ga.
  - Dodati šećer so i sirće i krčkati 15 minuta uz mešanje.
  - Tegle zagrejati u rerni napuniti i odmah zatvoriti (celofan natopljen esencijom).
  - Vruće tegle uviti u ćebe dok se potpuno ne ohlade.
  en:
  - Chop the peppers; warm enough oil to cover the base of the pot.
  - Add the sugar salt and vinegar and simmer 15 minutes stirring.
  - Warm the jars in the oven fill and close at once (cellophane soaked in essence).
  - Wrap the hot jars in a blanket until completely cool.
