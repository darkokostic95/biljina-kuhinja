---
slug: slana-torta-od-tost-hleba
title:
  sr: Slana torta od tost hleba
  en: Savoury Toast Sandwich Cake
category: salate-predjela
tags:
- meso
- sir
- bez-pecenja
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 18 parčadi tost hleba
  - 300 g šunke
  - 400 g majoneza
  - 400 g pavlake
  - 4 kuvana jaja
  - 150 g sira
  - 200 g kačkavalja
  - 6 kiselih krastavaca
  - 50 ml kečapa
  en:
  - 18 slices toast bread
  - 300 g ham
  - 400 g mayonnaise
  - 400 g sour cream
  - 4 boiled eggs
  - 150 g white cheese
  - 200 g kashkaval cheese
  - 6 pickled gherkins
  - 50 ml ketchup
steps:
  sr:
  - Pavlaku i majonez sjediniti i podeliti na 3 dela.
  - U I deo izmrviti sir i 2 rendana krastavca.
  - U II deo rendanu šunku i kečap.
  - U III deo 4 rendana krastavca i rendana kuvana jaja.
  - Poređati 6 parčadi tosta i filovati — tri reda — a odozgo narendati kačkavalj.
  en:
  - Combine the sour cream and mayonnaise and divide into 3.
  - Into the first crumble the cheese and 2 grated gherkins.
  - Into the second grated ham and ketchup.
  - Into the third 4 grated gherkins and grated boiled eggs.
  - Lay 6 slices of toast per layer and fill — three layers — then grate kashkaval on top.
