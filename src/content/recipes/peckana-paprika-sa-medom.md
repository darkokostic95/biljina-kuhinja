slug: peckana-paprika-sa-medom
title:
  sr: Peckana paprika sa medom
  en: Roasted Peppers with Honey
category: zimnica
tags:
- povrce
- med
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 5 kg očišćene ispečkane paprike
  - 1 l vode
  - 1 šoljica esencije
  - 100 g meda
  - 100 g soli
  - 50 g šećera
  - 2 vinobrana
  - 2 konzervansa
  - beli luk
  - šargarepa
  - peršun
  en:
  - 5 kg cleaned lightly roasted peppers
  - 1 l water
  - 1 small cup vinegar essence
  - 100 g honey
  - 100 g salt
  - 50 g sugar
  - 2 sachets vinobran
  - 2 sachets preservative
  - garlic
  - carrot
  - parsley
steps:
  sr:
  - Vodu, esenciju, med, so, šećer, vinobran i konzervans da provri.
  - Naliti papriku i ostaviti 24 sata uz povremeno mešanje.
  - Slagati u tegle sa belim lukom, šargarepom i peršunom.
  en:
  - Bring the water, essence, honey, salt, sugar, vinobran and preservative to a boil.
  - Pour over the peppers and leave 24 hours stirring occasionally.
  - Pack into jars with garlic, carrot and parsley.
notes:
  sr: 'Verzija bez vode (IMG_5664, "dobra"): 5 kg paprike, 300 g šećera, 150 g soli, 1 šolja zejtina, 1 kašičica esencije i 1 salicil — stoji 12 sati uz mešanje pa u tegle.'
  en: 'Water-free version (IMG_5664, "good"): 5 kg peppers, 300 g sugar, 150 g salt, 1 cup oil, 1 tsp essence and 1 salicylic — stand 12 hours stirring then jar.'
