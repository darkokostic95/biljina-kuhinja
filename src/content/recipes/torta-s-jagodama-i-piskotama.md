title:
  sr: Torta s jagodama i piškotama
  en: Strawberry Ladyfinger Cake
category: torte
tags:
- jagode
- voce

- bez-pecenja
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 1 l želo šlaga
  - 2 čaše šećera
  - 2 kisele pavlake
  - 4 pakovanja piškota
  - sok ili mleko
  - jagode
  en:
  - 1 l liquid whipping cream
  - 2 glasses sugar
  - 2 tubs sour cream
  - 4 packs ladyfingers
  - juice or milk
  - strawberries
steps:
  sr:
  - Šlag umutiti sa šećerom pa dodati pavlaku.
  - Piškote samo provući kroz sok ili mleko.
  - 'Ređati: piškote, fil, jagode, fil, piškote, fil.'
  en:
  - Whip the cream with the sugar then add the sour cream.
  - Dip the ladyfingers briefly in juice or milk.
  - 'Layer: ladyfingers, cream, strawberries, cream, ladyfingers, cream.'
