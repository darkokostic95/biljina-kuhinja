slug: puslice-sa-kokosom-i-susamom
title:
  sr: Puslice sa kokosom i susamom
  en: Coconut Sesame Cookies
category: sitni-kolaci
tags:
- kokos
- susam
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 1 margarin
  - 1 jaje
  - 1 jogurtska čaša šećera
  - 1 jogurtska čaša zejtina (sipati do venca)
  - 100 g kokosa
  - 100 g susama
  - 600 g brašna
  - 1 prašak za pecivo
  en:
  - 1 margarine block
  - 1 egg
  - 1 yogurt glass sugar
  - 1 yogurt glass oil
  - 100 g coconut
  - 100 g sesame
  - 600 g flour
  - 1 baking powder
steps:
  sr:
  - Umutiti margarin jaje i šećer pa dodati zejtin.
  - Dodati kokos susam brašno i prašak i zamesiti.
  - Praviti bombice (kuglice) i ispeći.
  en:
  - Beat the margarine egg and sugar then add the oil.
  - Add the coconut sesame flour and baking powder and knead.
  - Shape balls and bake.
