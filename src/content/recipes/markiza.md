ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci

  sr:
  - 150 g keksa
  - mleko ili kafa
  - 1 margarin
  - 250 g šećera u prahu
  - 5 žumanaca
  - 200 g rastopljene čokolade
  - mleveni orasi
  en:
  - 150 g biscuits
  - milk or coffee
  - 1 margarine block
  - 250 g powdered sugar
  - 5 egg yolks
  - 200 g melted chocolate
  - ground walnuts
steps:
  sr:
  - Keks natopiti u mleko ili kafu i poređati na dno.
  - Umutiti margarin sa šećerom u prahu i žumancima (jedno po jedno).
  - Dodati rastopljenu čokoladu.
  - Staviti preko keksa i posuti mlevenim orasima.
  en:
  - Dip the biscuits in milk or coffee and line the dish.
  - Cream the margarine with the powdered sugar and yolks (one at a time).
  - Add the melted chocolate.
  - Spread over the biscuits and sprinkle with ground walnuts.
