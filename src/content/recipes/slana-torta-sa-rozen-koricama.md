  sr: Slana torta sa rozen koricama
  en: Savoury Wafer Layer Cake

ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 1 pakovanje slanih rozen korica
  - 1/2 l vode
  - 6 kašika gustina
  - malo soli
  - 1/2 margarina
  - 150 g ajvara
  - 150 g rendanog kačkavalja
  - 300 g šampinjona iz tegle
  - 4-5 kašika kuvanog spanaća
  - malo brašna
  en:
  - 1 pack savoury wafer sheets
  - 1/2 l water
  - 6 tbsp cornstarch
  - a little salt
  - 1/2 margarine block
  - 150 g ajvar
  - 150 g grated kashkaval cheese
  - 300 g jarred mushrooms
  - 4-5 tbsp cooked spinach
  - a little flour
steps:
  sr:
  - Skuvati vodu sa gustinom i solju.
  - Kad se ohladi umutiti sa margarinom i podeliti na 3 dela.
  - U I deo dodati ajvar, u II kačkavalj, u III šampinjone.
  - Posebno skuvati spanać, posoliti i dodati malo brašna.
  - 'Ređati: kora, I fil, kora, II fil, kora, III fil, spanać, kora, III fil, kora, II fil, kora, I fil.'
  en:
  - Cook the water with the cornstarch and salt.
  - When cool beat with the margarine and divide into 3.
  - Add ajvar to the first part, cheese to the second and mushrooms to the third.
  - Separately cook the spinach, season with salt and add a little flour.
  - 'Layer: sheet, filling I, sheet, II, sheet, III, spinach, sheet, III, sheet, II, sheet, I.'
