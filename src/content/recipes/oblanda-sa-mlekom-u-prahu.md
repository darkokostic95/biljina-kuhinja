title:
  sr: Oblanda sa pahuljicama
  en: Wafer Slice with White Flakes
category: bez-pecenja
tags:
- bez-pecenja
- oblanda
- cokolada
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 400 g šećera
  - malo vode
  - 100 g čokolade
  - 1 margarin
  - 200 g mleka u prahu
  - 100 g belih pahuljica
  - 2 lista oblande
  en:
  - 400 g sugar
  - a little water
  - 100 g chocolate
  - 1 margarine block
  - 200 g milk powder
  - 100 g white flakes (cereal)
  - 2 wafer sheets
steps:
  sr:
  - Šećer ukuvati sa malo vode da kane kao med.
  - Dodati čokoladu margarin i mleko u prahu.
  - Ravne strane oba lista oblande namazati smesom.
  - U ostatak smese dodati pahuljice i staviti između listova.
  en:
  - Boil the sugar with a little water until it drips like honey.
  - Add the chocolate margarine and milk powder.
  - Spread the flat sides of both wafer sheets with the mixture.
  - Stir the flakes into the rest and sandwich between the sheets.
