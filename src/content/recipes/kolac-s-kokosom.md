---
slug: kolac-s-kokosom
title:
  sr: Kolač s kokosom i limunom
  en: Coconut Lemon Sponge Slice
category: kolaci
tags:
- kokos
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Kora
    en: Sponge
  sr:
  - 5 belanaca
  - 300 g šećera
  - 1 isceđen limun
  - 100 g brašna
  - 150 g kokosovog brašna
  - 1/2 praška za pecivo
  - 3 šoljice zaslađenog mleka
  en:
  - 5 egg whites
  - 300 g sugar
  - juice of 1 lemon
  - 100 g flour
  - 150 g desiccated coconut
  - 1/2 baking powder
  - 3 small cups sweetened milk
- group:
    sr: Fil
    en: Filling
  sr:
  - 5 žumanaca
  - 5 kašika šećera
  - 6 kašika brašna
  - 1/2 l mleka
  - 1 margarin
  - 100 g šećera u prahu
  - 2 vanilin šećera
  en:
  - 5 egg yolks
  - 5 tbsp sugar
  - 6 tbsp flour
  - 1/2 l milk
  - 1 margarine block
  - 100 g powdered sugar
  - 2 vanilla sugar sachets
steps:
  sr:
  - Ispeći koru i preliti je zaslađenim mlekom; ohladiti.
  - Skuvati žumanca šećer i brašno u mleku.
  - Umutiti margarin sa šećerom u prahu i vanilom i sjediniti sa filom.
  - Premazati koru.
  en:
  - Bake the sponge and soak with sweetened milk; chill.
  - Cook the yolks sugar and flour in the milk.
  - Cream the margarine with powdered sugar and vanilla and combine.
  - Spread over the sponge.
