title:
  sr: Kiflice pečene u kalupu
  en: Walnut Crescents Dipped in Chocolate
category: sitni-kolaci
tags:
- orasi
- cokolada
- praznicno
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 400 g šećera u prahu
  - 200 g mlevenih oraha
  - 180 g brašna
  - 1 prašak za pecivo
  - 1 margarin
  - čokolada za umakanje
  en:
  - 400 g powdered sugar
  - 200 g ground walnuts
  - 180 g flour
  - 1 baking powder
  - 1 margarine block
  - chocolate for dipping
steps:
  sr:
  - Umesiti testo od svih sastojaka.
  - Puniti modlice i peći na 130 °C.
  - Pre nego što se ohlade krajeve umakati u čokoladu.
  en:
  - Knead a dough from all the ingredients.
  - Press into moulds and bake at 130 °C.
  - While still warm dip the ends in chocolate.
