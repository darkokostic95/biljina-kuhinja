steps:
  sr:
  - Umutiti belanca sa šećerom pa dodati vanilu kokos brašno i prašak.
  - Staviti na list oblande i u rernu zagrejanu na 250 °C pa smanjiti na 50 °C i sušiti.
  - Fil skuvati na pari i dodati margarin umućen sa šećerom u prahu.
  - Premazati koru i posuti kokosom.
  en:
  - Whisk the whites with the sugar then add the vanilla coconut flour and baking powder.
  - Spread on the wafer put into a 250 °C oven then lower to 50 °C and dry.
  - Cook the filling over steam and add the margarine creamed with powdered sugar.
  - Spread over and sprinkle with coconut.
