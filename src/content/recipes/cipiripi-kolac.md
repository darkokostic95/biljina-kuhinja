  sr: Cipiripi kolač
  en: Cipiripi Chocolate Slice
category: kolaci
tags:
- cokolada
sources:

ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Kora
    en: Base
  sr:
  - 6 belanaca
  - 1-2 šoljice šećera
  - 1 šoljica zejtina
  - 7 kašika brašna
  - 1 prašak za pecivo
  - 100 g rendane čokolade
  en:
  - 6 egg whites
  - 1-2 small cups sugar
  - 1 small cup oil
  - 7 tbsp flour
  - 1 baking powder
  - 100 g grated chocolate
- group:
    sr: Fil
    en: Filling
  sr:
  - 6 žumanaca
  - 6 kašika šećera
  - 1 margarin
  - 150 g čokolade za kuvanje
  - 1 Cipiripi blok čokolada
  en:
  - 6 egg yolks
  - 6 tbsp sugar
  - 1 margarine block
  - 150 g cooking chocolate
  - 1 Cipiripi chocolate bar
steps:
  sr:
  - Umutiti belanca sa šećerom pa dodati zejtin, brašno, prašak i čokoladu; ispeći.
  - Žumanca i šećer kuvati na pari pa dodati margarin i čokoladu da se istopi.
  - Premazati koru filom i odozgo narendati Cipiripi čokoladu.
  en:
  - Whisk the whites with the sugar then add the oil, flour, baking powder and chocolate; bake.
  - Cook the yolks and sugar over steam then add the margarine and chocolate to melt.
  - Spread the filling over the base and grate the Cipiripi chocolate on top.
