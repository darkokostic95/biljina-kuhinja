---
slug: pita-s-jogurtom-od-kora
title:
  sr: Pita s jogurtom od iseckanih kora
  en: Chopped Filo Yogurt Pie
category: pite
tags:
- brzo
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 1 pakovanje kora
  - 4 jaja
  - 1 čaša jogurta
  - malo više od pola čaše zejtina
  - ostatak čaše dopuniti kiselom vodom
  - 1 prašak za pecivo
  - malo soli
  en:
  - 1 pack filo sheets
  - 4 eggs
  - 1 glass yogurt
  - a little over half a glass oil
  - glass topped up with sparkling water
  - 1 baking powder
  - a little salt
steps:
  sr:
  - Kore iseckati na faltice (trake).
  - Umutiti jaja jogurt zejtin kiselu vodu prašak i so.
  - Preliti preko kora i peći u zagrejanoj rerni 15-20 minuta.
  en:
  - Chop the filo into pieces.
  - Whisk the eggs yogurt oil sparkling water baking powder and salt.
  - Pour over the filo and bake in a hot oven for 15–20 minutes.
