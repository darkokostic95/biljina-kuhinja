  en:
  - 250 g biscuits
  - 100 g powdered sugar
  - 2-3 tbsp cocoa
  - 100 g margarine
  - a little rum
  - a little milk
- group:
    sr: Fil
    en: Filling
  sr:
  - 1 puding od vanile
  - 3 kašike šećera
  - 1/2 l mleka
  - 100 g margarina
  - 100 g šećera u prahu
  - 0,5 kg jagoda
  en:
  - 1 vanilla pudding mix
  - 3 tbsp sugar
  - 1/2 l milk
  - 100 g margarine
  - 100 g powdered sugar
  - 0.5 kg strawberries
steps:
  sr:
  - Izdrobljen keks dobro izmešati sa šećerom u prahu, kakaom i margarinom, rumom i mlekom.
  - Staviti na dno duboke posude.
  - Preko poređati oprane jagode.
  - Skuvati puding sa šećerom i mlekom, ohlađen umutiti sa margarinom i šećerom u prahu i staviti preko jagoda.
  - Odozgo čokolada ili šlag.
  en:
  - Mix the crushed biscuits well with the powdered sugar, cocoa, margarine, rum and milk.
  - Press into the bottom of a deep dish.
  - Arrange the washed strawberries on top.
  - Cook the pudding with the sugar and milk; when cool beat it with the margarine and powdered sugar and spread over the strawberries.
  - Finish with chocolate or whipped cream.
