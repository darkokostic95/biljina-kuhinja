  sr:
  - Ratluk istopiti na tihoj vatri (sa malo margarina ili zejtina).
  - Dodati propržen susam i šećer u prahu.
  - Oblikovati piramidu ili salamu i preliti otopljenom čokoladom.
  en:
  - Melt the Turkish delight over low heat (with a little margarine or oil).
  - Add the toasted sesame and powdered sugar.
  - Shape into a pyramid or log and cover with melted chocolate.
