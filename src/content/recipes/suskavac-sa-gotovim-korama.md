---
slug: suskavac-sa-gotovim-korama
title:
  sr: Šuškavac sa gotovim korama
  en: Quick Meringue Cake with Ready Layers
category: torte
tags:
- lesnik
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Fil
    en: Filling
  sr:
  - 5 žumanaca
  - 8 kašika šećera
  - 1 kašika brašna
  - 1 kašika griza
  - 1 kesica pudinga od vanile
  - 1/2 l mleka
  - 1 margarin
  - 100 g lešnika
  - 200 g šlaga
  - gotove kore za šuškavac
  en:
  - 5 egg yolks
  - 8 tbsp sugar
  - 1 tbsp flour
  - 1 tbsp semolina
  - 1 vanilla pudding mix
  - 1/2 l milk
  - 1 margarine block
  - 100 g hazelnuts
  - 200 g whipped topping
  - ready-made meringue layers
steps:
  sr:
  - Skuvati žumanca šećer brašno griz i puding u mleku.
  - Kad se ohladi dodati prethodno umućen margarin.
  - Filovati gotove kore.
  - Odozgo lešnik i šlag.
  en:
  - Cook the yolks sugar flour semolina and pudding in the milk.
  - When cool beat in the creamed margarine.
  - Fill the ready meringue layers.
  - Top with hazelnuts and whipped cream.
