slug: slana-torta-sa-sunkom
title:
  sr: Slana torta sa šunkom
  en: Savoury Ham Layer Cake
category: salate-predjela
tags:
- meso
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Kora
    en: Sponge
  sr:
  - 125 g margarina
  - 1/2 l mleka
  - 4-6 kašika oštrog brašna
  - 6 jaja
  - so
  en:
  - 125 g margarine
  - 1/2 l milk
  - 4–6 tbsp strong flour
  - 6 eggs
  - salt
- group:
    sr: Fil
    en: Filling
  sr:
  - 250 g šunke
  - 2 kisele pavlake
  - malo majoneza
  - rendani kačkavalj ili jaja
  en:
  - 250 g ham
  - 2 tubs sour cream
  - a little mayonnaise
  - grated kashkaval or eggs
steps:
  sr:
  - Istopiti margarin na tihoj vatri dodati brašno pa polako mlako mleko i mešati dok se ne zgusne; ohladiti.
  - Umešati umućena žumanca pa sneg od belanaca.
  - Sipati u podmazan pleh i ispeći u dobro zagrejanoj rerni.
  - Koru podeliti na 2 dela i filovati seckanom šunkom majonezom i pavlakom.
  - Ukrasiti rendanim kačkavaljem ili jajima.
  en:
  - Melt the margarine over low heat add the flour then the warm milk slowly stirring until thick; cool.
  - Fold in the beaten yolks then the whisked whites.
  - Pour into a greased tin and bake in a hot oven.
  - Cut in 2 and fill with chopped ham mayonnaise and sour cream.
  - Decorate with grated cheese or egg.
