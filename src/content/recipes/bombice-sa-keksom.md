  - 1 egg
  - 300 g powdered sugar
  - 1 margarine block
  - 2 tbsp cocoa
  - 200 g ground biscuits
  - 2 vanilla sugar sachets
  - 1 tbsp rum
  - coconut or walnuts or chocolate for rolling

  sr:

  - Knead everything together.
  - Shape truffles and roll in coconut walnuts or chocolate.
  - Chill.
