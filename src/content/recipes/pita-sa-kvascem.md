  - 1 kašika suvog kvasca
  - 1 kašičica soli
  - 200 ml ulja
  - 300 ml mleka
  en:
  - 10 tbsp flour
  - 1 tbsp sugar
  - 1 tbsp dry yeast
  - 1 tsp salt
  - 200 ml oil
  - 300 ml milk
- group:
    sr: Fil
    en: Filling
  sr:
  - 3 jaja
  - 500 g sira
  - malo praška za pecivo
  - kore za pitu
  en:
  - 3 eggs
  - 500 g white cheese
  - a little baking powder
  - filo sheets
steps:
  sr:
  - Izmešati preliv.
  - Po 5 kora za 1 rolat — svaku premazati prelivom.
  - Na poslednju koru staviti fil od jaja i sira i uviti.
  - Premazati odozgo prelivom i ostaviti 2 sata pre pečenja.
  en:
  - Mix the batter.
  - Use 5 sheets per roll — brush each with the batter.
  - Spread the egg and cheese filling on the last sheet and roll up.
  - Brush the top and leave 2 hours before baking.
