title:
  sr: Svinjski medaljoni sa šampinjon sosom
  en: Pork Medallions with Mushroom Sauce
category: glavna-jela
tags:
- meso
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 200 g svinjskog filea
  - 100 g šampinjona
  - buter
  - brašno
  - hladno mleko
  - 1/2 l belog vina
  en:
  - 200 g pork tenderloin
  - 100 g mushrooms
  - butter
  - flour
  - cold milk
  - 1/2 l white wine
steps:
  sr:
  - Meso iseći na 3 dela saviti u okrugao oblik i ispeći na roštilju ili tiganju.
  - Buter zagrejati dodati brašno i hladno mleko da se dobije sos.
  - Dodati šampinjone i kuvati 2-3 minuta.
  - Meso preliti sosom.
  en:
  - Cut the meat into 3 pieces shape into rounds and grill or pan-fry.
  - Heat the butter add flour and cold milk to make a sauce.
  - Add the mushrooms and cook 2–3 minutes.
  - Pour the sauce over the meat.
