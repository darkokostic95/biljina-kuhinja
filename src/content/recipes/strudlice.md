title:
  sr: Štrudlice
  en: Little Strudels
category: peciva
tags:
- kvasac
- orasi
- cokolada
sources:
- IMG_5543
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Testo
    en: Dough
  sr:
  - 250 g omekšalog margarina
  - 2 žumanca
  - 3 kašike šećera
  - 200 ml toplog mleka
  - rendana kora od 1 limuna
  - 1 i po kašičica suvog kvasca
  - 700 g brašna
  en:
  - 250 g softened margarine
  - 2 egg yolks
  - 3 tbsp sugar
  - 200 ml warm milk
  - grated zest of 1 lemon
  - 1.5 tsp dry yeast
  - 700 g flour
- group:
    sr: Nadev (za svaki od 4 dela)
    en: Filling (for each of 4 parts)
  sr:
  - 3 kašike džema
  - 3 kašike šećera
  - 3 kašike mlevenih oraha
  en:
  - 3 tbsp jam
  - 3 tbsp sugar
  - 3 tbsp ground walnuts
- group:
    sr: Preliv
    en: Topping
  sr:
  - 150 g čokolade
  - 3 kašike zejtina
  en:
  - 150 g chocolate
  - 3 tbsp oil
steps:
  sr:
  - Kašikom umutiti margarin, dodati žumanca, šećer, toplo mleko i koru limuna i dobro sjediniti.
  - Kvasac podmesiti sa 4-5 kašika brašna i malo mleka i vode; kad naraste sipati u smesu sa margarinom.
  - Postepeno dodati brašno i umesiti testo rukama.
  - Testo podeliti na 4 dela i svaki tanko razvaljati u pravougaonik.
  - Svaki deo premazati džemom, posuti šećerom i mlevenim orasima.
  - Ispeći i odozgo premazati otopljenom čokoladom sa zejtinom.
  en:
  - Beat the margarine with a spoon; add the yolks, sugar, warm milk and lemon zest and mix well.
  - Activate the yeast with 4-5 tbsp flour and a little milk and water; once risen add it to the margarine mixture.
  - Add the flour gradually and knead by hand.
  - Divide into 4 parts and roll each thinly into a rectangle.
  - Spread each with jam, sprinkle with sugar and ground walnuts.
  - Bake and brush the top with chocolate melted with the oil.
