ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 1 kutija graška (250-300 g)
  - 1 čašica sirćeta
  - 1 tanjir šargarepe
  - 1 tanjir krompira
  - belanca od 6-8 kuvanih jaja
  - 1 tanjir krastavčića
  - majonez
  en:
  - 1 tin peas (250–300 g)
  - 1 small glass vinegar
  - 1 plate of carrots
  - 1 plate of potatoes
  - whites of 6–8 boiled eggs
  - 1 plate of gherkins
  - mayonnaise
steps:
  sr:
  - Grašak skuvati u slanoj vodi sa čašicom sirćeta.
  - Skuvati i iseći šargarepu i krompir na kockice.
  - Iseći belanca i krastavčiće.
  - Sve pomešati sa majonezom.
  en:
  - Cook the peas in salted water with the vinegar.
  - Cook and dice the carrots and potatoes.
  - Dice the egg whites and gherkins.
  - Mix everything with mayonnaise.
