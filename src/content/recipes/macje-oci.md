  sr: Mačje oči
  en: Cat's Eyes Banana Roll
category: torte
tags:

ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Kora
    en: Sponge
  sr:
  - 5 jaja
  - 5 kašika šećera
  - 5 kašika mlevenih oraha
  - 3-5 kašika brašna
  - malo praška za pecivo
  en:
  - 5 eggs
  - 5 tbsp sugar
  - 5 tbsp ground walnuts
  - 3–5 tbsp flour
  - a little baking powder
- group:
    sr: Fil
    en: Filling
  sr:
  - 3 jaja
  - 100 g šećera u prahu
  - 100 g čokolade
  - 1 margarin
  - 2-3 vanilin šećera
  - banane
  - šlag
  en:
  - 3 eggs
  - 100 g powdered sugar
  - 100 g chocolate
  - 1 margarine block
  - 2–3 vanilla sugar sachets
  - bananas
  - whipped cream
steps:
  sr:
  - Ispeći koru i vruću uviti u vlažnu krpu.
  - Jaja i šećer u prahu kuvati na pari pa dodati čokoladu margarin i vanilu.
  - Koru premazati tankim slojem fila poređati banane i preko fil.
  - Uviti u rolat odozgo fil pa šlag.
  en:
  - Bake the sponge and roll it up in a damp cloth while hot.
  - Cook the eggs and powdered sugar over steam then add the chocolate margarine and vanilla.
  - Spread a thin layer of filling on the sponge lay bananas on top and cover with filling.
  - Roll up spread filling on top then whipped cream.
