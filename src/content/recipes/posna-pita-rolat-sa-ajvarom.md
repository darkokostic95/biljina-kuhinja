---
slug: posna-pita-rolat-sa-ajvarom
title:
  sr: Posna pita rolat sa ajvarom
  en: Lenten Ajvar Filo Roll
category: pite
tags:
- posno
- povrce
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Smesa za premazivanje
    en: Spreading mixture
  sr:
  - 1 jogurtska čaša ulja
  - 2 čaše kisele vode
  - 10 kašika griza
  - 4 kašike brašna
  - 1 prašak za pecivo
  - so po ukusu
  en:
  - 1 yogurt glass oil
  - 2 glasses sparkling water
  - 10 tbsp semolina
  - 4 tbsp flour
  - 1 baking powder
  - salt to taste
- group:
    sr: Ostalo
    en: Other
  sr:
  - 1 pakovanje kora
  - ajvar (ili spanać)
  - susam
  - posni majonez
  - rendani posni kačkavalj
  en:
  - 1 pack filo sheets
  - ajvar (or spinach)
  - sesame
  - vegan mayonnaise
  - grated vegan cheese
steps:
  sr:
  - Kore podeliti na 3 dela.
  - Prve 3 kore premazati smesom a treću i ajvarom.
  - Ostale kore premazati samo smesom i na kraju posuti susamom.
  - Ispeći raširene pa vruće uviti u vlažnu krpu kao rolat.
  - Kad se ohladi premazati majonezom i posuti kačkavaljem.
  en:
  - Divide the sheets into 3 stacks.
  - Brush the first 3 sheets with the mixture and the third also with ajvar.
  - Brush the remaining sheets with the mixture only and sprinkle with sesame.
  - Bake flat then roll in a damp cloth while hot.
  - When cool spread with mayonnaise and sprinkle with cheese.
notes:
  sr: Drugi rolat može sa spanaćem umesto ajvara.
  en: Make the second roll with spinach instead of ajvar.
---
