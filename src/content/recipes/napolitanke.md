ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci

  sr:
  - 2 jaja
  - 1/2 kg šećera
  - 12 kašika mleka ili vode
  - 100 g čokolade
  - 1 margarin
  - listovi oblande
  en:
  - 2 eggs
  - 1/2 kg sugar
  - 12 tbsp milk or water
  - 100 g chocolate
  - 1 margarine block
  - wafer sheets
steps:
  sr:
  - Skuvati jaja šećer i mleko pa dodati čokoladu i margarin.
  - Mlakim filom filovati listove oblande.
  - Seći kad se stegne.
  en:
  - Cook the eggs sugar and milk then add the chocolate and margarine.
  - Spread the lukewarm filling between wafer sheets.
  - Cut once set.
