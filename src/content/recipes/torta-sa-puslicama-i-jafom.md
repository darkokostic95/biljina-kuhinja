title:
  sr: Torta sa puslicama i jafom
  en: Meringue Kiss and Jaffa Cake
category: torte
tags:
- keks

sources:
- IMG_5558
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 7 jaja
  - 7 kašika šećera
  - 250 g mleka u prahu
  - 200 g čokolade za kuvanje
  - 2 margarina
  - 300 g šećera u prahu
  - 2 kutije piškota
  - 2 kutije jafa keksa
  - 2 kese puslica
  - šlag
  - sok za potapanje
  en:
  - 7 eggs
  - 7 tbsp sugar
  - 250 g milk powder
  - 200 g cooking chocolate
  - 2 margarine blocks
  - 300 g powdered sugar
  - 2 boxes ladyfingers
  - 2 boxes Jaffa cakes
  - 2 bags meringue kisses
  - whipped cream
  - juice for dipping
steps:
  sr:
  - Jaja umutiti sa šećerom kao za patišpanj.
  - Postepeno dodavati mleko u prahu uz mućenje pa otopljenu čokoladu.
  - Posebno umutiti margarin sa šećerom u prahu i sjediniti sa filom.
  - 'Ređati: piškote — fil — jafa — fil — puslice — fil i odozgo šlag (piškote i jafu umakati u sok).'
  en:
  - Whisk the eggs with the sugar as for a sponge.
  - Gradually beat in the milk powder then the melted chocolate.
  - Separately cream the margarine with the powdered sugar and combine.
  - 'Layer: ladyfingers — filling — Jaffa cakes — filling — meringue kisses — filling and whipped cream on top (dip the ladyfingers and Jaffa cakes in juice).'
