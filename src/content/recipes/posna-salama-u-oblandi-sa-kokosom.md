  - 300 g water
  - 250 g sugar
  - 125 g vegan margarine
  - 1/2 kg biscuits
  - 2 tbsp cocoa
  - 100 g chopped walnuts (optional)
- group:
    sr: II fil (kokos)
    en: Filling II (coconut)
  sr:
  - 100 g vode
  - 200 g šećera
  - 125 g posnog margarina
  - 200 g kokosovog brašna

  - 100 g water
  - 200 g sugar
  - 125 g vegan margarine
  - 200 g desiccated coconut
  - jelly sweets
  - 3 wafer sheets
steps:
  sr:
  - Skuvati braon fil.
  - Vodu šećer i margarin da provri pa dodati kokos.
  - Svaku oblandu premazati braon filom a kokos staviti samo na početak kore.
  - Preko kokosa poređati žele bombone i uviti u salamu.
  en:
  - Cook the brown filling.
  - Boil the water sugar and margarine then add the coconut.
  - Spread each wafer with brown filling and put the coconut only along one edge.
  - Lay jelly sweets on the coconut and roll up.
