slug: rolat-sa-keksom-i-orasima
title:
  sr: Rolat sa keksom i orasima
  en: Biscuit and Walnut Roll
category: bez-pecenja
tags:
- bez-pecenja
- keks
- orasi
- cokolada
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 4 jaja
  - 300 g šećera
  - 1 margarin
  - 300 g seckanih oraha
  - 300 g sitno izdrobljenog keksa
  - 100 g suvog grožđa
  - 100 g čokolade
  en:
  - 4 eggs
  - 300 g sugar
  - 1 margarine block
  - 300 g chopped walnuts
  - 300 g finely crushed biscuits
  - 100 g raisins
  - 100 g chocolate
steps:
  sr:
  - Jaja i šećer kuvati na pari pa dodati margarin i čokoladu.
  - Umešati orahe, keks i grožđe.
  - Oblikovati rolat i ohladiti.
  en:
  - Cook the eggs and sugar over steam then add the margarine and chocolate.
  - Stir in the walnuts, biscuits and raisins.
  - Shape into a roll and chill.
