---
slug: slavski-kolac
title:
  sr: Slavski kolač
  en: Slava Bread
category: peciva
tags:
- praznicno
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Testo za figure
    en: Decoration dough
  sr:
  - 200 g brašna
  - 100 g gustina
  - 1 kašika soli
  - 1-2 dl hladne vode
  en:
  - 200 g flour
  - 100 g cornstarch
  - 1 tbsp salt
  - 1–2 dl cold water
- group:
    sr: Lepak za figure
    en: Glue
  sr:
  - 110 g brašna
  - 2 kašike gustina
  - 1 kašičica soli
  - 80 ml vode
  en:
  - 110 g flour
  - 2 tbsp cornstarch
  - 1 tsp salt
  - 80 ml water
steps:
  sr:
  - Sve pomešati a vodu dodavati postepeno i mešati viljuškom.
  - Ako se mrvi dodati još malo vode — testo ne treba da bude meko ni lepljivo.
  - Umešeno testo staviti u kesu i u frižider na 15 minuta.
  - Pripremiti tacnu sa krpom posutom brašnom za sušenje figura i vodu i četkicu za mazanje.
  - Seći testo po malo da se ostatak ne suši i praviti figure; lepiti ih lepkom.
  en:
  - Mix everything adding the water gradually with a fork.
  - If crumbly add a little more water — the dough should be neither soft nor sticky.
  - Put the dough in a bag and chill 15 minutes.
  - Prepare a floured cloth on a tray for drying the figures and water with a brush.
  - Cut off a little dough at a time so the rest doesn't dry and shape the figures; attach them with the glue.
---
