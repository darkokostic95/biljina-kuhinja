  - 3 kašike brašna

  en:
  - 6 eggs
  - 6 tbsp sugar
  - 12 tbsp ground walnuts
  - 3 tbsp flour
  - 1 vanilla sugar sachet
- group:
    sr: Sirup
    en: Syrup
  sr:
  - 2 kesice karamela od lešnika
  - 200 ml mleka
  en:
  - 2 bags hazelnut caramels
  - 200 ml milk
- group:
    sr: Fil
    en: Filling
  sr:
  - 1 margarin
  - 250 g šećera u prahu
  - 300 g mlevene plazme
  - 2 kisele pavlake
  - 100 g otopljene čokolade
  en:
  - 1 margarine block
  - 250 g powdered sugar
  - 300 g ground Plazma biscuits
  - 2 tubs sour cream
  - 100 g melted chocolate
steps:
  sr:
  - Ispeći koru.
  - Karamele rastopiti u mleku i preliti koru.
  - Umutiti margarin sa šećerom u prahu pa dodati plazmu, pavlaku i čokoladu.
  - Filovati i odozgo staviti čokoladu ili šlag.
  en:
  - Bake the sponge.
  - Melt the caramels in the milk and pour over the sponge.
  - Cream the margarine with the powdered sugar then add the Plazma, sour cream and chocolate.
  - Fill and top with chocolate or whipped cream.
