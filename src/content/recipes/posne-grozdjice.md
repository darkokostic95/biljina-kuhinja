steps:
  sr:
  - Ratluk i keks zamesiti i napraviti 6 loptica.
  - Od loptica napraviti štapiće.
  - 'Ređati: 3 štapića pa čokolada, 2 štapića pa čokolada, 1 štapić pa čokolada.'
  - Ostaviti da se stegne i seći.
  en:
  - Knead the Turkish delight with the biscuits and make 6 balls.
  - Roll the balls into sticks.
  - 'Stack: 3 sticks then chocolate, 2 sticks then chocolate, 1 stick then chocolate.'
  - Leave to set and slice.
