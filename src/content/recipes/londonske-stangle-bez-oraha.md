ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 200 g mlevenog keksa
  - 200 g šećera u prahu
  - 100 g putera
  - 150 g žele bombona
  - 1 jaje
  en:
  - 200 g ground biscuits
  - 200 g powdered sugar
  - 100 g butter
  - 150 g jelly sweets
  - 1 egg
- group:
    sr: Preliv
    en: Icing
  sr:
  - 150 g šećera u prahu
  - nekoliko kapi limuna
  - kesica vanile
  - vrela voda
  en:
  - 150 g powdered sugar
  - a few drops lemon juice
  - a sachet vanilla
  - boiling water
steps:
  sr:
  - Pomešati puter šećer u prahu keks žele bombone i jaje.
  - Napraviti rolnu.
  - Za preliv mutiti šećer sa vanilom i limunom dodajući kap po kap vrele vode dok se ne zgusne.
  - Preliti rolnu i ostaviti da se prosuši.
  en:
  - Mix the butter powdered sugar biscuits jelly sweets and egg.
  - Shape into a roll.
  - For the icing beat the sugar with vanilla and lemon adding boiling water drop by drop until thick.
  - Pour over the roll and leave to dry.
