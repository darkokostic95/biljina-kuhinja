  en: Chicken Breast with Vegetables

tags:

ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 3 pileće grudi
  - brašno
  - ulje
  - 200 g paradajz pirea
  - šolja pileće supe
  - 100 g šargarepe
  - 100 g tikvice
  - 100 g paprike
  - so
  en:
  - 3 chicken breasts
  - flour
  - oil
  - 200 g tomato purée
  - a cup of chicken stock
  - 100 g carrot
  - 100 g courgette
  - 100 g pepper
  - salt
steps:
  sr:
  - Povrće iseći na rezance.
  - Meso izlupati poprašiti i propržiti.
  - Dodati povrće supu i so i polako dinstati.
  - Na kraju dodati paradajz pire.
  en:
  - Cut the vegetables into strips.
  - Pound the chicken dredge in flour and fry.
  - Add the vegetables stock and salt and braise gently.
  - Stir in the tomato purée at the end.
