ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Kore (×4)
    en: Sponges (×4)
  sr:
  - 4 belanca
  - 5 kašika šećera
  - 5 kašika oraha
  - 1/2 kašike brašna
  en:
  - 4 egg whites
  - 5 tbsp sugar
  - 5 tbsp walnuts
  - 1/2 tbsp flour
- group:
    sr: Fil
    en: Filling
  sr:
  - 1 l mleka
  - 16 žumanaca
  - 17 kašika šećera
  - 10 kašika brašna
  - 2 vanile
  - 1 margarin
  - 150 g šećera u prahu
  en:
  - 1 l milk
  - 16 egg yolks
  - 17 tbsp sugar
  - 10 tbsp flour
  - 2 vanilla sugar sachets
  - 1 margarine block
  - 150 g powdered sugar
- group:
    sr: Čokoladni premaz
    en: Chocolate coating
  sr:
  - 250 g čokolade
  - 250 g margarina
  en:
  - 250 g chocolate
  - 250 g margarine
steps:
  sr:
  - Ispeći 4 kore.
  - Žumanca, šećer, brašno i vanilu skuvati u mleku.
  - U prohlađen krem dodati margarin umućen sa šećerom u prahu.
  - Svaku koru premazati vrućom smesom čokolade i margarina.
  - Kad se kore ohlade filovati; glazura po volji.
  en:
  - Bake 4 sponge layers.
  - Cook the yolks, sugar, flour and vanilla in the milk.
  - Beat the margarine with the powdered sugar into the cooled custard.
  - Brush each sponge with the hot chocolate–margarine mixture.
  - Once the sponges have cooled fill the cake; glaze as you like.
