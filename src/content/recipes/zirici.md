---
slug: zirici
title:
  sr: Žirići
  en: Acorn Cookies
category: sitni-kolaci
tags:
- kokos
- orasi
- cokolada
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Testo
    en: Dough
  sr:
  - 250 g šećera u prahu
  - 250 g masti
  - 1 prašak za pecivo
  - 500 g brašna
  - džem za spajanje
  en:
  - 250 g powdered sugar
  - 250 g lard
  - 1 baking powder
  - 500 g flour
  - jam for sandwiching
- group:
    sr: Šerbet
    en: Dip
  sr:
  - 8 kašika mleka
  - 4 kašike šećera
  - 4 kašike zejtina
  - 1 kašika kakaa
  - kokos ili mleveni orasi
  en:
  - 8 tbsp milk
  - 4 tbsp sugar
  - 4 tbsp oil
  - 1 tbsp cocoa
  - coconut or ground walnuts
steps:
  sr:
  - Umutiti šećer, mast i prašak pa dodati brašno.
  - Vaditi kašičicom i ređati u pleh.
  - Rernu zagrejati na 250 °C pa isključiti i peći 15 minuta.
  - Pečene spojiti džemom.
  - Polovinu umočiti u prokuvan šerbet pa u kokos ili orahe.
  en:
  - Beat the sugar, lard and baking powder then add the flour.
  - Spoon small mounds onto a tray.
  - Heat the oven to 250 °C then switch it off and bake 15 minutes.
  - Sandwich pairs with jam.
  - Dip one half in the boiled cocoa dip then in coconut or walnuts.
