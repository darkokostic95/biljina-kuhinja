ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 800 g mleka
  - 10 kašika šećera
  - 3 pudinga od vanile
  - 1 margarin
  - 1 kg banana
  - 300 g keksa
  - 2 kesice šlaga
  en:
  - 800 g milk
  - 10 tbsp sugar
  - 3 vanilla pudding mixes
  - 1 margarine block
  - 1 kg bananas
  - 300 g biscuits
  - 2 sachets whipped topping
steps:
  sr:
  - Skuvati pudinge u mleku sa šećerom.
  - Kad se prohladi dodati margarin i umutiti.
  - Iseckati banane i izdrobiti keks i sve izmešati sa filom.
  - Staviti u posudu obloženu celofanom pa u frižider, a zatim odozgo umućen šlag.
  en:
  - Cook the puddings in the milk with the sugar.
  - When it has cooled a little add the margarine and beat.
  - Slice the bananas, crush the biscuits and mix everything with the filling.
  - Put into a dish lined with cling film and chill, then top with whipped cream.
