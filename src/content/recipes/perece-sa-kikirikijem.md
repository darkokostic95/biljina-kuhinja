slug: perece-sa-kikirikijem
title:
  sr: Perece sa kikirikijem
  en: Peanut Pretzel Sticks
category: peciva
tags:
- kikiriki
- susam
- kvasac
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 1 margarin
  - 200 ml zejtina
  - 100 g pečenog susama
  - 200 g samlevenog kikirikija
  - 200 ml mlake vode
  - parče kvasca
  - so
  - 700-800 g brašna
  - 1 prašak za pecivo
  en:
  - 1 margarine block
  - 200 ml oil
  - 100 g toasted sesame
  - 200 g ground peanuts
  - 200 ml lukewarm water
  - a piece of fresh yeast
  - salt
  - 700–800 g flour
  - 1 baking powder
steps:
  sr:
  - Umutiti margarin pa dodati zejtin susam i kikiriki.
  - Dodati vodu sa rastopljenim kvascem so i brašno sa praškom.
  - Testo propustiti kroz mašinu za perece i ispeći.
  en:
  - Beat the margarine then add the oil sesame and peanuts.
  - Add the water with dissolved yeast the salt and the flour with baking powder.
  - Press the dough through a cookie/pretzel machine and bake.
