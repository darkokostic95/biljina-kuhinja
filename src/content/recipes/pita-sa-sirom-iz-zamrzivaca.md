title:
  sr: Pita sa sirom iz zamrzivača
  en: Freezer Cheese Pie
category: pite
tags:
- sir
sources:
- IMG_5611
- IMG_5674
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 1/2 kg kora za pitu
  - 500 g sira
  - 4 jaja
  - 1 pavlaka
  - 1 i po šoljica ulja
  - 1 prašak za pecivo
  - spanać (po želji)
  - jogurt i susam za vrh
  en:
  - 1/2 kg filo sheets
  - 500 g white cheese
  - 4 eggs
  - 1 tub sour cream
  - 1.5 small cups oil
  - 1 baking powder
  - spinach (optional)
  - yogurt and sesame for the top
steps:
  sr:
  - Kore podeliti na 2 (ili 3) dela.
  - Svaku koru premazati filom od sira jaja pavlake ulja i praška i uviti u rolat.
  - Staviti u zamrzivač i polusmrznutu iseći.
  - Odozgo malo jogurta i susam pa peći.
  en:
  - Divide the sheets into 2 (or 3) stacks.
  - Spread each sheet with the cheese egg cream oil and baking-powder filling and roll up.
  - Freeze and cut when half-frozen.
  - Top with a little yogurt and sesame and bake.
notes:
  sr: Bilja sada ne stavlja zejtin u fil nego kore premazuje smesom od 100 ml ulja i 100 ml mleka a kore deli na 3 dela.
  en: Bilja now leaves the oil out of the filling and brushes the sheets with 100 ml oil mixed with 100 ml milk dividing the sheets into 3.
