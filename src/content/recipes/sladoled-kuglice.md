ingredients:
- group:

  sr:
  - 200 g putera
  - 300 g šećera u prahu
  - 1 celo jaje
  - 50 g kakaa
  - 2 vanilin šećera
  - 1 kašika ruma
  - 200 g mlevenog keksa
  - orasi za valjanje
  en:
  - 200 g butter
  - 300 g powdered sugar
  - 1 whole egg
  - 50 g cocoa
  - 2 vanilla sugar sachets
  - 1 tbsp rum
  - 200 g ground biscuits
  - walnuts for rolling
steps:
  sr:
  - Penasto umutiti puter šećer u prahu i jaje.
  - Dodati kakao vanilu rum i keks.
  - Oblikovati kuglice i valjati u orahe.
  en:
  - Beat the butter powdered sugar and egg until fluffy.
  - Add the cocoa vanilla rum and biscuits.
  - Shape balls and roll in walnuts.
