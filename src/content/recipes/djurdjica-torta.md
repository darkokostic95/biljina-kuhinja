---
slug: djurdjica-torta
title:
  sr: Đurđica torta
  en: Đurđica Cake
category: torte
tags:
- orasi
- lesnik
- cokolada
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Kore (×3)
    en: Sponges (×3)
  sr:
  - 4 jaja
  - 4 kašike šećera
  - 8 kašika oraha
  - 2 kašike brašna
  - pola praška za pecivo
  - ceđena kafa za kvašenje
  en:
  - 4 eggs
  - 4 tbsp sugar
  - 8 tbsp walnuts
  - 2 tbsp flour
  - half a baking powder
  - brewed coffee for soaking
- group:
    sr: Fil
    en: Filling
  sr:
  - 250 g margarina
  - 250 g šećera u prahu
  - 150 g oraha
  - 150 g pečenog lešnika
  - 15 kašika vrelog zaslađenog mleka
  - 2 šlag kreme od čokolade
  - 100 g čokolade za kuvanje
  en:
  - 250 g margarine
  - 250 g powdered sugar
  - 150 g walnuts
  - 150 g roasted hazelnuts
  - 15 tbsp hot sweetened milk
  - 2 sachets chocolate whipped topping
  - 100 g cooking chocolate
steps:
  sr:
  - Umutiti jaja sa šećerom pa dodati orahe, brašno i prašak; ispeći 3 kore.
  - Kore pokvasiti kafom.
  - Margarin umutiti sa šećerom u prahu.
  - Orahe i lešnik popariti sa vrelim zaslađenim mlekom; kad se prohladi sjediniti sa margarinom.
  - Dodati čokoladni šlag i čokoladu.
  - 'Ređati: kora, fil, lešnik — ponoviti — i posuti lešnikom.'
  en:
  - Whisk the eggs with the sugar then add the walnuts, flour and baking powder; bake 3 sponges.
  - Soak the sponges with coffee.
  - Cream the margarine with the powdered sugar.
  - Scald the walnuts and hazelnuts with the hot sweetened milk; when cool combine with the margarine.
  - Add the chocolate whipped topping and chocolate.
  - 'Layer: sponge, filling, hazelnuts — repeat — and sprinkle with hazelnuts.'
