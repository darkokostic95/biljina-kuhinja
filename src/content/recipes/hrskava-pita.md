---
slug: hrskava-pita
title:
  sr: Hrskava pita
  en: Crispy Filo Pie
category: pite
tags:
- sir
- brzo
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Fil
    en: Batter
  sr:
  - 4 jaja
  - so
  - 100 g kukuruznog brašna
  - 300 g grčkog (ili bilo kog) jogurta
  - 200 ml ulja
  - 200 ml kisele vode
  - kore za pitu
  - sir ili drugi nadev
  en:
  - 4 eggs
  - salt
  - 100 g cornmeal
  - 300 g Greek (or any) yogurt
  - 200 ml oil
  - 200 ml sparkling water
  - filo sheets
  - cheese or other filling
steps:
  sr:
  - Umutiti fil.
  - Po 2 kore premazati filom i na početak staviti sir ili šta želite pa uviti.
  - Tepsiju namazati uljem a pitu odozgo premazati kiselom vodom i uljem.
  - Peći na 180 °C dok ne porumeni.
  en:
  - Whisk the batter.
  - Brush every 2 sheets with batter put cheese (or another filling) along the edge and roll up.
  - Oil the tin and brush the top with sparkling water and oil.
  - Bake at 180 °C until golden.
