title:
  sr: Salata sa pečurkama i piletinom
  en: Chicken and Mushroom Salad
category: salate-predjela
tags:
- piletina
sources:
- IMG_5654
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 1 veće kuvano pile
  - 1 velika kašika senfa
  - 300 g majoneza
  - 2 pavlake
  - 1 tegla pečuraka
  en:
  - 1 large boiled chicken
  - 1 heaped tbsp mustard
  - 300 g mayonnaise
  - 2 tubs sour cream
  - 1 jar mushrooms
steps:
  sr:
  - Piletinu i pečurke iseckati.
  - Pomešati sa senfom majonezom i pavlakom.
  en:
  - Chop the chicken and mushrooms.
  - Mix with the mustard mayonnaise and sour cream.
notes:
  sr: 'Druga varijanta: dodati krastavčiće i malo kuvanih makarona (makarone kuvati sa solju i zejtinom). Može i pola mere.'
  en: 'Variation: add gherkins and a little cooked macaroni (cooked with salt and oil). Halving works too.'
