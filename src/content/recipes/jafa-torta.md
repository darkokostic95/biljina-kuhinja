title:
  sr: Jafa torta
  en: Jaffa Cake Torte
category: torte
tags:
- keks

ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 1 paket jafa keksa
  - 350 ml mleka
  - 2 margarina
  - šlag
  en:
  - 1 pack Jaffa cakes
  - 350 ml milk
  - 2 margarine blocks
  - whipped cream
steps:
  sr:
  - Jafu podeliti na pola.
  - Mleko i margarin da provri pa sve pomešati sa jafom.
  - Staviti na tacnu i okrenuti; odozgo šlag.
  en:
  - Split the Jaffa cakes in half.
  - Bring the milk and margarine to the boil and mix with the biscuits.
  - Set on a plate and turn out; top with whipped cream.
