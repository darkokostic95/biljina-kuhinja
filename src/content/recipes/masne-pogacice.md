---
slug: masne-pogacice
title:
  sr: Masne pogačice
  en: Flaky Lard Scones
category: peciva
tags:
- kvasac
- sir
- praznicno
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 750 g brašna
  - 250 g margarina ili masti
  - 200-300 g sitnog sira
  - 1 kesica suvog ili kocka svežeg kvasca
  - 1 žumance
  - 1 kašika soli
  - 1 dl mleka i mlaka voda po potrebi
  - belance i susam za premazivanje
  en:
  - 750 g flour
  - 250 g margarine or lard
  - 200–300 g crumbly white cheese
  - 1 sachet dry or 1 cube fresh yeast
  - 1 egg yolk
  - 1 tbsp salt
  - 1 dl milk and lukewarm water as needed
  - egg white and sesame for brushing
steps:
  sr:
  - Sve zamesiti i razvući testo.
  - Izrendati margarin preko testa i uviti u rolat.
  - Ostaviti u frižideru preko noći.
  - Ujutru ponovo razvući i vaditi pogačice.
  - Premazati belancetom i posuti susamom pa ispeći.
  en:
  - Knead everything together and roll out.
  - Grate the margarine over the dough and roll up.
  - Leave in the fridge overnight.
  - Next morning roll out again and cut rounds.
  - Brush with egg white and sprinkle with sesame then bake.
