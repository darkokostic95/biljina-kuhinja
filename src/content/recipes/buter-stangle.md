slug: buter-stangle
title:
  sr: Buter štangle
  en: Butter Walnut Bars
category: kolaci
tags:
- orasi
sources:
- IMG_5591
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Kora
    en: Base
  sr:
  - 150 g šećera
  - 150 g margarina
  - 100 g mlevenih oraha
  - 100 g brašna
  - 4 belanca
  en:
  - 150 g sugar
  - 150 g margarine
  - 100 g ground walnuts
  - 100 g flour
  - 4 egg whites
- group:
    sr: I fil
    en: Filling I
  sr:
  - 4 žumanca
  - 150 g šećera u prahu
  en:
  - 4 egg yolks
  - 150 g powdered sugar
- group:
    sr: II fil
    en: Filling II
  sr:
  - 2 šoljice mleka
  - 10 kašika šećera
  - 200 g mlevenih oraha
  - 1 vanilin šećer
  - 100 g margarina
  - orasi za posipanje
  en:
  - 2 small cups milk
  - 10 tbsp sugar
  - 200 g ground walnuts
  - 1 vanilla sugar sachet
  - 100 g margarine
  - walnuts for sprinkling
steps:
  sr:
  - Umutiti šećer i margarin pa dodati orahe i brašno i na kraju sneg od belanaca; ispeći.
  - Žumanca pomešati sa šećerom u prahu, premazati koru i vratiti u rernu da se prosuši.
  - Mleko i šećer da provri pa dodati orahe i vanilu; kad se ohladi dodati umućen margarin.
  - Premazati ohlađen kolač, posuti orasima i seći na štangle.
  en:
  - Cream the sugar and margarine then add the walnuts, flour and finally the whisked whites; bake.
  - Mix the yolks with powdered sugar, spread over and return to the oven to dry.
  - Boil the milk and sugar then add the walnuts and vanilla; when cool beat in the margarine.
  - Spread over the cooled cake, sprinkle with walnuts and cut into bars.
