title:
  sr: Salama od smokava u oblandi
  en: Fig and Walnut Wafer Slice
category: bez-pecenja
tags:
- bez-pecenja
- oblanda
- orasi
- voce
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 1/2 kg šećera
  - 1/2 l vode
  - 1/2 kg iseckanih smokava
  - 1 pakovanje žele bombona
  - 200 g seckanih oraha
  - 150 g suvog grožđa
  - 4 lista oblande
  en:
  - 1/2 kg sugar
  - 1/2 l water
  - 1/2 kg chopped dried figs
  - 1 pack jelly sweets
  - 200 g chopped walnuts
  - 150 g raisins
  - 4 wafer sheets
steps:
  sr:
  - Ukuvati šećer sa vodom i dodati smokve.
  - Na kraju dodati žele bombone orahe i grožđe.
  - Filovati 4 lista oblande.
  en:
  - Boil the sugar with the water and add the figs.
  - Finally add the jelly sweets walnuts and raisins.
  - Fill 4 wafer sheets.
