---
slug: pijano-meso
title:
  sr: Pijano meso
  en: Drunken Meat with Potatoes
category: glavna-jela
tags:
- meso
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 500 g junetine teletine ili svinjetine
  - 3 glavice crnog luka na režnjeve
  - 2 male glavice belog luka
  - biber
  - 1 lovorov list
  - peršun
  - 2 čaše vina
  - 2 čaše vode
  - 1 čaša zejtina
  - 600 g krompira na kriške
  - 2 kašike vegete
  - malo aleve paprike
  en:
  - 500 g beef veal or pork
  - 3 onions in wedges
  - 2 small heads garlic
  - pepper
  - 1 bay leaf
  - parsley
  - 2 glasses wine
  - 2 glasses water
  - 1 glass oil
  - 600 g potatoes in wedges
  - 2 tbsp Vegeta
  - a little paprika
steps:
  sr:
  - Meso luk beli luk i začine staviti u šerpu.
  - Preliti vinom vodom i zejtinom pa dodati krompir.
  - Odozgo vegeta i aleva paprika.
  - Kad provri kuvati — teletinu 15 minuta a junetinu i svinjetinu 20-25 minuta.
  en:
  - Put the meat onions garlic and herbs in a pot.
  - Pour over the wine water and oil then add the potatoes.
  - Sprinkle Vegeta and paprika on top.
  - Once boiling cook — veal 15 minutes beef or pork 20–25 minutes.
