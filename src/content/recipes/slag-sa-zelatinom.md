  sr: Stabilan šlag sa želatinom
  en: Stabilised Whipped Cream

ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 300 g mleka
  - 1 želatin
  - 2-3 kašike šećera
  - 2 šlag kreme
  en:
  - 300 g milk
  - 1 sachet gelatine
  - 2–3 tbsp sugar
  - 2 sachets whipped topping
steps:
  sr:
  - Mleko želatin i šećer da vri 5 minuta.
  - Staviti 30 minuta u zamrzivač.
  - Umutiti šlag i postepeno dodati mleko sa želatinom.
  en:
  - Boil the milk gelatine and sugar for 5 minutes.
  - Put in the freezer for 30 minutes.
  - Whip the topping and gradually beat in the gelatine milk.
