ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci

  sr:
  - 250 g šećera
  - malo vode
  - kašičica ruma
  - 150 g mlevenih oraha
  - 250 g mlevenog keksa
  - 250 g žele bombona
  - glazura
  en:
  - 250 g sugar
  - a little water
  - a tsp rum
  - 150 g ground walnuts
  - 250 g ground biscuits
  - 250 g jelly sweets
  - glaze
steps:
  sr:
  - Ukuvati šećer sa malo vode i rumom.
  - Dodati orahe keks i žele bombone i dobro umešati.
  - Oblikovati rolat i preliti glazurom.
  en:
  - Boil the sugar with a little water and the rum.
  - Add the walnuts biscuits and jelly sweets and mix well.
  - Shape a roll and glaze.
