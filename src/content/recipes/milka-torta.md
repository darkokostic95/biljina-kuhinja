  en:
  - 7 egg whites
  - 7 tbsp sugar
  - 9 tbsp ground walnuts

  - a little baking powder

    sr: Karamel

  - 200 g lešnik karamela

  - 200 g hazelnut caramels
  - 250 ml milk
- group:
    sr: Fil
    en: Filling
  sr:
  - 1 l mleka
  - 3 pudinga od vanile
  - 4 kašike brašna
  - 18 žumanaca
  - 24 kašike šećera
  - 2 margarina
  - 200 g bele čokolade
  - 200 g crne čokolade
  en:
  - 1 l milk
  - 3 vanilla pudding mixes
  - 4 tbsp flour
  - 18 egg yolks
  - 24 tbsp sugar
  - 2 margarine blocks
  - 200 g white chocolate
  - 200 g dark chocolate
steps:
  sr:
  - Ispeći 3 kore.
  - Karamele otopiti u mleku i preliti sve tri kore.
  - Skuvati fil; kad se ohladi dodati margarine i podeliti na dva dela.
  - U jedan deo dodati struganu belu čokoladu a u drugi crnu.
  - 'Filovati: kora, braon fil, žuti fil — ponoviti — odozgo šlag.'
  en:
  - Bake 3 sponge layers.
  - Melt the caramels in the milk and pour over all three sponges.
  - Cook the filling; when cool add the margarine and divide in two.
  - Add grated white chocolate to one half and dark to the other.
  - 'Layer: sponge, brown filling, yellow filling — repeat — whipped cream on top.'
