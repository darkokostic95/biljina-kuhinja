  en: Savoury Cheese Bajadera
category: salate-predjela
tags:
- sir

ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 1 kg sira
  - 1 margarin
  - 100 g seckane šunke
  - malo peršuna
  - aleva paprika
  - 100 g susama
  en:
  - 1 kg white cheese
  - 1 margarine block
  - 100 g chopped ham
  - a little parsley
  - paprika
  - 100 g sesame
steps:
  sr:
  - Sir izrendati i dodati umućen margarin; podeliti na 3 dela.
  - U I deo dodati šunku i peršun.
  - U II deo dodati alevu papriku.
  - U III deo dodati pečen susam.
  - Slagati slojeve jedan preko drugog i ohladiti.
  en:
  - Grate the cheese and add the creamed margarine; divide into 3.
  - Add ham and parsley to the first.
  - Add paprika to the second.
  - Add toasted sesame to the third.
  - Layer one over the other and chill.
