---
slug: sirup-za-iskasljavanje-sa-orasima
title:
  sr: Sirup za iskašljavanje sa orasima
  en: Walnut Cough Syrup
category: napici
tags:
- orasi
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 600 g vode
  - 10 kašika šećera
  - 2 kašike zejtina
  - 9 oraha (malo slomljenih)
  en:
  - 600 g water
  - 10 tbsp sugar
  - 2 tbsp oil
  - 9 walnuts (lightly cracked)
steps:
  sr:
  - Sve kuvati kao za slatko sa ljuspom (orasi u ljusci).
  - Piti 3 puta dnevno po 3 kašike pola sata pre jela.
  - Posle 7 dana po potrebi ponoviti još jednu turu.
  en:
  - Cook everything like a fruit preserve, with the shells on.
  - Take 3 tbsp three times a day half an hour before meals.
  - After 7 days repeat one more batch if needed.
notes:
  sr: Narodni recept — nije zamena za lekara.
  en: A folk remedy — not a substitute for a doctor.
