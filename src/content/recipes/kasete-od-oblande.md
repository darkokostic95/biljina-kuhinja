slug: kasete-od-oblande
title:
  sr: Kasete od oblande
  en: Wafer Cassettes
category: bez-pecenja
tags:
- bez-pecenja
- oblanda
- keks
- orasi
- cokolada
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 20 kašika vode
  - 18 kašika šećera
  - malo manje od 1 margarina
  - 200 g mlevenih oraha
  - 200 g keksa
  - 100 g čokolade
  - 1 list oblande
  en:
  - 20 tbsp water
  - 18 tbsp sugar
  - just under 1 margarine block
  - 200 g ground walnuts
  - 200 g biscuits
  - 100 g chocolate
  - 1 wafer sheet
steps:
  sr:
  - Vodu šećer i margarin da provri pa dodati orahe i keks.
  - Podeliti fil na 2 dela — u veći dodati čokoladu.
  - List oblande podeliti na 4 trake po dužini.
  - 'Filovati: oblanda, svetli fil, oblanda, braon fil — 2 reda — pa opet braon pa beli fil i oblanda.'
  en:
  - Bring the water sugar and margarine to a boil then add the walnuts and biscuits.
  - Divide in 2 — add chocolate to the larger part.
  - Cut the wafer sheet lengthwise into 4 strips.
  - 'Layer: wafer, light filling, wafer, brown filling — 2 rows — then brown again, white filling and wafer.'
