title:
  sr: Slane štangle sa sirom
  en: Cheese Sticks
category: peciva
tags:
- sir
sources:
- IMG_5593
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 1 margarin
  - 1 jaje
  - 1 belance
  - 150 g sira
  - malo soli
  - 15 kašika mleka
  - 1 prašak za pecivo
  - 1/2 kg brašna
  en:
  - 1 margarine block
  - 1 egg
  - 1 egg white
  - 150 g white cheese
  - a little salt
  - 15 tbsp milk
  - 1 baking powder
  - 1/2 kg flour
steps:
  sr:
  - Umutiti margarin, jaje, belance, sir, so i mleko.
  - Dodati prašak i brašno i umesiti testo.
  - Razvaljati, iseći na štangle i ispeći.
  en:
  - Beat the margarine, egg, egg white, cheese, salt and milk.
  - Add the baking powder and flour and knead.
  - Roll out, cut into sticks and bake.
