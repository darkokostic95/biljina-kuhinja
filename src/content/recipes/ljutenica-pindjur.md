ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 6 kg oljuštene pečene paprike
  - 6 kg poparenog paradajza
  - 800 g zejtina
  - 1 jogurtska čaša šećera
  - 1 jogurtska čaša alkoholnog sirćeta
  - 4 kašike soli
  - 4-5 glavica belog luka
  - 1 konzervans
  en:
  - 6 kg peeled roasted peppers
  - 6 kg blanched tomatoes
  - 800 g oil
  - 1 yogurt glass sugar
  - 1 yogurt glass spirit vinegar
  - 4 tbsp salt
  - 4-5 heads garlic
  - 1 sachet preservative
steps:
  sr:
  - Upržiti paradajz pa dodati zejtin.
  - Dodati papriku, šećer, sirće i so (po potrebi više) i kuvati.
  - Kad je gotovo dodati beli luk i konzervans.
  - Sipati u tegle.
  en:
  - Cook the tomatoes down then add the oil.
  - Add the peppers, sugar, vinegar and salt (more if needed) and cook.
  - When done add the garlic and preservative.
  - Fill into jars.
