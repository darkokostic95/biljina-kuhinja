---
slug: detelina
title:
  sr: Detelina
  en: Clover Three-Colour Pyramid
category: bez-pecenja
tags:
- bez-pecenja
- orasi
- cokolada
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 300 g šećera u prahu
  - 1-2 šake mlevenih oraha
  - malo vrele vode
  - malo čokolade ili kakaa
  - ekstrakt maline ili jagode
  en:
  - 300 g powdered sugar
  - 1–2 handfuls ground walnuts
  - a little boiling water
  - a little chocolate or cocoa
  - raspberry or strawberry extract
- group:
    sr: Preliv
    en: Glaze
  sr:
  - 2 štanglice čokolade
  - 1 kašika ulja
  - buter veličine oraha
  en:
  - 2 small bars chocolate
  - 1 tbsp oil
  - a walnut-sized knob of butter
steps:
  sr:
  - Zamesiti šećer i orahe sa malo vrele vode da testo bude tvrdo.
  - Podeliti na 3 dela — u prvi čokolada ili kakao u drugi ekstrakt maline a treći ostaje beo.
  - Složiti piramidu od tri boje.
  - Preliti prelivom od čokolade ulja i butera.
  en:
  - Knead the sugar and walnuts with a little boiling water into a stiff paste.
  - Divide into 3 — chocolate or cocoa in one raspberry extract in another and leave the third plain.
  - Stack into a three-coloured pyramid.
  - Pour over the chocolate oil and butter glaze.
