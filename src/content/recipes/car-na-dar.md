ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 1 margarin
  - 250 g šećera u prahu
  - 200 g čokolade
  - 7 kašika meda
  - 100 g preprženog susama
  - 100 g seckanih oraha
  - malo manje od 1 kutije Čar na dar sa kakaom
  en:
  - 1 margarine block
  - 250 g powdered sugar
  - 200 g chocolate
  - 7 tbsp honey
  - 100 g toasted sesame
  - 100 g chopped walnuts
  - just under 1 box Čar na dar cocoa cereal
steps:
  sr:
  - U većoj šerpi istopiti iseckan margarin šećer u prahu i čokoladu.
  - Dodati med susam i orahe pa Čar na dar i sve izmešati.
  - Sipati u podmazan pleh i dobro istapkati rukom.
  - Kad se prohladi staviti u frižider 1-2 sata i seći oštrim nožem na štangle.
  en:
  - Melt the chopped margarine powdered sugar and chocolate in a large pan.
  - Add the honey sesame and walnuts then the cereal and mix.
  - Tip into a greased tin and press down firmly by hand.
  - When cool chill 1–2 hours and cut into bars with a sharp knife.
