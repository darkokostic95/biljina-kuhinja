---
slug: pijana-pogaca
title:
  sr: Pijana pogača
  en: Drunken Bread
category: peciva
tags:
- kvasac
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 1 kg brašna
  - 1 kašičica soli
  - 1/4 margarina
  - 1 jaje
  - 1 kašika zejtina
  - 1 kašika šećera
  - 1 kašika rakije
  - 1 kašika belog vina
  - 1 kašika sirćeta
  - 1/2 l mleka
  - parče kvasca
  - žumance za premazivanje
  en:
  - 1 kg flour
  - 1 tsp salt
  - 1/4 margarine block
  - 1 egg
  - 1 tbsp oil
  - 1 tbsp sugar
  - 1 tbsp rakija
  - 1 tbsp white wine
  - 1 tbsp vinegar
  - 1/2 l milk
  - a piece of fresh yeast
  - egg yolk for brushing
steps:
  sr:
  - Kvasac rastopiti u mleku.
  - Zamesiti pogaču od svih sastojaka.
  - Premazati žumancetom i ispeći kad nadođe.
  en:
  - Dissolve the yeast in the milk.
  - Knead the bread from all the ingredients.
  - Brush with yolk and bake once risen.
---
