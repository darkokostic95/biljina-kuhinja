ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 400 g šećera
  - 300 g vode
  - 250 g margarina
  - 100 g čokolade
  - 400 g mlevenog keksa
  - 300 g mlevenih oraha
  - 300 g jafa keksa
  - 2 veća lista oblande
  en:
  - 400 g sugar
  - 300 g water
  - 250 g margarine
  - 100 g chocolate
  - 400 g ground biscuits
  - 300 g ground walnuts
  - 300 g Jaffa cakes
  - 2 large wafer sheets
steps:
  sr:
  - Šećer i vodu prokuvati pa dodati margarin i čokoladu da se istope.
  - Skloniti sa vatre i umešati keks i orahe; prohladiti.
  - Na list oblande staviti pola fila, poređati jafu pa ostatak fila.
  - Pokriti drugim listom.
  en:
  - Boil the sugar and water then melt in the margarine and chocolate.
  - Take off the heat and stir in the biscuits and walnuts; cool slightly.
  - Spread half on a wafer sheet, lay the Jaffa cakes on top then the rest of the filling.
  - Cover with the second sheet.
