---
slug: meke-kiflice-sa-mascu
title:
  sr: Meke kiflice sa mašću
  en: Soft Lard Crescent Rolls
category: peciva
tags:
- kvasac
- susam
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 1 kg mekog brašna
  - 1 kvasac
  - 2 kašičice soli
  - 1/2 l mleka
  - 200 g masti
  - 50 g ulja
  - 1 jaje
  - 2 dcl kiselog mleka
  - 3 kašike šećera
  - suncokret lan i susam za posipanje
  en:
  - 1 kg soft flour
  - 1 yeast
  - 2 tsp salt
  - 1/2 l milk
  - 200 g lard
  - 50 g oil
  - 1 egg
  - 2 dl sour milk
  - 3 tbsp sugar
  - sunflower flax and sesame for sprinkling
steps:
  sr:
  - Podmesiti kvasac pa zamesiti srednje testo od svih sastojaka; odmara 30 minuta.
  - Podeliti na 36 loptica.
  - Testo razvlačiti prema spolja i uviti kiflice; odmaraju 10-15 minuta.
  - Posuti semenkama i peći na 200 °C dok ne porumene.
  en:
  - Start the yeast then knead a medium dough from everything; rest 30 minutes.
  - Divide into 36 balls.
  - Roll out and shape crescents; rest 10–15 minutes.
  - Sprinkle with seeds and bake at 200 °C until golden.
notes:
  sr: Testo se pravi i sa heljdinim brašnom — 1 čaša heljde na 2 čaše običnog brašna.
  en: Also works with buckwheat — 1 glass buckwheat to 2 glasses plain flour.
---
