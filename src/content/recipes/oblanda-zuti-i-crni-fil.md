---
slug: oblanda-zuti-i-crni-fil
title:
  sr: Oblanda sa žutim i crnim filom
  en: Two-Filling Wafer Slice
category: bez-pecenja
tags:
- oblanda
- keks
- cokolada
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Žuti fil
    en: Yellow filling
  sr:
  - 6 žumanaca
  - 250-350 g šećera u prahu
  - 1 margarin
  en:
  - 6 egg yolks
  - 250–350 g powdered sugar
  - 1 margarine block
- group:
    sr: Crni fil
    en: Dark filling
  sr:
  - 6 belanaca
  - 6 kašika šećera ili 250 g šećera u prahu
  - 150-250 g mlevenog keksa
  - 100 g čokolade
  - listovi oblande
  en:
  - 6 egg whites
  - 6 tbsp sugar or 250 g powdered sugar
  - 150–250 g ground biscuits
  - 100 g chocolate
  - wafer sheets
steps:
  sr:
  - Žumanca, šećer u prahu i margarin izmiksirati.
  - Belanca umutiti sa šećerom pa dodati keks i rastopljenu čokoladu.
  - Filovati oblandu naizmenično žutim i crnim filom.
  en:
  - Beat the yolks, powdered sugar and margarine.
  - Whisk the whites with the sugar then add the biscuits and melted chocolate.
  - Fill the wafer sheets alternating yellow and dark filling.
notes:
  sr: Spojena dva zapisa (IMG_5593 i "Šarena oblanda" IMG_5596) — isti recept sa malo drugačijim merama.
  en: Two entries combined (IMG_5593 and "Šarena oblanda" IMG_5596) — the same recipe with slightly different amounts.
