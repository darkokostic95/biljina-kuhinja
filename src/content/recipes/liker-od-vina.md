  en: Wine and Vanilla Liqueur

- group:
    sr: Sastojci

  sr:
  - 1 l vina

  - 400 g rakije
  en:
  - 1 l wine
  - 1 kg sugar
  - 6 vanilla sugar sachets
  - 400 g rakija

  sr:
  - Vino i šećer da vri na laganoj vatri 10 minuta.
  - Dodati vanilu i rakiju.
  - Ohladiti i sipati u flaše.
  en:
  - Simmer the wine and sugar gently for 10 minutes.
  - Add the vanilla and rakija.
  - Cool and bottle.
