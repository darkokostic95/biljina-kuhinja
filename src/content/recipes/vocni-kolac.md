title:
  sr: Voćni kolač
  en: Fruit Sponge Slice
category: kolaci
tags:
- voce
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Patišpanj
    en: Sponge
  sr:
  - 4 jaja
  - 12 kašika šećera
  - 12 kašika mleka
  - 12 kašika ulja
  - 12 kašika brašna
  - 1 prašak za pecivo
  - 400 g mleka za prelivanje
  en:
  - 4 eggs
  - 12 tbsp sugar
  - 12 tbsp milk
  - 12 tbsp oil
  - 12 tbsp flour
  - 1 baking powder
  - 400 g milk for soaking
- group:
    sr: Voćni fil
    en: Fruit topping
  sr:
  - 1/2 kg voća
  - 300 g šećera
  - 1 čaša vode
  - 2 pudinga od vanile
  - šlag
  en:
  - 1/2 kg fruit
  - 300 g sugar
  - 1 glass water
  - 2 vanilla pudding mixes
  - whipped cream
steps:
  sr:
  - Ispeći patišpanj i vruć preliti mlekom.
  - Voće prokuvati sa šećerom i vodom pa izvaditi i poređati preko patišpanja.
  - U tom soku skuvati pudinge i preliti preko voća.
  - Odozgo šlag.
  en:
  - Bake the sponge and pour the milk over it while hot.
  - Simmer the fruit with the sugar and water then lift it out and arrange on the sponge.
  - Cook the puddings in that syrup and pour over the fruit.
  - Top with whipped cream.
notes:
  sr: 'Druga verzija (IMG_5706): patišpanj od 5 jaja, preko pošećerene jagode; fil od 1 l mleka, 8 kašika brašna i 8 kašika šećera, sjedinjen sa margarinom umućenim sa 200 g šećera u prahu, pa dodati 1 šlag kremu i koru od 1 limuna i mutiti još 5 minuta; odozgo šlag.'
  en: 'Another version (IMG_5706): a 5-egg sponge topped with sugared strawberries; filling of 1 l milk, 8 tbsp flour and 8 tbsp sugar combined with margarine creamed with 200 g powdered sugar, then 1 whipped topping and the zest of 1 lemon beaten in for 5 more minutes; whipped cream on top.'
