title:
  sr: Torta s jafom i piškotama
  en: Jaffa and Ladyfinger Banana Cake
category: torte
tags:
- keks
- plazma
- banane
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Kora od keksa
    en: Biscuit base
  sr:
  - 4 žumanca
  - 250 g šećera u prahu
  - 1 margarin
  - 300 g mlevenog keksa ili plazme
  - 200 g soka od narandže
  en:
  - 4 egg yolks
  - 250 g powdered sugar
  - 1 margarine block
  - 300 g ground biscuits or Plazma
  - 200 g orange juice
- group:
    sr: Fil
    en: Filling
  sr:
  - 1,5 l mleka
  - 4 žumanca
  - 200 g šećera
  - 2 pudinga od vanile
  - 10 kašika brašna
  - 1 margarin
  - jafa keks
  - piškote
  - krem banane
  en:
  - 1.5 l milk
  - 4 egg yolks
  - 200 g sugar
  - 2 vanilla pudding mixes
  - 10 tbsp flour
  - 1 margarine block
  - Jaffa cakes
  - ladyfingers
  - cream banana filling
steps:
  sr:
  - Umutiti žumanca sa šećerom u prahu dodati margarin keks i sok; rasporediti kao koru.
  - Skuvati fil u mleku; kad se prohladi dodati umućen margarin.
  - 'Ređati: kora od keksa, fil, jafa, fil, piškote, fil i krem od banane.'
  en:
  - Beat the yolks with the powdered sugar add the margarine biscuits and juice; press into a base.
  - Cook the filling in the milk; when cool beat in the margarine.
  - 'Layer: biscuit base, filling, Jaffa cakes, filling, ladyfingers, filling and banana cream.'
