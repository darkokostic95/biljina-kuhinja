ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 4 jaja
  - 4 kašike šećera
  - 2 kašike vode
  - 200 g čokolade za kuvanje
  - 1 margarin
  - 1/2 kg keksa
  - vruće mleko ili kafa
  en:
  - 4 eggs
  - 4 tbsp sugar
  - 2 tbsp water
  - 200 g cooking chocolate
  - 1 margarine block
  - 1/2 kg biscuits
  - hot milk or coffee
steps:
  sr:
  - Jaja šećer vodu i čokoladu mutiti na mlakoj ringli dok se ne zgusne pa prohladiti.
  - Posebno umutiti margarin i dodati prohlađenu masu.
  - Još malo mutiti na pari da se masa izjednači i ostaviti da se prohladi.
  - Keks umakati u vruće mleko ili kafu i slagati sa filom.
  en:
  - Whisk the eggs sugar water and chocolate on a warm hob until thick then cool.
  - Separately cream the margarine and add the cooled mixture.
  - Beat a little more over steam until smooth and let cool.
  - Dip the biscuits in hot milk or coffee and layer with the filling.
