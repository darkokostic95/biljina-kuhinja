---
slug: cokoladne-korpice
title:
  sr: Čokoladne korpice sa višnjom
  en: Chocolate Cups with Sour Cherry
category: bez-pecenja
tags:
- cokolada
- voce
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 350 g šećera
  - pola jogurtske čaše vode
  - 6 kašika brašna
  - pola margarina
  - 300 g čokolade
  - malo ulja
  - višnje
  - papirne korpice
  en:
  - 350 g sugar
  - half a yogurt glass water
  - 6 tbsp flour
  - half a margarine block
  - 300 g chocolate
  - a little oil
  - sour cherries
  - paper cases
steps:
  sr:
  - Šećer i vodu kuvati 5 minuta.
  - Skloniti i dodati brašno; mešati dok se ne sjedini pa dodati margarin i umutiti mikserom.
  - Rastopiti čokoladu sa malo ulja.
  - U korpice sipati čokoladu pa smesu i po jednu višnju.
  - Odozgo opet čokolada.
  en:
  - Boil the sugar and water for 5 minutes.
  - Remove from heat and stir in the flour; when smooth add the margarine and beat with a mixer.
  - Melt the chocolate with a little oil.
  - Fill the cases with chocolate then the cream and one cherry.
  - Top with more chocolate.
