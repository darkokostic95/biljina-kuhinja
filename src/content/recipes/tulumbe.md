---
slug: tulumbe
title:
  sr: Tulumbe
  en: Tulumbe (Syrup Fritters)
category: sitni-kolaci
tags: []
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Testo
    en: Dough
  sr:
  - 2 čaše vode (od 2 dl)
  - 2 kašike šećera
  - 2 kašike zejtina
  - 2 čaše brašna
  - 1 prašak za pecivo
  - 4 jaja
  - ulje za prženje
  en:
  - 2 glasses water (2 dl each)
  - 2 tbsp sugar
  - 2 tbsp oil
  - 2 glasses flour
  - 1 baking powder
  - 4 eggs
  - oil for frying
- group:
    sr: Sirup
    en: Syrup
  sr:
  - 600 g šećera
  - vode koliko da prelije šećer
  en:
  - 600 g sugar
  - enough water to cover
steps:
  sr:
  - Vodu šećer i zejtin da provri pa umešati brašno.
  - Kad se testo ohladi dodati prašak i jaja (mutiti po 2 jaja).
  - Istiskivati tulumbe i pržiti.
  - Preliti sirupom.
  en:
  - Bring the water sugar and oil to a boil then stir in the flour.
  - When cool beat in the baking powder and the eggs, two at a time.
  - Pipe the tulumbe and fry.
  - Pour over the syrup.
---
