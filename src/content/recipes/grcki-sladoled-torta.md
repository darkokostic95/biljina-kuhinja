  sr: Grčka sladoled torta
  en: Greek Ice-Cream Cake

ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 650 g šlag pene
  - 4 kesice želatina
  - 500 g šećera
  - 2 i po l mleka
  - 300 g napolitanki (Lions ili Njamb)
  - 150 g mlečne čokolade
  en:
  - 650 g whipped topping powder
  - 4 sachets gelatine
  - 500 g sugar
  - 2.5 l milk
  - 300 g wafer biscuits (Lions or Njamb)
  - 150 g milk chocolate
steps:
  sr:
  - U 1 i po l mleka staviti šećer da provri.
  - Želatin razmutiti u malo hladnog mleka, sipati u provrelo mleko i kratko kuvati.
  - Masu podeliti u 3 veće činije i mešati dok se hladi.
  - Šlag umutiti sa 9 dl mleka; kad masa počne da se zgušnjava u svaki deo dodati po 9 kašika šlaga i mutiti.
  - U I deo dodati rendanu čokoladu, II ostaje beo (može žele bombone), u III dodati mlevene napolitanke.
  - Ređati u pleh obložen folijom i sutradan prevrnuti na poslužavnik; odozgo preostali šlag.
  en:
  - Bring 1.5 l of the milk to the boil with the sugar.
  - Dissolve the gelatine in a little cold milk, add to the hot milk and cook briefly.
  - Divide into 3 large bowls and stir while it cools.
  - Whip the topping with 9 dl milk; once the mixture starts to set fold 9 tbsp of whipped cream into each bowl.
  - Add grated chocolate to the first, leave the second white (jelly sweets optional), add ground wafers to the third.
  - Layer in a foil-lined tin and turn out onto a platter the next day; top with the remaining whipped cream.
