ingredients:

    sr: Korice (10 kom)

  sr:
  - 1 jaje

  - parče margarina veličine oraha
  - 250 g brašna
  - na vrh noža sode bikarbone
  - 100 g šećera u prahu
  - otopljen margarin
  en:
  - 1 egg
  - a small cup milk
  - a walnut-sized knob of margarine
  - 250 g flour
  - a pinch of baking soda
  - 100 g powdered sugar
  - melted margarine
- group:
    sr: Fil
    en: Filling
  sr:
  - 1 l mleka
  - 3 pudinga od vanile
  - 200 g bele čokolade
  - 1 margarin
  - 300 g šlag kreme
  en:
  - 1 l milk
  - 3 vanilla pudding mixes
  - 200 g white chocolate
  - 1 margarine block
  - 300 g whipped topping
steps:
  sr:
  - Zamesiti testo i podeliti na 10 kuglica.
  - Svaku tanko razvući i ispeći na poleđini pleha.
  - Skuvati pudinge u mleku pa dodati belu čokoladu i margarin.
  - Filovati korice a odozgo šlag.
  en:
  - Knead the dough and divide into 10 balls.
  - Roll each very thin and bake on the back of a baking tray.
  - Cook the puddings in the milk then add the white chocolate and margarine.
  - Fill the layers and top with whipped cream.
