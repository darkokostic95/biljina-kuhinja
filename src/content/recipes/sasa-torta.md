  - 1/2-1 l mleka

  en:
  - 1/2-1 l milk
  - 2 vanilla pudding mixes
  - 15 tbsp sugar
  - 5 tbsp cornstarch
  - 5 egg yolks
  - 1-2 margarine blocks
  - 200 g powdered sugar
  - 100 g chocolate
steps:
  sr:
  - Ispeći dve kore od celih jaja i treću od belanaca.
  - Skuvati puding u mleku sa šećerom, gustinom i žumancima.
  - Kad se ohladi dodati margarin umućen sa šećerom u prahu i čokoladu (ostatak od rendanja).
  - 'Filovati: I kora, fil, III kora, fil, II kora, fil, odozgo šlag.'
  en:
  - Bake two sponges from whole eggs and the third from egg whites.
  - Cook the pudding in the milk with the sugar, cornstarch and yolks.
  - When cool add the margarine creamed with powdered sugar and the chocolate (left over from grating).
  - 'Layer: sponge I, filling, sponge III, filling, sponge II, filling, whipped cream on top.'
