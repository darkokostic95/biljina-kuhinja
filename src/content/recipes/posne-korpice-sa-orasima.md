ingredients:

  sr:
  - 200 g čokolade
  - pola posnog margarina
  - 100 g šećera
  - šoljica vode
  - 100 g oraha
  - 100 g mlevenog keksa
  - papirne korpice
  en:
  - 200 g chocolate
  - half a vegan margarine block
  - 100 g sugar
  - a small cup water
  - 100 g walnuts
  - 100 g ground biscuits
  - paper cases
steps:
  sr:
  - Otopiti čokoladu i margarin i staviti po kašičicu u svaku korpicu.
  - Šećer i vodu da provri pa dodati orahe i keks.
  - Napuniti korpice i zaliti čokoladom.
  en:
  - Melt the chocolate and margarine and put a teaspoon in each case.
  - Boil the sugar and water then add the walnuts and biscuits.
  - Fill the cases and top with chocolate.
