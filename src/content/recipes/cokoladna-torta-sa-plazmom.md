---
slug: cokoladna-torta-sa-plazmom
title:
  sr: Čokoladna torta sa plazmom
  en: Chocolate Plazma Cake
category: torte
tags:
- cokolada
- plazma
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Masa (za kore i fil)
    en: Mixture (for layers and filling)
  sr:
  - 15 belanaca
  - 15 kašika šećera
  - 15 žumanaca
  - 250 g šećera u prahu
  - 2 margarina
  - 300 g mlevene plazme
  - 400 g otopljene čokolade
  en:
  - 15 egg whites
  - 15 tbsp sugar
  - 15 egg yolks
  - 250 g powdered sugar
  - 2 margarine blocks
  - 300 g ground Plazma biscuits
  - 400 g melted chocolate
- group:
    sr: Šlag
    en: Cream
  sr:
  - 5-6 kesica šlaga
  en:
  - 5-6 sachets whipped topping
steps:
  sr:
  - Umutiti belanca sa šećerom.
  - Posebno umutiti žumanca sa šećerom u prahu pa postepeno dodati margarine.
  - Sjediniti pa dodati plazmu i mlaku čokoladu i na kraju belanca.
  - Od dela mase ispeći 3 tanke kore (oko 10 minuta) u plehu obloženom papirom.
  - 'Filovati: kora, 1/3 preostale mase, 2 šlaga, kora, fil, šlag... odozgo 3-4 šlaga.'
  en:
  - Whisk the whites with the sugar.
  - Separately beat the yolks with the powdered sugar then gradually add the margarine.
  - Combine then add the Plazma and lukewarm chocolate and finally the whites.
  - Bake 3 thin layers from part of the mixture (about 10 minutes) on lined trays.
  - 'Layer: sponge, 1/3 of the remaining mixture, whipped cream, sponge, filling, cream... finish with 3-4 sachets of whipped cream.'
