---
slug: padobranci
title:
  sr: Padobranci
  en: Walnut Meringue Parachutes
category: sitni-kolaci
tags:
- orasi
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 5 belanaca
  - 250 g šećera
  - 300 g mlevenih oraha
  - 5 žumanaca
  - 5 kašika šećera
  - šećer u prahu
  en:
  - 5 egg whites
  - 250 g sugar
  - 300 g ground walnuts
  - 5 egg yolks
  - 5 tbsp sugar
  - powdered sugar
steps:
  sr:
  - Umutiti belanca sa šećerom pa dodati orahe.
  - Špricem stavljati u pleh kao puslice i peći (sušiti).
  - Žumanca i šećer skuvati na pari i filovati.
  - Iseći na kocke i valjati u šećer u prahu.
  en:
  - Whisk the whites with the sugar then fold in the walnuts.
  - Pipe onto a tray like meringue kisses and bake (dry).
  - Cook the yolks and sugar over steam and use as filling.
  - Cut into pieces and roll in powdered sugar.
