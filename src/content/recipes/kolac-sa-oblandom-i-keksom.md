  - kuvani fil
  - 1 margarin

  - cooked custard
  - 1 margarine block

  - 2 wafer sheets
  - 200 g biscuits
  - cold milk
  - 150 g chocolate for glaze
steps:
  sr:
  - Ispeći koricu od belanaca.
  - Kad se fil dobro skuva dodati margarin umućen sa šećerom u prahu.
  - 'Ređati: oblanda, fil, keks potopljen u hladno mleko, fil, pečena kora, fil, oblanda okrenuta naopako.'
  - Odozgo glazura od 150 g čokolade.
  en:
  - Bake the meringue sponge.
  - When the custard is cooked add the margarine creamed with powdered sugar.
  - 'Layer: wafer, filling, biscuits dipped in cold milk, filling, baked sponge, filling, wafer upside down.'
  - Glaze with 150 g chocolate.
