ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 1,5 l mleka
  - 15 kašika šećera
  - 4 želatina
  - 200 g vode
  - šlag kreme
  - 200 g Lions napolitanki
  - banane
  - rendana čokolada
  en:
  - 1.5 l milk
  - 15 tbsp sugar
  - 4 sachets gelatine
  - 200 g water
  - whipped topping
  - 200 g Lions wafer biscuits
  - bananas
  - grated chocolate
steps:
  sr:
  - Mleko skuvati sa šećerom; kad proključa sipati želatin razmućen u vodi.
  - Prohladiti na sobnoj temperaturi pa podeliti na 3 dela po 1/2 l i staviti u zamrzivač 30 minuta.
  - I deo umutiti sa 1/4 šlag kreme i napolitankama i sipati u pleh obložen kesom.
  - II deo umutiti sa šlagom i dodati banane.
  - III deo umutiti sa šlagom i dodati rendanu čokoladu.
  - Odozgo 2 šlag kreme.
  en:
  - Boil the milk with the sugar; when it boils add the gelatine dissolved in water.
  - Cool at room temperature then divide into 3 × 1/2 l and freeze for 30 minutes.
  - Beat part I with 1/4 of the topping and the crushed wafers and pour into a bag-lined tin.
  - Beat part II with topping and add bananas.
  - Beat part III with topping and add grated chocolate.
  - Finish with 2 sachets of whipped cream.
