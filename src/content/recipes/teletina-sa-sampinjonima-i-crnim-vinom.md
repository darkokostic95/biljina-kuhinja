slug: teletina-sa-sampinjonima-i-crnim-vinom
title:
  sr: Teletina sa šampinjonima i crnim vinom
  en: Veal with Mushrooms and Red Wine
category: glavna-jela
tags:
- meso
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 300 g teletine
  - 200 g crnog luka
  - 200 g šampinjona
  - 200 g kiselih krastavčića
  - 1 čaša crnog vina
  - 2 kašike senfa
  - 2 kašike kisele pavlake
  - malo supe
  - kuvan pirinač
  en:
  - 300 g veal
  - 200 g onion
  - 200 g mushrooms
  - 200 g gherkins
  - 1 glass red wine
  - 2 tbsp mustard
  - 2 tbsp sour cream
  - a little stock
  - cooked rice
steps:
  sr:
  - Ispržiti meso pa ga izvaditi.
  - U masnoći propržiti luk i šampinjone.
  - Dodati krastavčiće vino senf pavlaku i malo supe.
  - Vratiti meso i služiti uz pirinač.
  en:
  - Fry the veal then remove it.
  - Sauté the onion and mushrooms in the fat.
  - Add the gherkins wine mustard sour cream and a little stock.
  - Return the meat and serve with rice.
