  sr: Posni rolat od ratluka
  en: Lenten Turkish Delight Walnut Roll
category: bez-pecenja
tags:
- posno

ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 200 g ratluka
  - 200 g šećera u prahu
  - kora od limuna
  - 200 g oraha
  - malo vode
  en:
  - 200 g Turkish delight
  - 200 g powdered sugar
  - lemon zest
  - 200 g walnuts
  - a little water
steps:
  sr:
  - Ratluk šećer u prahu i koru limuna dobro umesiti i razvući koru.
  - Orahe ukuvati sa malo vode.
  - Premazati koru filom i uviti u rolat.
  en:
  - Knead the Turkish delight powdered sugar and lemon zest well and roll out.
  - Cook the walnuts with a little water and sugar syrup.
  - Spread over and roll up.
