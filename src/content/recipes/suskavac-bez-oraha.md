title:
  sr: Šuškavac bez oraha
  en: Nut-Free Meringue Cake
category: torte
tags:

ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Kora
    en: Meringue
  sr:
  - 8 belanaca
  - 550 g šećera
  - 1 kašika sirćeta
  en:
  - 8 egg whites
  - 550 g sugar
  - 1 tbsp vinegar
- group:
    sr: Fil
    en: Filling
  sr:
  - 750 g mleka
  - 8 žumanaca
  - 1 puding od vanile
  - 3 kašike gustina
  - 8 kašika šećera
  - 1 i po margarin
  - 200 g šećera u prahu
  - 300 g mlevene plazme
  - šlag
  - banane
  en:
  - 750 g milk
  - 8 egg yolks
  - 1 vanilla pudding mix
  - 3 tbsp cornstarch
  - 8 tbsp sugar
  - 1.5 margarine blocks
  - 200 g powdered sugar
  - 300 g ground Plazma biscuits
  - whipped cream
  - bananas
steps:
  sr:
  - Koru napraviti i sušiti kao za običan šuškavac.
  - Skuvati fil od mleka, žumanaca, pudinga, gustina i šećera.
  - Margarin umutiti sa šećerom u prahu i postepeno dodavati prohlađen fil pa umešati plazmu.
  - 'Filovati: I kora, fil, šlag umućen sa kiselom vodom, banane, II kora, fil, šlag.'
  en:
  - Make and dry the meringue as for the classic Šuškavac.
  - Cook the filling from the milk, yolks, pudding, cornstarch and sugar.
  - Cream the margarine with the powdered sugar and gradually add the cooled filling then fold in the Plazma.
  - 'Layer: meringue I, filling, cream whipped with sparkling water, bananas, meringue II, filling, cream.'
