slug: kolac-sa-cajnim-kolutima
title:
  sr: Kolač sa čajnim kolutićima
  en: Tea Biscuit Wafer Slice
category: bez-pecenja
tags:
- bez-pecenja
- keks
- orasi
- oblanda
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 400 g šećera
  - 2 dcl vode
  - 200 g mlevenog keksa
  - 200 g mlevenih oraha
  - 200 g margarina
  - 1 veći list oblande
  - džem
  - 500 g čajnih kolutića
  - sok ili gazirani sok
  - glazura od bele ili crne čokolade
  en:
  - 400 g sugar
  - 2 dl water
  - 200 g ground biscuits
  - 200 g ground walnuts
  - 200 g margarine
  - 1 large wafer sheet
  - jam
  - 500 g ring tea biscuits
  - juice or fizzy drink
  - white or dark chocolate glaze
steps:
  sr:
  - Ukuvati šećer sa vodom pa dodati keks, orahe i margarin.
  - List oblande premazati džemom.
  - Poređati čajne kolutiće prethodno provučene kroz sok.
  - Preliti filom tako da upadne u rupice i lepo poravnati.
  - Odozgo glazura od čokolade.
  en:
  - Boil the sugar with the water into a syrup then add the biscuits, walnuts and margarine.
  - Spread the wafer sheet with jam.
  - Arrange the tea rings dipped in juice on top.
  - Pour the filling over so it fills the holes and smooth it.
  - Finish with a chocolate glaze.
notes:
  sr: U svesci piše da se može dodati još pola mere.
  en: The notebook suggests making 1.5× the amount.
