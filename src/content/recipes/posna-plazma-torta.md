ingredients:

  sr:
  - 300 g šećera
  - 400 ml kisele (ili obične) vode
  - 5 kašika ulja
  - 5 kašika marmelade
  - 1 prašak za pecivo
  - 2 kašike kakaa
  - 100 g mlevenih oraha
  - 100 g mlevene posne plazme
  en:
  - 300 g sugar
  - 400 ml sparkling (or still) water
  - 5 tbsp oil
  - 5 tbsp jam
  - 1 baking powder
  - 2 tbsp cocoa
  - 100 g ground walnuts
  - 100 g ground vegan Plazma biscuits
- group:
    sr: Fil
    en: Filling
  sr:
  - 800 ml vode
  - 150 g šećera
  - 3 pudinga od čokolade
  - 3 kašike šećera
  - 200 g čokolade za kuvanje
  - parče posnog margarina
  - 1 posni margarin
  - 100 g šećera u prahu
  - 200 g plazme
  - 100 g mlevenih oraha
  en:
  - 800 ml water
  - 150 g sugar
  - 3 chocolate pudding mixes
  - 3 tbsp sugar
  - 200 g cooking chocolate
  - a knob of vegan margarine
  - 1 vegan margarine block
  - 100 g powdered sugar
  - 200 g Plazma biscuits
  - 100 g ground walnuts
- group:
    sr: Šlag
    en: Cream
  sr:
  - 300 g posnog šlaga
  - 500 ml kisele vode
  - 2 kašike kakaa
  en:
  - 300 g vegan whipped topping
  - 500 ml sparkling water
  - 2 tbsp cocoa
steps:
  sr:
  - Ispeći koru u većem plehu i podeliti je na 2 dela.
  - Pudinge razmutiti sa 3 kašike šećera i delom vode pa skuvati u ostatku vode sa šećerom i čokoladom; na kraju dodati parče margarina.
  - Margarin umutiti sa šećerom u prahu i postepeno dodati prohlađen puding pa plazmu i orahe.
  - Umutiti šlag sa kiselom vodom i kakaom; 3 kašike umešati u fil.
  - 'Ređati: kora, fil, šlag, kora, fil, šlag.'
  en:
  - Bake the base in a large tin and split into 2 layers.
  - Mix the puddings with 3 tbsp sugar and some of the water then cook in the rest of the water with sugar and chocolate; finish with a knob of margarine.
  - Cream the margarine with powdered sugar and gradually add the cooled pudding then the Plazma and walnuts.
  - Whip the topping with sparkling water and cocoa; fold 3 tbsp into the filling.
  - 'Layer: base, filling, cream, base, filling, cream.'
