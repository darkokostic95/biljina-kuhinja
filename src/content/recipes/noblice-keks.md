  en:
  - 1 margarine block
  - 100 g powdered sugar
  - 1 egg

  - 430 g flour
  - half a tsp baking powder
  - 3 tsp cocoa

steps:
  sr:
  - Umutiti margarin sa šećerom u prahu jajima i vanilom pa dodati brašno i prašak.
  - Podeliti na 2 dela — u jedan dodati kakao.
  - Oba dela uviti u kese i staviti u frižider na sat vremena.
  - Rastanjiti i vaditi modlom; peći na 180 °C kao vanilice da se ne prepeku.
  - Ohlađene spajati eurokremom.
  en:
  - Cream the margarine with the powdered sugar eggs and vanilla then add the flour and baking powder.
  - Divide in 2 — add cocoa to one half.
  - Wrap both in bags and chill for an hour.
  - Roll out and cut shapes; bake at 180 °C like vanilice without over-baking.
  - Sandwich the cooled cookies with chocolate spread.
