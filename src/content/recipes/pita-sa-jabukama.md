ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci

  sr:
  - 15-20 kašika šećera
  - 15-20 kašika griza
  - 3-4 kašike prezle
  - 1-2 vanilin šećera
  - margarin
  - zejtin
  - kore za pitu
  - dosta jabuka
  en:
  - 15–20 tbsp sugar
  - 15–20 tbsp semolina
  - 3–4 tbsp breadcrumbs
  - 1–2 vanilla sugar sachets
  - margarine
  - oil
  - filo sheets
  - plenty of apples
steps:
  sr:
  - Šećer griz prezle i vanilu pomešati na suvo.
  - Margarin istopiti i dodati zejtin.
  - Po 4 kore u jednom rolatu — svaku premazati margarinom i posuti smesom; na četvrtu dodati dosta jabuka.
  - Uviti u rolat i ponavljati dok ima kora.
  - Peći na 200 °C dok ne porumeni pa pokriti folijom i još malo peći.
  en:
  - Mix the sugar semolina breadcrumbs and vanilla dry.
  - Melt the margarine and add the oil.
  - Use 4 sheets per roll — brush each with margarine and sprinkle with the mixture; add lots of apple on the fourth.
  - Roll up and repeat with the remaining sheets.
  - Bake at 200 °C until golden then cover with foil and bake a little longer.
notes:
  sr: Bilja je označila ovu pitu kao svoju.
  en: Bilja marked this pie as her own.
