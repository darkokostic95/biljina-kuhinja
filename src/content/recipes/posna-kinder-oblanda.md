ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 800 g šećera
  - 2 dcl vode
  - 1 posni margarin
  - 600 g posnog mleka u prahu
  - crna čokolada
  - bela čokolada
  - listovi oblande
  en:
  - 800 g sugar
  - 2 dl water
  - 1 vegan margarine block
  - 600 g vegan milk powder
  - dark chocolate
  - white chocolate
  - wafer sheets
steps:
  sr:
  - Ukuvati šećer sa vodom kao za slatko.
  - Posebno umutiti margarin i dodati mleko u prahu.
  - Vreo sirup sipati u margarin i dobro izmiksirati; prohladiti.
  - Podeliti na 2 dela — u jedan otopljena crna a u drugi bela čokolada.
  - Filovati crni fil, oblanda, beli fil i pokriti listom oblande.
  en:
  - Boil the sugar and water to a syrup.
  - Separately beat the margarine and add the milk powder.
  - Pour the hot syrup into the margarine and beat well; cool slightly.
  - Divide in 2 — melted dark chocolate in one and white in the other.
  - Layer dark filling, wafer, white filling and cover with a wafer sheet.
notes:
  sr: Između dva lista može i pola mere sa seckanim voćem kikirikijem ili lešnikom.
  en: You can make half the amount between two sheets with chopped fruit peanuts or hazelnuts.
