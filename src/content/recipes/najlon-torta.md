  en:
  - Whisk the whites with powdered sugar and dry — make 2 layers.
  - Cook the filling in the milk over steam.
  - Cream the margarine with powdered sugar combine with the cooled filling then fold in whipped cream.
  - Divide in 2 — cream bananas in one Plazma and chocolate in the other.
  - 'Layer: meringue, chocolate filling, meringue, banana filling and whipped cream.'
