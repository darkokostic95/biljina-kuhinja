ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Krem
    en: Cream
  sr:
  - 500 g slatke pavlake
  - 250 g šećera u prahu
  - 600 g kisele pavlake
  - 2 g neskafe (1 kašičica) razmućene u malo vode
  - 8 g ruma (2 kašičice)
  en:
  - 500 g whipping cream
  - 250 g powdered sugar
  - 600 g sour cream
  - 2 g instant coffee (1 tsp) dissolved in a little water
  - 8 g rum (2 tsp)
- group:
    sr: Za natapanje
    en: For dipping
  sr:
  - 8 g neskafe (4 kašičice)
  - 250 g vode
  - 15 g ruma (4 kašičice)
  en:
  - 8 g instant coffee (4 tsp)
  - 250 g water
  - 15 g rum (4 tsp)
- group:
    sr: Ostalo
    en: Other
  sr:
  - 315 g piškota (40-42 komada / 3 mala pakovanja)
  - kakao za posipanje
  en:
  - 315 g ladyfingers (40–42 pieces / 3 small packs)
  - cocoa for dusting
steps:
  sr:
  - Neskafu razmutiti u vodi i dodati rum — u ovo se umaču piškote. Umesto neskafe možete skuvati 270 g jake kafe.
  - Slatku pavlaku blago umutiti pa dodati šećer u prahu i mutiti dok ne postane čvrsta.
  - Kratko umešati kiselu pavlaku.
  - Dodati kafu za krem i rum i kratko mutiti dok se ne sjedini.
  - Piškote brzo umakati u kafu i ređati red u posudu.
  - Preko rasporediti polovinu krema pa ponoviti red piškota i ostatak krema i poravnati.
  - Ostaviti u frižideru nekoliko sati (najbolje preko noći).
  - Pre služenja posuti kakaom kroz cediljku.
  en:
  - Dissolve the instant coffee in the water and add the rum — this is for dipping the ladyfingers. Instead of instant you can brew 270 g strong coffee.
  - Lightly whip the cream then add the powdered sugar and whip until firm.
  - Briefly fold in the sour cream.
  - Add the coffee for the cream and the rum and mix briefly until combined.
  - Dip the ladyfingers quickly in the coffee and lay one layer in the dish.
  - Spread half the cream over then repeat a layer of ladyfingers and the rest of the cream and smooth.
  - Chill for several hours (overnight is best).
  - Dust with cocoa through a sieve before serving.
notes:
