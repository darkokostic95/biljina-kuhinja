title:
  sr: Sirup za iskašljavanje sa ratlukom
  en: Cough Syrup with Turkish Delight
category: napici
tags: []
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - pola litra vode
  - 4 lista rezetle
  - 2 ratluka
  - 2 negro bombone
  en:
  - half a litre water
  - 4 leaves of rezetla (herb)
  - 2 pieces Turkish delight
  - 2 Negro (menthol) sweets
steps:
  sr:
  - Sve kuvati dok se ne dobije sirup koliko za 1 šoljicu kafe.
  - Piti ujutru i uveče po 1 kafenu kašičicu.
  en:
  - Simmer until reduced to about one coffee cup of syrup.
  - Take 1 coffee spoon morning and evening.
notes:
  sr: Narodni recept — nije zamena za lekara.
  en: A folk remedy — not a substitute for a doctor.
