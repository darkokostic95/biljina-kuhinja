  sr:
  - 300 g sojinih komadića
  - so
  - 2 lovorova lista
  - biber
  - 3 stabljike praziluka
  - 2 šargarepe
  - 2-3 suve crvene paprike
  - 1/2 glavice crnog luka
  - 700 g pečuraka
  - vegeta

  en:
  - 300 g soy chunks
  - salt
  - 2 bay leaves
  - pepper
  - 3 leeks
  - 2 carrots
  - 2-3 dried red peppers
  - 1/2 onion
  - 700 g mushrooms
  - Vegeta seasoning
  - crushed red pepper
steps:
  sr:
  - Soju potopiti u vrelu vodu na pola sata.
  - Dodati so, lovor i biber i kuvati pola sata.
  - Posebno na tihoj vatri dinstati praziluk, šargarepu na kockice i suve paprike.
  - Dodati proceđenu soju i još malo pržiti.
  - Posebno ispržiti crni luk i pečurke.
  - Sve sjediniti i na kraju dodati vegetu i tucanu papriku.
  en:
  - Soak the soy in boiling water for half an hour.
  - Add salt, bay leaves and pepper and cook for half an hour.
  - Separately sauté the leeks, diced carrots and dried peppers over low heat.
  - Add the drained soy and fry a little more.
  - Separately fry the onion and mushrooms.
  - Combine everything and finish with Vegeta and crushed red pepper.
