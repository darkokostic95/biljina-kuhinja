---
slug: rolatici-sa-sirom
title:
  sr: Rolatići sa sirom
  en: Cheese Pinwheel Rolls
category: peciva
tags:
- kvasac
- sir
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - parče kvasca
  - 250 g mleka
  - 1/2 šolje zejtina
  - 2 jajeta
  - malo soli
  - brašno po potrebi
  - 1/2 margarina
  - sir
  - jaja za fil
  en:
  - a piece of fresh yeast
  - 250 g milk
  - 1/2 cup oil
  - 2 eggs
  - a little salt
  - flour as needed
  - 1/2 margarine block
  - white cheese
  - eggs for the filling
steps:
  sr:
  - Kvasac rastopiti u mleku i sačekati da nadođe.
  - Dodati zejtin, jaja i so i umesiti meko testo.
  - Razviti koru, narendati margarin i uviti u rolat.
  - Ponovo razviti, premazati sirom i jajima i uviti.
  - Seći na kriške, ređati u pleh, ostaviti da nadođe i ispeći.
  en:
  - Dissolve the yeast in the milk and let it froth.
  - Add the oil, eggs and salt and knead a soft dough.
  - Roll out, grate the margarine over and roll up.
  - Roll out again, spread with cheese and egg and roll up.
  - Slice, arrange in a tin, let rise and bake.
---
