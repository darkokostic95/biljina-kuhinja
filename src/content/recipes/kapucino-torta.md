  en:
  - 7 egg whites
  - 1 egg yolk
  - 9 tbsp sugar
  - 2 tbsp flour
  - 2 tbsp cocoa
  - 10 tbsp ground walnuts
  - 3 tbsp oil
- group:
    sr: Za kvašenje
    en: For soaking
  sr:
  - 1-1 i po l čaja
  en:
  - 1-1.5 l tea
- group:
    sr: Fil
    en: Filling
  sr:
  - 18 žumanaca
  - 20 kašika šećera
  - 1 i po l mleka
  - 3 pudinga od vanile
  - 9 kašika gustina
  - 5 kesica kapućina od vanile
  - 100 ml vode
  - 3 margarina
  - 250 g šećera u prahu
  - 400 g šlag pene
  en:
  - 18 egg yolks
  - 20 tbsp sugar
  - 1.5 l milk
  - 3 vanilla pudding mixes
  - 9 tbsp cornstarch
  - 5 sachets vanilla cappuccino
  - 100 ml water
  - 3 margarine blocks
  - 250 g powdered sugar
  - 400 g whipped topping
steps:
  sr:
  - Ispeći 3 kore i pokvasiti ih čajem.
  - Na pari skuvati žumanca, šećer, mleko, pudinge i gustin.
  - Kad je masa skoro kuvana dodati kapućino razmućen u vodi i još malo kuvati pa ohladiti.
  - Umutiti margarine sa šećerom u prahu i sjediniti sa filom.
  - 'Filovati: kora, fil, šlag — ponoviti.'
  en:
  - Bake 3 sponge layers and soak them with tea.
  - Cook the yolks, sugar, milk, puddings and cornstarch over steam.
  - When nearly done add the cappuccino dissolved in water, cook a little longer then cool.
  - Cream the margarine with the powdered sugar and combine with the filling.
  - 'Layer: sponge, filling, whipped cream — repeat.'
