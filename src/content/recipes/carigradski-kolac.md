---
slug: carigradski-kolac
title:
  sr: Carigradski kolač
  en: Constantinople Wafer Cake
category: kolaci
tags:
- oblanda
- orasi
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 9 belanaca
  - 18 kašika šećera
  - 3 lista oblande
  - seckani orasi
  - 9 žumanaca
  - 11 kašika šećera
  - 1 margarin
  en:
  - 9 egg whites
  - 18 tbsp sugar
  - 3 wafer sheets
  - chopped walnuts
  - 9 egg yolks
  - 11 tbsp sugar
  - 1 margarine block
steps:
  sr:
  - Belanca umutiti sa šećerom i premazati 3 lista oblande; posuti seckanim orasima.
  - Peći na 200 °C.
  - Žumanca i šećer skuvati na pari; u hladan fil dodati margarin.
  - Filovati preko 2 lista pa staviti treći i pritisnuti da listovi lepo nalegnu.
  en:
  - Whisk the whites with the sugar and spread over the 3 wafer sheets; sprinkle with walnuts.
  - Bake at 200 °C.
  - Cook the yolks and sugar over steam; beat the margarine into the cold filling.
  - Spread over 2 sheets then add the third and weigh down so they settle.
