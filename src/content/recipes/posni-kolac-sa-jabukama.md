slug: posni-kolac-sa-jabukama
title:
  sr: Posni kolač sa jabukama
  en: Lenten Apple Cake
category: kolaci
tags:
- posno
- jabuke
- brzo
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 5 jabuka
  - 4 šoljice šećera
  - 2 šoljice zejtina
  - 1 kesica sode bikarbone
  - 2 jogurtske čaše brašna
  - glazura po želji
  en:
  - 5 apples
  - 4 small cups sugar
  - 2 small cups oil
  - 1 sachet baking soda
  - 2 yogurt glasses flour
  - glaze of choice
steps:
  sr:
  - Izrendati jabuke i dodati šećer, zejtin i sodu.
  - Ostaviti da stoji 45 minuta.
  - Dodati brašno i ispeći (peče se brzo).
  - Odozgo glazura po želji.
  en:
  - Grate the apples and add the sugar, oil and baking soda.
  - Leave for 45 minutes.
  - Add the flour and bake (it bakes quickly).
  - Glaze as you like.
