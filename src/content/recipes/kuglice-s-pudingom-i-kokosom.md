  - 1/2 l milk
  - 2 pudding mixes
  - 1 margarine block
  - 250 g powdered sugar
  - 200 g coconut

  - Umutiti margarin sa šećerom u prahu i dodati puding i kokos.
  - Praviti kuglice.
  en:
  - Cook the puddings in the milk and cool.
  - Cream the margarine with powdered sugar and add the pudding and coconut.
  - Shape balls.
