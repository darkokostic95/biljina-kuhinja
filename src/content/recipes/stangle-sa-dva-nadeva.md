  sr: Štangle sa dva nadeva
  en: Two-Layer Walnut Bars

sources:

ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Kora
    en: Base
  sr:
  - 3 belanca
  - 100 g šećera
  - 100 g margarina
  - 100 g mlevenih oraha
  - 100 g brašna
  en:
  - 3 egg whites
  - 100 g sugar
  - 100 g margarine
  - 100 g ground walnuts
  - 100 g flour
- group:
    sr: I nadev
    en: Layer I
  sr:
  - 3 žumanca
  - 100 g šećera
  en:
  - 3 egg yolks
  - 100 g sugar
- group:
    sr: II nadev
    en: Layer II
  sr:
  - 1 šoljica mleka
  - 100 g šećera
  - 40 g margarina
  - 2 štanglice čokolade ili kakao
  - 100 g oraha
  en:
  - 1 small cup milk
  - 100 g sugar
  - 40 g margarine
  - 2 small bars chocolate or cocoa
  - 100 g walnuts
steps:
  sr:
  - Umutiti sastojke za koru i peći bez praška.
  - Na pola pečenja premazati umućenim žumancima sa šećerom i pustiti na 150 °C da se osuši.
  - Skuvati drugi nadev i premazati kolač.
  - Seći na štangle.
  en:
  - Mix the base and bake without baking powder.
  - Halfway through spread with yolks beaten with sugar and dry at 150 °C.
  - Cook the second layer and spread over.
  - Cut into bars.
