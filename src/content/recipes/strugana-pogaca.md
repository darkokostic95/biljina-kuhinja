---
slug: strugana-pogaca
title:
  sr: Strugana pogača sa pecivom
  en: Scraped Flatbread
category: peciva
tags:
- brzo
- posno
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 1 kg brašna
  - 2 praška za pecivo
  - 1 šoljica zejtina
  - hladna voda s česme
  en:
  - 1 kg flour
  - 2 baking powders
  - 1 small cup oil
  - cold tap water
steps:
  sr:
  - Umesiti testo i staviti u dobro podmazanu tepsiju.
  - Odozgo podmazati.
  - Uključiti rernu na najjače i peći.
  - Pečenu pogaču izrendati (ostrugati) odozgo.
  en:
  - Knead and put into a well-greased tin.
  - Brush the top with oil.
  - Bake at the highest oven setting.
  - Scrape the top of the baked bread.
