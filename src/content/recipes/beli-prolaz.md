---
slug: beli-prolaz
title:
  sr: Beli trougao
  en: White Triangle Slice
category: bez-pecenja
tags:
- bez-pecenja
- kokos
- keks
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 250 g šećera
  - 1 dl vode
  - 100 g mlevenog keksa
  - 100 g margarina
  - 100 g kokosovog brašna
  - 100 g mleka u prahu
  - 100 g iseckanih žele bombona
  en:
  - 250 g sugar
  - 1 dl water
  - 100 g ground biscuits
  - 100 g margarine
  - 100 g desiccated coconut
  - 100 g milk powder
  - 100 g chopped jelly sweets
steps:
  sr:
  - Ukuvati šećer i vodu.
  - Dodati keks, margarin, kokos, mleko u prahu i žele bombone.
  - Dobro sjediniti i staviti u kalup.
  en:
  - Boil the sugar and water into a syrup.
  - Add the biscuits, margarine, coconut, milk powder and jelly sweets.
  - Mix well and press into a mould.
