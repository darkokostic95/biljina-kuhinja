ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 200-250 pečenih paprika (očišćenih i samlevenih)
  - 1 l zejtina
  - 1 šoljica šećera
  - 1 šoljica soli (po ukusu)
  - 1 šoljica alkoholnog sirćeta
  - 1 konzervans
  en:
  - 200–250 roasted peppers (peeled and minced)
  - 1 l oil
  - 1 small cup sugar
  - 1 small cup salt (to taste)
  - 1 small cup spirit vinegar
  - 1 sachet preservative
steps:
  sr:
  - Zejtin, šećer, so i sirće staviti u šerpu da provri.
  - Dodati samlevenu papriku i kuvati 1 do 1 i po sat uz mešanje.
  - Skloniti sa vatre i dodati konzervans.
  - Sipati u zagrejane tegle.
  en:
  - Bring the oil, sugar, salt and vinegar to a boil in a large pot.
  - Add the minced peppers and cook 1 to 1.5 hours stirring.
  - Take off the heat and add the preservative.
  - Fill into warm jars.
notes:
  sr: 'Druga verzija (IMG_5632): paprika se samelje sirova, prži 30-60 minuta na malo zejtina, pa se popari sa pola litra zejtina, kašičica esencije, malo salicila i so.'
  en: 'Another version (IMG_5632): the peppers are minced raw, fried 30–60 minutes in a little oil, then half a litre of oil, a teaspoon of vinegar essence, a little salicylic preservative and salt are added.'
