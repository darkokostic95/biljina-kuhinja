  - 200 g ground walnuts
  - 200 g powdered sugar
  - 1 egg white
  - 3 tbsp rum
  - 3 small bars melted chocolate
- group:

  - 1 žumance
  - 2 vanilin šećera

  - 1 egg yolk
  - 2 vanilla sugar sachets
  - 125 g butter
  - ground walnuts or grated chocolate for rolling
steps:
  sr:
  - Sve sastojke za kuglice dobro izmešati.
  - Umutiti žumance, vanilu i maslac za fil.
  - Praviti kuglice sa filom u sredini.
  - Valjati u mlevene orahe ili rendanu čokoladu.
  en:
  - Mix the ball ingredients well.
  - Beat the yolk, vanilla and butter for the filling.
  - Shape balls with a little filling inside.
  - Roll in ground walnuts or grated chocolate.
