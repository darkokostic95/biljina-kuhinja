  - 8 eggs
  - 8 tbsp sugar
  - 1 bag ground walnuts
  - 4 tbsp flour
  - 1 vanilla sugar sachet
- group:
    sr: Fil
    en: Filling

  - 1 veće pakovanje plazma keksa

  - 1 large pack Plazma biscuits
  - orange juice
  - 1 margarine block
  - 250 g powdered sugar
  - ground walnuts
  - whipped cream
steps:
  sr:
  - Umutiti jaja sa šećerom dodati orahe brašno i vanilu i ispeći.
  - Plazmu prelivati sokom od narandže.
  - Umutiti margarin sa šećerom u prahu i dodati orahe.
  - Slagati koru plazmu i fil; odozgo šlag.
  en:
  - Whisk the eggs with the sugar add the walnuts flour and vanilla and bake.
  - Soak the Plazma with orange juice.
  - Cream the margarine with powdered sugar and add walnuts.
  - Layer sponge Plazma and filling; top with whipped cream.
