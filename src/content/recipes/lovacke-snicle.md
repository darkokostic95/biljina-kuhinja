ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Za 2 osobe

  - šnicle

  - vegeta

  en:
  - cutlets
  - 1 tbsp flour
  - 1–2 tbsp mustard
  - mushrooms (optional)
  - 2 glasses water
  - Vegeta seasoning
  - oil
steps:
  sr:
  - Ispržiti šnicle sa obe strane da porumene pa ih izvaditi.
  - U masnoću dodati brašno senf šampinjone i čašu vode.
  - Poređati šnicle u sos dodati vegetu i još čašu vode i krčkati na tihoj vatri.
  en:
  - Fry the cutlets on both sides until golden then remove.
  - Add the flour mustard mushrooms and a glass of water to the fat.
  - Return the cutlets add Vegeta and another glass of water and simmer gently.
