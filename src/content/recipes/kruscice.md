---
slug: kruscice
title:
  sr: Kruščice
  en: Little Pear Cookies
category: sitni-kolaci
tags:
- praznicno
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Testo
    en: Dough
  sr:
  - 300 g masti
  - 400 g šećera
  - 4 jaja
  - 1 kašika brašna + brašno po potrebi
  - 1 prašak za pecivo
  en:
  - 300 g lard
  - 400 g sugar
  - 4 eggs
  - 1 tbsp flour + flour as needed
  - 1 baking powder
- group:
    sr: Fil i boja
    en: Filling and colour
  sr:
  - 2 kašike džema
  - malo vrelog mleka
  - malo šećera
  - 1 šoljica mleka
  - ekstrakt jagode ili maline
  - ekstrakt kruške
  - kristal šećer
  en:
  - 2 tbsp jam
  - a little hot milk
  - a little sugar
  - 1 small cup milk
  - strawberry or raspberry extract
  - pear extract
  - granulated sugar
steps:
  sr:
  - Umutiti mast, šećer i jaja pa dodati kašiku brašna i prašak; ostaviti 1 sat.
  - Dodati brašna da testo bude ni tvrdo ni meko.
  - Oblikovati kruščice i ispeći; pečene izdubiti.
  - Mrvice pomešati sa džemom, mlekom i šećerom i spajati kruščice.
  - Jednu stranu umočiti u mleko sa ekstraktom maline a drugu u mleko sa ekstraktom kruške pa uvaljati u kristal šećer.
  en:
  - Beat the lard, sugar and eggs then add the tablespoon of flour and baking powder; rest 1 hour.
  - Add flour until the dough is neither hard nor soft.
  - Shape little pears and bake; hollow them out when baked.
  - Mix the crumbs with jam, milk and sugar and sandwich the pear halves together.
  - Dip one side in milk with raspberry extract and the other in milk with pear extract then roll in granulated sugar.
---
