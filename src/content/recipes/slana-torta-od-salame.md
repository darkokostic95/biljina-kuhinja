---
slug: slana-torta-od-salame
title:
  sr: Slana torta od salame
  en: Salami and Cheese Party Cake
category: salate-predjela
tags:
- meso
- sir
- bez-pecenja
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 1/2 kg sira
  - 1 žumance
  - 2 kašike kajmaka
  - salama na kolutove
  - majonez
  en:
  - 1/2 kg white cheese
  - 1 egg yolk
  - 2 tbsp kajmak (clotted cream)
  - sliced salami
  - mayonnaise
steps:
  sr:
  - Sir žumance i kajmak umutiti mikserom kao penu.
  - Kolutove salame slagati tako da jedan preklapa drugi.
  - 'Ređati: salama, fil; salama, fil, majonez — četiri reda.'
  - Tortu iseći pre serviranja.
  en:
  - Beat the cheese yolk and kajmak to a mousse.
  - Lay the salami slices overlapping.
  - 'Layer: salami, cheese; salami, cheese, mayonnaise — four layers.'
  - Slice before serving.
