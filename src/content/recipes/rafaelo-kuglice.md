ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 1/2 l mleka
  - 6 kašika šećera
  - 6 kašika gustina
  - 1 margarin
  - 4 kašike šećera u prahu
  - 250 g kokosovog brašna
  - lešnici
  - kokos za valjanje
  en:
  - 1/2 l milk
  - 6 tbsp sugar
  - 6 tbsp cornstarch
  - 1 margarine block
  - 4 tbsp powdered sugar
  - 250 g desiccated coconut
  - hazelnuts
  - coconut for rolling
steps:
  sr:
  - Skuvati mleko, šećer i gustin.
  - Kad se ohladi dodati margarin i šećer u prahu pa kokos.
  - Praviti kuglice sa lešnikom u sredini i valjati u kokos.
  en:
  - Cook the milk, sugar and cornstarch.
  - When cool add the margarine and powdered sugar then the coconut.
  - Shape balls with a hazelnut inside and roll in coconut.
