slug: posne-kiflice
title:
  sr: Posne kiflice
  en: Lenten Crescent Rolls
category: peciva
tags:
- posno
- kvasac
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 300 ml tople vode
  - 3 šoljice zejtina
  - 1 kašika soli
  - 2 kašike šećera
  - 1 parče kvasca
  - 1 prašak za pecivo
  - brašno po potrebi
  en:
  - 300 ml warm water
  - 3 small cups oil
  - 1 tbsp salt
  - 2 tbsp sugar
  - 1 piece fresh yeast
  - 1 baking powder
  - flour as needed
steps:
  sr:
  - Zamesiti testo i ostaviti da naraste.
  - Podeliti na 6-7 loptica i razvući.
  - Premazati zejtinom iseći trouglove i uviti kiflice.
  - Ostaviti 1-1 i po sat da narastu pa ispeći.
  en:
  - Knead the dough and let it rise.
  - Divide into 6–7 balls and roll out.
  - Brush with oil cut triangles and roll crescents.
  - Leave 1–1.5 hours to rise then bake.
