  en: Sesame Biscuit Bars
category: bez-pecenja
tags:

ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 200 g šećera
  - srednja šolja vode
  - 100 g čokolade
  - 100 g seckanih oraha
  - 100 g mlevenih oraha
  - 100 g mlevenog keksa
  - 1/2 margarina
  - 100 g susama
  en:
  - 200 g sugar
  - a medium cup of water
  - 100 g chocolate
  - 100 g chopped walnuts
  - 100 g ground walnuts
  - 100 g ground biscuits
  - 1/2 margarine block
  - 100 g sesame
steps:
  sr:
  - Susam ispržiti na suvo.
  - Šećer i vodu ukuvati (ušpilovati).
  - Dodati čokoladu orahe keks margarin i susam i sve sjediniti.
  - Rasporediti u pleh i kad se stegne seći na štanglice.
  en:
  - Dry-toast the sesame.
  - Boil the sugar and water to a syrup.
  - Add the chocolate walnuts biscuits margarine and sesame and mix.
  - Spread in a tin and cut into bars once set.
notes:
