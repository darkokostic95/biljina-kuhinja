---
slug: pogacice-s-krompirom
title:
  sr: Pogačice s krompirom
  en: Potato Scones
category: peciva
tags:
- kvasac
- sir
- susam
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 1/2 kg krompira
  - 150 g masti
  - 3 dl mleka
  - 2 žumanca
  - 1 parče kvasca
  - 800 g brašna
  - 1 kašika soli
  - belance
  - sir ili susam
  en:
  - 1/2 kg potatoes
  - 150 g lard
  - 3 dl milk
  - 2 egg yolks
  - 1 piece fresh yeast
  - 800 g flour
  - 1 tbsp salt
  - egg white
  - cheese or sesame
steps:
  sr:
  - Skuvati krompir i napraviti pire.
  - Kad se malo ohladi dodati žumanca mast mleko sa kvascem i so pa zamesiti.
  - Ostaviti da odstoji kao za krofne.
  - Razviti koru debljine prsta i vaditi pogačice čašom.
  - Premazati belancetom (može udubljenje sa sirom ili susamom) i ispeći.
  en:
  - Boil the potatoes and mash.
  - When slightly cool add the yolks lard milk with yeast and salt and knead.
  - Leave to rise as for doughnuts.
  - Roll a finger thick and cut rounds with a glass.
  - Brush with egg white (or make a dip filled with cheese or sesame) and bake.
