ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Kora (×2)
    en: Sponge (×2)
  sr:
  - 8 belanaca
  - 8 kašika šećera
  - 8 kašika mlevenih oraha
  - 8 kašika keksa
  - 1 i po kašika brašna
  en:
  - 8 egg whites
  - 8 tbsp sugar
  - 8 tbsp ground walnuts
  - 8 tbsp ground biscuits
  - 1.5 tbsp flour
- group:
    sr: Fil
    en: Filling
  sr:
  - 1 l mleka
  - 16 žumanaca
  - 2 pudinga od vanile
  - 5 kašika gustina
  - 200 g šećera
  - 1 margarin
  - šećer u prahu
  - ostatak (od 300 g) plazma keksa
  - 150 g seckane čokolade
  - 250 g seckanih oraha
  - 1 rendana pomorandža
  en:
  - 1 l milk
  - 16 egg yolks
  - 2 vanilla pudding mixes
  - 5 tbsp cornstarch
  - 200 g sugar
  - 1 margarine block
  - powdered sugar
  - 300 g Plazma biscuits
  - 150 g chopped chocolate
  - 250 g chopped walnuts
  - 1 grated orange
steps:
  sr:
  - Ispeći dve kore.
  - U mleku na pari skuvati krem od žumanaca, pudinga, gustina i šećera.
  - U ohlađen krem dodati margarin umućen sa šećerom u prahu.
  - Umešati izlomljenu plazmu, čokoladu, orahe i rendanu pomorandžu i filovati.
  - Odozgo glazura od čokolade ili šlag.
  en:
  - Bake two sponge layers.
  - Cook the yolks, pudding, cornstarch and sugar in the milk over steam.
  - Beat in the margarine creamed with powdered sugar once the custard is cool.
  - Fold in the broken Plazma biscuits, chocolate, walnuts and grated orange and fill the cake.
  - Top with chocolate glaze or whipped cream.
