title:
  sr: Mešana salata sa cveklom
  en: Beetroot and Potato Salad
category: salate-predjela
tags:
- povrce
- orasi
sources:
- IMG_5623
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 500 g kuvanog krompira
  - 300 g cvekle
  - 100 g oraha
  - 4 kuvana jaja
  - 500 g krastavaca
  - 100 g maslina
  - 1 dl jogurta
  - sirće
  - ulje
  - biber
  - so
  en:
  - 500 g boiled potatoes
  - 300 g beetroot
  - 100 g walnuts
  - 4 boiled eggs
  - 500 g cucumbers
  - 100 g olives
  - 1 dl yogurt
  - vinegar
  - oil
  - pepper
  - salt
steps:
  sr:
  - Krompir cveklu jaja i krastavce iseći na kocke a orahe iseckati.
  - Dodati masline i začiniti jogurtom sirćetom uljem biberom i solju.
  - Sipati u tanjir i ukrasiti kolutovima cvekle.
  en:
  - Dice the potatoes beetroot eggs and cucumbers and chop the walnuts.
  - Add the olives and dress with yogurt vinegar oil pepper and salt.
  - Serve on a plate decorated with beetroot slices.
