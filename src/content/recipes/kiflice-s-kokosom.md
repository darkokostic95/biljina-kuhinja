---
slug: kiflice-s-kokosom
title:
  sr: Kiflice s kokosom
  en: Coconut Crescent Cookies
category: sitni-kolaci
tags:
- kokos
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 2 jaja
  - 100 g šećera u prahu
  - 1 margarin
  - 200 g kokosovog brašna
  - 500 g brašna
  - 1 prašak za pecivo
  en:
  - 2 eggs
  - 100 g powdered sugar
  - 1 margarine block
  - 200 g desiccated coconut
  - 500 g flour
  - 1 baking powder
steps:
  sr:
  - Umutiti jaja sa šećerom u prahu i margarinom.
  - Dodati kokos brašno i prašak.
  - Oblikovati kiflice i peći u nepodmazanom plehu.
  en:
  - Beat the eggs with the powdered sugar and margarine.
  - Add the coconut flour and baking powder.
  - Shape crescents and bake on an ungreased tray.
