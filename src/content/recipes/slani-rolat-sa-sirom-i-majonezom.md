  sr: Slani rolat sa grisinama
  en: Savoury Roulade with Breadsticks
category: salate-predjela
tags:

sources:

ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Kora
    en: Sponge
  sr:
  - 6 jaja
  - 1 kašičica soli
  - 6 kašika brašna
  - 2 dl jogurta
  - 1 kašika ulja
  - 1 prašak za pecivo
  en:
  - 6 eggs
  - 1 tsp salt
  - 6 tbsp flour
  - 2 dl yogurt
  - 1 tbsp oil
  - 1 baking powder
- group:
    sr: Fil
    en: Filling
  sr:
  - 300 g tvrdog sira
  - 1 pavlaka
  - 100 g majoneza
  - 3 kašike majoneza sa kikirikijem
  - kačkavalj
  - grisine
  en:
  - 300 g hard cheese
  - 1 tub sour cream
  - 100 g mayonnaise
  - 3 tbsp peanut mayonnaise
  - kashkaval cheese
  - breadsticks (grissini)
steps:
  sr:
  - Umutiti belanca pa dodati umućena žumanca sa solju ulje jogurt i brašno sa praškom.
  - Sipati u pleh sa papirom i peći na 200 °C 15 minuta.
  - Pečenu koru uviti u vlažnu krpu i ohladiti.
  - Premazati filom, poređati grisine i uviti u rolat.
  - Odozgo namazati fil i posuti kačkavaljem.
  en:
  - Whisk the whites then add the yolks beaten with salt and the oil yogurt and flour with baking powder.
  - Pour onto a lined tray and bake at 200 °C for 15 minutes.
  - Roll the hot sheet in a damp cloth and let cool.
  - Spread with the filling, lay breadsticks along it and roll up.
  - Spread filling on top and sprinkle with cheese.
