slug: rolat-pogaca
title:
  sr: Rolat pogača
  en: Pull-Apart Roll Bread
category: peciva
tags:
- kvasac
- susam
sources:
- IMG_5673
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - parče kvasca
  - 1/2 l mleka
  - brašno po potrebi
  - 2 jaja
  - 1 kašika soli
  - mast
  - žumance
  - susam
  en:
  - a piece of fresh yeast
  - 1/2 l milk
  - flour as needed
  - 2 eggs
  - 1 tbsp salt
  - lard
  - egg yolk
  - sesame
steps:
  sr:
  - Kvasac rastopiti u mleku i zamesiti testo sa jajima i solju da se odvaja od ruke.
  - Razviti koru premazati mašću i uviti u rolat.
  - Seći i ređati u tepsiju.
  - Kad nadođe premazati žumancetom posuti susamom i ispeći.
  en:
  - Dissolve the yeast in the milk and knead with the eggs and salt until it leaves the hand.
  - Roll out spread with lard and roll up.
  - Slice and arrange in a tin.
  - When risen brush with yolk sprinkle with sesame and bake.
