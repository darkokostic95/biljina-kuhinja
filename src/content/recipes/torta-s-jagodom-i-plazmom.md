  - 1 kg whipped topping powder
  - 1 l sparkling water
  - 300 g ground Plazma
  - strawberry whipped topping
  - grated chocolate

  sr:
  - Šlag umutiti sa kiselom vodom i podeliti na 3 dela.
  - U I deo dodati plazmu u II šlag sa jagodom u III rendanu čokoladu.
  - Slagati slojeve u činiju ili kalup i ohladiti.
  en:
  - Whip the topping with sparkling water and divide into 3.
  - Add Plazma to the first strawberry topping to the second and grated chocolate to the third.
  - Layer in a bowl or mould and chill.
