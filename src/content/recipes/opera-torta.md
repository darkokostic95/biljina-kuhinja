ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Kore (×3)
    en: Sponges (×3)
  sr:
  - 300 g šećera
  - 2 dcl vode
  - 300 g mlevenih oraha
  - 1 štangla čokolade
  - 1 kašičica kafe
  - 2 kašike brašna
  - prašak za pecivo
  - 10 žumanaca
  - 10 belanaca
  en:
  - 300 g sugar
  - 2 dl water
  - 300 g ground walnuts
  - 1 bar chocolate
  - 1 tsp coffee
  - 2 tbsp flour
  - baking powder
  - 10 egg yolks
  - 10 egg whites
- group:
    sr: Crni fil
    en: Dark filling
  sr:
  - 300 g mlevene plazme
  - 1 gust sok
  - 1 margarin
  - 1 štangla otopljene čokolade
  - 3 žumanca
  en:
  - 300 g ground Plazma
  - 1 carton thick juice
  - 1 margarine block
  - 1 bar melted chocolate
  - 3 egg yolks
- group:
    sr: Beli fil
    en: White filling
  sr:
  - 3 belanca
  - 150 g šećera u prahu
  - 3 vanilin šećera
  en:
  - 3 egg whites
  - 150 g powdered sugar
  - 3 vanilla sugar sachets
steps:
  sr:
  - Šećer i vodu da provri pa dodati orahe čokoladu kafu brašno i prašak.
  - U ohlađeno dodati umućena žumanca pa sneg od belanaca; podeliti u 3 dela i ispeći 3 korice.
  - Plazmu potopiti u sok; posebno umutiti margarin čokoladu i žumanca pa sjediniti.
  - Belanca umutiti sa šećerom u prahu i vanilom.
  - 'Filovati: kora, braon fil, beli fil — ponoviti — na poslednju koru samo braon fil; odozgo šlag ili glazura.'
  en:
  - Bring the sugar and water to a boil then add the walnuts chocolate coffee flour and baking powder.
  - When cool add the beaten yolks then the whisked whites; divide in 3 and bake 3 sponges.
  - Soak the Plazma in juice; beat the margarine chocolate and yolks separately then combine.
  - Whisk the whites with powdered sugar and vanilla.
  - 'Layer: sponge, brown filling, white filling — repeat — only brown filling on the top sponge; finish with cream or glaze.'
