ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 200 g keksa
  - 200 g oraha
  - 100 g čokolade
  - 1/2 kg kora
  - 1 margarin
  en:
  - 200 g biscuits
  - 200 g walnuts
  - 100 g chocolate
  - 1/2 kg filo sheets
  - 1 margarine block
- group:
    sr: Šerbet
    en: Syrup
  sr:
  - 1/2 kg šećera
  - 2 dl vode
  - pola limuna
  en:
  - 1/2 kg sugar
  - 2 dl water
  - half a lemon
steps:
  sr:
  - Sastaviti fil od keksa oraha i čokolade.
  - Fil uviti u kore u rolnice i premazati otopljenim margarinom.
  - Ispeći.
  - Vruće rolnice preliti hladnim šerbetom.
  en:
  - Mix the biscuits walnuts and chocolate for the filling.
  - Roll the filling up in the filo and brush with melted margarine.
  - Bake.
  - Pour the cold syrup over the hot rolls.
