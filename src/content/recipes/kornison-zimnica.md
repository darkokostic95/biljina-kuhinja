title:
  sr: Kornišoni (zimnica)
  en: Pickled Gherkins
category: zimnica
tags:
- povrce
aliases:
  sr:
  - krastavčići
  - kiseli krastavci
  en:
  - pickles
  - cucumbers
sources:
- IMG_5581
- IMG_5582
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Rastvor (za 4 tegle)
    en: Brine (for 4 jars)
  sr:
  - 1 l vode
  - 250 g alkoholnog sirćeta
  - 1 kašika soli
  - malo šećera
  en:
  - 1 l water
  - 250 g white spirit vinegar
  - 1 tbsp salt
  - a little sugar
- group:
    sr: Za tegle
    en: For the jars
  sr:
  - kornišoni
  - zrna bibera
  en:
  - gherkins
  - peppercorns
steps:
  sr:
  - Kornišone naslagati u tegle.
  - Vodu, sirće, so i šećer promešati da se otopi i naliti tegle (ili u svaku teglu staviti šoljicu sirćeta + kašičicu šećera + kašičicu soli + biber pa doliti vodu).
  - Zatvoriti tegle, promućkati i pasterizovati.
  - Ostaviti da se same ohlade.
  en:
  - Pack the gherkins into jars.
  - Stir the water, vinegar, salt and sugar until dissolved and fill the jars (or put a small cup of vinegar + 1 tsp sugar + 1 tsp salt + peppercorns into each jar and top up with water).
  - Close the jars, shake and pasteurise.
  - Let them cool by themselves.
