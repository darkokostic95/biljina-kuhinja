  - 250 g otopljene čokolade
  en:
  - 1 margarine block
  - 150 g powdered sugar
  - 12 egg yolks
  - 250 g melted chocolate
- group:
    sr: Korice (×3)
    en: Sponges (×3)
  sr:
  - 4 belanca
  - 4 kašike šećera
  - 1 kašika brašna
  - 1 kašičica limunovog soka
  - 100 g mlevenih oraha
  en:
  - 4 egg whites
  - 4 tbsp sugar
  - 1 tbsp flour
  - 1 tsp lemon juice
  - 100 g ground walnuts
- group:
    sr: Puding fil
    en: Pudding filling
  sr:
  - 700 g mleka
  - 3 pudinga od čokolade
  - 200 g šećera
  - 100 g čokolade
  - 150 g margarina
  en:
  - 700 g milk
  - 3 chocolate pudding mixes
  - 200 g sugar
  - 100 g chocolate
  - 150 g margarine
steps:
  sr:
  - Umutiti margarin sa šećerom u prahu i žumancima pa dodati otopljenu čokoladu.
  - Umutiti tri korice od belanaca i u svaku umešati po 3 kašike čokoladnog fila; ispeći.
  - U mleku skuvati pudinge sa šećerom i čokoladom.
  - Kad se ohladi sjediniti sa ostatkom čokoladnog fila i još 150 g margarina.
  - Filovati korice.
  en:
  - Beat the margarine with the powdered sugar and yolks then add the melted chocolate.
  - Whisk the three meringue sponges and fold 3 tbsp of the chocolate filling into each; bake.
  - Cook the puddings in the milk with the sugar and chocolate.
  - When cool combine with the remaining chocolate filling and another 150 g margarine.
  - Fill the sponges.

  en: 'Older version in the notebook (IMG_5547): filling of 3 margarine blocks, 300 g powdered sugar, 300 g chocolate and 21 yolks; 3 sponges from 7 whites, 7 tbsp sugar, 14 tbsp walnuts and 4 tbsp flour, sprinkle with juice, bake at 200 °C.'
