---
slug: snicle-u-sosu
title:
  sr: Šnicle u sosu od kečapa
  en: Schnitzels Baked in Ketchup Sauce
category: glavna-jela
tags:
- meso
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - šnicle
  - so
  - ulje
  - 200 g kečapa
  - majonez
  - pavlaka
  en:
  - schnitzels (thin cutlets)
  - salt
  - oil
  - 200 g ketchup
  - mayonnaise
  - sour cream
steps:
  sr:
  - Šnicle izlupati posoliti i kratko prodinstati sa obe strane.
  - Poređati u pleh.
  - U ulje dodati kečap i svaku šniclu umočiti sa obe strane.
  - Odozgo namazati majonez pa pavlaku i zapeći.
  en:
  - Pound the cutlets season and briefly sear on both sides.
  - Arrange in a baking dish.
  - Stir the ketchup into the oil and dip each cutlet on both sides.
  - Spread with mayonnaise then sour cream and bake until golden.
