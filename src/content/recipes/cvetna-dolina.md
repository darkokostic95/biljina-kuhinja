---
slug: cvetna-dolina
title:
  sr: Cvetna dolina
  en: Flower Valley Cake
category: torte
tags:
- cokolada
- orasi
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Kore (×3)
    en: Sponges (×3)
  sr:
  - 5 belanaca
  - 5 kašika šećera
  - 10 kašika mlevenih oraha
  - 1 kašika brašna
  en:
  - 5 egg whites
  - 5 tbsp sugar
  - 10 tbsp ground walnuts
  - 1 tbsp flour
- group:
    sr: Beli fil
    en: White filling
  sr:
  - 1 l mleka
  - 6-7 kašika šećera
  - 10 kašika brašna
  - 2-3 vanile
  - 1 margarin
  - 200 g šećera u prahu
  en:
  - 1 l milk
  - 6-7 tbsp sugar
  - 10 tbsp flour
  - 2-3 vanilla sugar sachets
  - 1 margarine block
  - 200 g powdered sugar
- group:
    sr: Čokolada
    en: Chocolate
  sr:
  - 300 g rastopljene čokolade
  en:
  - 300 g melted chocolate
- group:
    sr: Žuti fil
    en: Yellow filling
  sr:
  - 15 žumanaca
  - 15 kašika šećera
  - 1 margarin
  - 200 g šećera u prahu
  en:
  - 15 egg yolks
  - 15 tbsp sugar
  - 1 margarine block
  - 200 g powdered sugar
steps:
  sr:
  - Ispeći 3 kore.
  - Za beli fil skuvati mleko, šećer, brašno i vanilu pa dodati margarin umućen sa šećerom u prahu.
  - Žumanca i šećer kuvati na pari pa dodati margarin umućen sa šećerom u prahu.
  - 'Filovati: kora — beli fil — preliti čokoladom — žuti fil, i tako redom.'
  en:
  - Bake 3 sponge layers.
  - For the white filling cook the milk, sugar, flour and vanilla then add margarine creamed with powdered sugar.
  - Cook the yolks and sugar over steam then add margarine creamed with powdered sugar.
  - 'Layer: sponge — white filling — melted chocolate — yellow filling, and repeat.'
