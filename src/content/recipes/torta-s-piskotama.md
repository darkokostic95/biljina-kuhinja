---
slug: torta-s-piskotama
title:
  sr: Torta s piškotama
  en: Ladyfinger Cake
category: torte
tags:
- cokolada
- lesnik
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Fil
    en: Filling
  sr:
  - 2 l mleka
  - 4 pudinga od vanile
  - 24 kašike šećera
  - 10 kašika gustina
  - 3 margarina
  - 250 g šećera u prahu
  - 200 g rendane čokolade sa lešnikom
  en:
  - 2 l milk
  - 4 vanilla pudding mixes
  - 24 tbsp sugar
  - 10 tbsp cornstarch
  - 3 margarine blocks
  - 250 g powdered sugar
  - 200 g grated hazelnut chocolate
- group:
    sr: Preliv za piškote
    en: Ladyfinger glaze
  sr:
  - 1/2 margarina
  - 200-300 g čokolade za kuvanje
  - malo mleka
  en:
  - 1/2 margarine block
  - 200-300 g cooking chocolate
  - a little milk
- group:
    sr: Piškote
    en: Ladyfingers
  sr:
  - 450 g piškota
  - 400 g piškota
  - mleko za potapanje
  en:
  - 450 g ladyfingers
  - 400 g ladyfingers
  - milk for dipping
steps:
  sr:
  - Mleko, pudinge, šećer i gustin skuvati na pari i ohladiti.
  - U hladan fil dodati margarine umućene sa šećerom u prahu.
  - Fil podeliti na 2 dela (žuti deo neka bude malo veći); u jedan dodati čokoladu.
  - 'Ređati: 450 g piškota potopljenih u mleko, čokoladni fil, 400 g piškota potopljenih u preliv, žuti fil, šlag.'
  en:
  - Cook the milk, puddings, sugar and cornstarch over steam and cool.
  - Beat the margarine creamed with powdered sugar into the cold filling.
  - Divide in two (the yellow part a little bigger); add the chocolate to one part.
  - 'Layer: 450 g ladyfingers dipped in milk, chocolate filling, 400 g ladyfingers dipped in the glaze, yellow filling, whipped cream.'
notes:
  sr: 'Druga verzija (IMG_5659/5660): umesto drugog reda piškota ide 400 g puslica potopljenih u preliv od 1/2 margarina, 300 g čokolade i malo mleka.'
  en: 'Another version (IMG_5659/5660): the second layer is 400 g meringue kisses dipped in a glaze of 1/2 margarine block, 300 g chocolate and a little milk.'
