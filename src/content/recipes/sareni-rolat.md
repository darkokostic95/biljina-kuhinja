---
slug: sareni-rolat
title:
  sr: Šareni rolat od oblande
  en: Two-Colour Wafer Roll
category: bez-pecenja
tags:
- bez-pecenja
- oblanda
- kokos
- keks
- orasi
- cokolada
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Beli fil
    en: White filling
  sr:
  - 80 ml vode
  - 200 g šećera
  - 1/2 margarina
  - 200 g kokosa
  en:
  - 80 ml water
  - 200 g sugar
  - 1/2 margarine block
  - 200 g coconut
- group:
    sr: Crni fil
    en: Dark filling
  sr:
  - 300 ml vode
  - 250 g šećera
  - 1/2 margarina
  - 100 g čokolade
  - 500 g mlevenog keksa
  - 150 g mlevenih oraha
  - 2 kore od oblande
  en:
  - 300 ml water
  - 250 g sugar
  - 1/2 margarine block
  - 100 g chocolate
  - 500 g ground biscuits
  - 150 g ground walnuts
  - 2 wafer sheets
steps:
  sr:
  - Ukuvati vodu i šećer dodati margarin i kokos.
  - Za crni fil ukuvati vodu i šećer dodati margarin i čokoladu pa keks i orahe.
  - Filovati oblande crnim pa belim filom.
  - Uvijati od belog ka crnom filu.
  en:
  - Boil the water and sugar add the margarine and coconut.
  - For the dark filling boil the water and sugar add the margarine and chocolate then the biscuits and walnuts.
  - Spread the wafers with dark then white filling.
  - Roll up starting from the white side.
