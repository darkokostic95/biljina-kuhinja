---
slug: rendana-salata
title:
  sr: Rendana salata
  en: Grated Egg and Cheese Salad
category: salate-predjela
tags:
- sir
- brzo
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 200 g majoneza
  - 2 pavlake
  - jaja
  - kačkavalj
  - krastavčići (po želji)
  en:
  - 200 g mayonnaise
  - 2 tubs sour cream
  - eggs
  - kashkaval cheese
  - gherkins (optional)
steps:
  sr:
  - Umutiti majonez sa pavlakom i premazati činiju.
  - Narendati jaje kačkavalj (i krastavčiće) pa premazati smesom.
  - Ponavljati dok ima sastojaka a na kraju majonez i ukras od jajeta.
  en:
  - Mix the mayonnaise with the sour cream and coat the bowl.
  - Grate in egg cheese (and gherkins) then spread with the dressing.
  - Repeat until used up finishing with dressing and egg decoration.
---
