ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 2 l Sinalka od narandže
  - 4 i po veće kutije piškota
  - 3 pudinga od vanile
  - 10 kašika šećera
  - 1 i po margarin
  - 1,5 kg banana
  - 1 kg kivija
  - 300 g šlaga
  en:
  - 2 l Sinalco orange soda
  - 4.5 large boxes ladyfingers
  - 3 vanilla pudding mixes
  - 10 tbsp sugar
  - 1.5 margarine blocks
  - 1.5 kg bananas
  - 1 kg kiwis
  - 300 g whipped topping
steps:
  sr:
  - U 1 l soka skuvati pudinge sa šećerom pa ohladiti i sjediniti sa margarinom.
  - Piškote umakati u drugi litar soka.
  - 'Ređati: piškote, krem, banane, kivi — ponoviti.'
  - Odozgo šlag.
  en:
  - Cook the puddings with the sugar in 1 l of juice then cool and beat in the margarine.
  - Dip the ladyfingers in the other litre of juice.
  - 'Layer: ladyfingers, cream, bananas, kiwi — repeat.'
  - Finish with whipped cream.
