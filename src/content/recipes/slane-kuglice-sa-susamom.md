---
slug: slane-kuglice-sa-susamom
title:
  sr: Slane kuglice sa susamom
  en: Savoury Sesame Balls
category: peciva
tags:
- susam
- kikiriki
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 125 g margarina
  - 125 g masti
  - 1 šoljica mleka
  - 1 prašak za pecivo
  - 1 žumance
  - brašno po potrebi
  - malo soli
  - tucani kikiriki
  - 1 belance
  - susam
  en:
  - 125 g margarine
  - 125 g lard
  - 1 small cup milk
  - 1 baking powder
  - 1 egg yolk
  - flour as needed
  - a little salt
  - crushed peanuts
  - 1 egg white
  - sesame seeds
steps:
  sr:
  - Zamesiti testo i dodati kikiriki.
  - Praviti kuglice (može sa kikirikijem u sredini).
  - Umakati u umućeno belance pa valjati u susam.
  - Ispeći.
  en:
  - Knead the dough and add the peanuts.
  - Shape small balls (a peanut inside is nice).
  - Dip in beaten egg white then roll in sesame.
  - Bake.
notes:
  sr: 'Druga verzija (IMG_5647): 1 margarin, 1 jaje, 2 žumanca, 1 kašičica soli, malo peciva i malo manje od 500 g brašna.'
  en: 'Another version (IMG_5647): 1 margarine block, 1 egg, 2 yolks, 1 tsp salt, a little baking powder and just under 500 g flour.'
