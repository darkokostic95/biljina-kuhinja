---
slug: salata-od-sargarepe-zimnica
title:
  sr: Salata od šargarepe (zimnica)
  en: Carrot and Pepper Salad for Winter
category: zimnica
tags:
- povrce
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 3 kg šargarepe
  - 30 paprika
  - 3-4 glavice belog luka
  - 1 jogurtska čaša zejtina
  - 1 čaša šećera
  - 1 čaša sirćeta
  - so po ukusu
  en:
  - 3 kg carrots
  - 30 peppers
  - 3–4 heads garlic
  - 1 yogurt glass oil
  - 1 glass sugar
  - 1 glass vinegar
  - salt to taste
steps:
  sr:
  - Šargarepu malo probariti i iseckati.
  - Iseckati paprike i beli luk i sve staviti u jedan sud.
  - Dodati zejtin šećer sirće i so i promešati.
  - Sipati u tegle.
  en:
  - Blanch the carrots briefly and chop.
  - Chop the peppers and garlic and put everything in one bowl.
  - Add the oil sugar vinegar and salt and mix.
  - Fill into jars.
