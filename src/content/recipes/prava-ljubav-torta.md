slug: prava-ljubav-torta
title:
  sr: Pravo na ljubav
  en: Right to Love Cake
category: torte
tags:
- plazma
- lesnik
- cokolada
- orasi
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Kore (×3)
    en: Sponges (×3)
  sr:
  - 5 belanaca
  - 10 kašika šećera
  - 2 kašike plazme
  - 1 kašika brašna
  - 5 kašika oraha
  - 1 ravna kašika belog sirćeta
  en:
  - 5 egg whites
  - 10 tbsp sugar
  - 2 tbsp Plazma
  - 1 tbsp flour
  - 5 tbsp walnuts
  - 1 level tbsp white vinegar
- group:
    sr: Fil
    en: Filling
  sr:
  - 15 žumanaca
  - 15 kašika šećera
  - 1 šlag (sa 350-400 g hladnog mleka)
  - 2 margarina
  - 250 g šećera u prahu
  - 300 g plazme
  - malo prženog lešnika
  - 300 g čokolade
  en:
  - 15 egg yolks
  - 15 tbsp sugar
  - 1 whipped topping (with 350–400 g cold milk)
  - 2 margarine blocks
  - 250 g powdered sugar
  - 300 g Plazma
  - a few roasted hazelnuts
  - 300 g chocolate
steps:
  sr:
  - Ispeći 3 kore.
  - Žumanca i šećer skuvati na pari i ohladiti.
  - Umutiti margarine sa šećerom u prahu pa dodati žumanca i umućen šlag.
  - Podeliti na pola — u jedan deo plazma i lešnik a u drugi prohlađena čokolada.
  - 'Filovati: kora, krem od plazme, krem od čokolade i tako redom.'
  en:
  - Bake 3 sponges.
  - Cook the yolks and sugar over steam and cool.
  - Cream the margarine with the powdered sugar then add the yolks and whipped topping.
  - Divide in two — Plazma and hazelnuts in one half and cooled chocolate in the other.
  - 'Layer: sponge, Plazma cream, chocolate cream and repeat.'
