  sr: Čokoladni mafini
  en: Chocolate Muffins

ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 4 jaja
  - 160 g šećera
  - 120 g brašna
  - 220 g čokolade
  - 250 g margarina
  en:
  - 4 eggs
  - 160 g sugar
  - 120 g flour
  - 220 g chocolate
  - 250 g margarine
steps:
  sr:
  - Umutiti jaja sa šećerom i dodati brašno.
  - Na pari istopiti čokoladu i margarin pa sve spojiti.
  - Sipati u papirne korpice i peći u zagrejanoj rerni 7 minuta.
  en:
  - Whisk the eggs with the sugar and add the flour.
  - Melt the chocolate and margarine over steam then combine everything.
  - Pour into paper cases and bake in a hot oven for 7 minutes.
notes:
