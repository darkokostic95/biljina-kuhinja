ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Testo
    en: Choux pastry
  sr:
  - 2 jogurtske čaše vode
  - 140 ml zejtina
  - 220 g brašna
  - 6 jaja
  en:
  - 2 yogurt glasses water
  - 140 ml oil
  - 220 g flour
  - 6 eggs
- group:
    sr: Fil
    en: Filling
  sr:
  - 1 l mleka
  - 6 šoljica šećera
  - 10 kašika brašna
  - 1 puding od vanile
  - 2 vanilin šećera
  - 200 g margarina
  en:
  - 1 l milk
  - 6 small cups sugar
  - 10 tbsp flour
  - 1 vanilla pudding mix
  - 2 vanilla sugar sachets
  - 200 g margarine
- group:
    sr: Glazura
    en: Glaze
  sr:
  - 1 štangla čokolade
  - trećina margarina
  - 1 jaje
  en:
  - 1 bar chocolate
  - a third of a margarine block
  - 1 egg
steps:
  sr:
  - Vodu i zejtin da provri pa dodati brašno i izmešati; ohladiti.
  - Dodavati jaja jedno po jedno.
  - Stavljati u kalupe (kao za tulumbe) i ispeći.
  - Odvojiti malo mleka i u njemu razmutiti šećer brašno puding i vanilu pa sipati u vrelo mleko i skuvati; dodati margarin.
  - Pečene eklere preseći i napuniti filom.
  - Čokoladu istopiti sa margarinom pa dodati jaje i umutiti; preliti eklere.
  en:
  - Boil the water and oil then add the flour and stir; let cool.
  - Beat in the eggs one at a time.
  - Pipe into moulds (like tulumbe) and bake.
  - Mix the sugar flour pudding and vanilla with a little cold milk then whisk into the boiling milk and cook; add the margarine.
  - Split the baked eclairs and fill.
  - Melt the chocolate with the margarine then whisk in the egg; glaze the eclairs.
