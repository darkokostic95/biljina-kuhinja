slug: posni-slani-rolat-s-oblandom
title:
  sr: Posni slani rolat s oblandom i tunjevinom
  en: Lenten Tuna Wafer Rolls
category: salate-predjela
tags:
- posno
- oblanda
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 1 pakovanje veće oblande
  - 2 konzerve tunjevine
  - 200 g kuvane žute soje (ljuspice)
  - 2 šolje od bele kafe kuvanog pirinča
  - 4 glavice crnog luka
  - prezle
  - pivo
  en:
  - 1 pack large wafer sheets
  - 2 tins tuna
  - 200 g cooked soy flakes
  - 2 coffee cups cooked rice
  - 4 onions
  - breadcrumbs
  - beer
steps:
  sr:
  - Upržiti luk pa skloniti sa vatre i dodati oceđenu tunjevinu soju i pirinač; ohladiti.
  - Oblande uviti u vlažne krpe da omeknu.
  - Premazati filom uviti u rolat i zamrznuti u tepsiji.
  - Pre služenja iseći umakati u pivo pa u prezle.
  - Ređati u podmazan pleh i peći na 250 °C dok ne porumene.
  en:
  - Fry the onions take off the heat and add the drained tuna soy and rice; cool.
  - Wrap the wafers in damp cloths to soften.
  - Spread with filling roll up and freeze in a tin.
  - Before serving slice dip in beer then breadcrumbs.
  - Bake on a greased tray at 250 °C until golden.
