---
slug: vocna-torta-sa-keksom
title:
  sr: Voćna torta sa keksom
  en: Fruit Biscuit Cake
category: torte
tags:
- keks
- voce
- bez-pecenja
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 500 g petit keksa
  - 1 margarin
  - 150 g pudinga
  - 12 kašika šećera
  - 1 l mleka ili vode
  - voće
  - sok ili mleko za umakanje
  - 150 g šlaga
  en:
  - 500 g petit-beurre biscuits
  - 1 margarine block
  - 150 g pudding powder
  - 12 tbsp sugar
  - 1 l milk or water
  - fruit
  - juice or milk for dipping
  - 150 g whipped topping
steps:
  sr:
  - U mleku ili vodi skuvati puding sa šećerom i ohladiti.
  - Umutiti margarin i postepeno dodavati puding.
  - 'Ređati: keks (umočen u sok), fil, voće, fil, keks.'
  - Odozgo šlag.
  en:
  - Cook the pudding with sugar in the milk or water and cool.
  - Cream the margarine and gradually beat in the pudding.
  - 'Layer: biscuits (dipped in juice), filling, fruit, filling, biscuits.'
  - Finish with whipped cream.
