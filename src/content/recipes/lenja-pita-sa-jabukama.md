title:
  sr: Lenja pita sa jabukama
  en: Lazy Apple Pie
category: pite
tags:
- jabuke
aliases:
  sr:
  - pita sa jabukama
  en:
  - apple slice
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Testo
    en: Batter
  sr:
  - 2 jaja
  - 250 g šećera
  - 1 jogurt
  - pola jogurtske čaše zejtina
  - 1 prašak za pecivo
  - 250 g brašna
  en:
  - 2 eggs
  - 250 g sugar
  - 1 tub yogurt
  - half a yogurt glass oil
  - 1 baking powder
  - 250 g flour
- group:
    sr: Fil
    en: Filling
  sr:
  - 10 jabuka (oko 1 kg)
  - 10 kašika šećera
  - malo cimeta
  - 2 kašike oraha ili griza
  - šećer u prahu
  en:
  - 10 apples (about 1 kg)
  - 10 tbsp sugar
  - a little cinnamon
  - 2 tbsp walnuts or semolina
  - powdered sugar
steps:
  sr:
  - Umutiti sastojke za testo.
  - Jabuke izrendati i sa šećerom propržiti da ispari voda (ili samo pomešati sa šećerom i cimetom).
  - U pleh sipati pola testa pa jabuke pa ostatak testa.
  - Ispeći i posuti šećerom u prahu.
  en:
  - Whisk the batter ingredients together.
  - Grate the apples and cook with the sugar until the liquid evaporates (or simply mix with sugar and cinnamon).
  - Pour half the batter into the tin then the apples then the rest of the batter.
  - Bake and dust with powdered sugar.
notes:
  sr: 'Starija verzija "iz banje" (IMG_5571): 2 žumanca, 1 šolja šećera, 1 šolja mleka, 1 šolja ulja, 2 i po šolje brašna i 1 prašak; fil 1 kg jabuka sa 1 šoljom šećera.'
  en: 'Older "spa" version (IMG_5571): 2 yolks, 1 cup sugar, 1 cup milk, 1 cup oil, 2.5 cups flour and 1 baking powder; filling 1 kg apples with 1 cup sugar.'
