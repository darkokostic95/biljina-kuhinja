  sr:
  - 2 šoljice vode

  - 1 smrznut limun (izrendan)
  - ekstrakt maline ili jagode
  - mleveni keks

  en:
  - 2 small cups water
  - 3 small cups sugar
  - 1 frozen lemon (grated)
  - raspberry or strawberry extract
  - ground biscuits
  - chocolate glaze
steps:
  sr:
  - Ukuvati vodu i šećer.
  - Dodati rendani limun i ekstrakt.
  - Dodati keksa koliko treba da se oblikuju kuglice.
  - Odozgo glazura od čokolade a preko nje malo bele glazure.
  en:
  - Boil the water and sugar to a syrup.
  - Add the grated lemon and extract.
  - Add enough biscuits to shape balls.
  - Top with chocolate glaze and a drizzle of white icing.
