---
slug: vocna-torta-sa-kiselom-pavlakom
title:
  sr: Voćna torta sa kiselom pavlakom
  en: Fruit and Sour Cream Cake
category: torte
tags:
- voce
- jagode
- banane
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 2 patišpanja od po 4 jaja (sa malo kakaa) ili keks ili piškote
  - 250 g šećera u prahu
  - 300 g šlag kreme
  - kisela voda
  - 2 kisele pavlake
  - 1 margarin
  - voće (jagode ananas banane kivi)
  en:
  - 2 four-egg sponges (with a little cocoa) or biscuits or ladyfingers
  - 250 g powdered sugar
  - 300 g whipped topping
  - sparkling water
  - 2 tubs sour cream
  - 1 margarine block
  - fruit (strawberries pineapple banana kiwi)
steps:
  sr:
  - Ispeći dva patišpanja (ili koristiti keks ili piškote).
  - Šlag umutiti sa kiselom vodom i šećerom u prahu pa dodati pavlaku.
  - Sjediniti sa umućenim margarinom.
  - Staviti fil između kora i dodati voće po želji.
  en:
  - Bake two sponges (or use biscuits or ladyfingers).
  - Whip the topping with sparkling water and powdered sugar then add the sour cream.
  - Combine with the creamed margarine.
  - Fill between the layers with fruit of your choice.
