  en: Three Delights Cake

ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Kora
    en: Sponge
  sr:
  - 7 belanaca
  - 7 kašika šećera
  - 3 kašike prezle
  - 2 kašike brašna
  - 150 g mlevenih oraha
  en:
  - 7 egg whites
  - 7 tbsp sugar
  - 3 tbsp breadcrumbs
  - 2 tbsp flour
  - 150 g ground walnuts
- group:
    sr: Fil
    en: Filling
  sr:
  - 7 žumanaca
  - 15 kašika šećera
  - 2 pudinga od vanile
  - 1/2 l mleka
  - 1 margarin
  - 100 g čokolade
  - 300 g keksa
  - mlako mleko
  - šlag sa želatinom
  - mleveni i seckani orasi
  en:
  - 7 egg yolks
  - 15 tbsp sugar
  - 2 vanilla pudding mixes
  - 1/2 l milk
  - 1 margarine block
  - 100 g chocolate
  - 300 g biscuits
  - lukewarm milk
  - gelatine-set whipped cream
  - ground and chopped walnuts
steps:
  sr:
  - Ispeći koru i ohladiti.
  - Skuvati fil na pari; umutiti margarin i dodati hladan fil.
  - Fil podeliti na 2 dela — u jedan dodati čokoladu.
  - 'Filovati: kora, žuti fil, red keksa natopljenog u mlako mleko, crni fil, šlag sa želatinom (recept „Stabilan šlag sa želatinom“).'
  - Odozgo mleveni i seckani orasi.
  en:
  - Bake and cool the sponge.
  - Cook the filling over steam; cream the margarine and add the cold filling.
  - Divide in 2 — add chocolate to one half.
  - 'Layer: sponge, yellow filling, biscuits dipped in warm milk, dark filling, gelatine whipped cream (see Stabilised Whipped Cream).'
  - Top with ground and chopped walnuts.
notes:
  sr: U svesci označeno „DOBRO".
  en: Marked "GOOD" in the notebook.
