  sr: Bele rozen štangle
  en: White Wafer Bars

ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 1 pakovanje rozen korica
  - 800 g mleka
  - 200 g šećera
  - 3 pudinga sa ukusom slatke pavlake
  - 150 g bele čokolade
  - 250 g margarina
  - 100 g mleka u prahu
  - glazura od bele ili crne čokolade
  en:
  - 1 pack thin wafer sheets (rozen)
  - 800 g milk
  - 200 g sugar
  - 3 cream-flavour pudding mixes
  - 150 g white chocolate
  - 250 g margarine
  - 100 g milk powder
  - white or dark chocolate glaze
steps:
  sr:
  - Skuvati pudinge u mleku sa šećerom.
  - Skloniti sa šporeta i dodati čokoladu i margarin.
  - Kad se sjedine dodati mleko u prahu i promešati.
  - Filovati korice; na poslednju samo glazura.
  - Kad se stegne iseći na štangle ili kocke.
  en:
  - Cook the puddings in the milk with the sugar.
  - Take off the heat and add the chocolate and margarine.
  - Once combined stir in the milk powder.
  - Spread between the wafer sheets; only glaze on the top sheet.
  - When set cut into bars or squares.
