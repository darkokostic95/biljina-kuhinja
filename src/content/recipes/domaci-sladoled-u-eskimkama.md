slug: domaci-sladoled-u-eskimkama
title:
  sr: Domaći sladoled u čašicama
  en: Homemade Frozen Yogurt Cups
category: kremovi-sladoledi
tags:
- bez-pecenja
- brzo
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 1 l mleka
  - 500 g šlag kreme
  - 1 l jogurta
  - 200-500 g šećera
  - 6 čašica od sladoleda (eskimke)
  en:
  - 1 l milk
  - 500 g whipped topping
  - 1 l yogurt
  - 200–500 g sugar
  - 6 ice-cream cups
steps:
  sr:
  - U jednoj činiji umutiti jogurt sa šećerom.
  - U drugoj umutiti mleko sa šlagom.
  - Sjediniti i kratko ohladiti u frižideru.
  - Sipati u čašice i zamrznuti.
  en:
  - Whisk the yogurt with the sugar in one bowl.
  - Whip the milk with the topping in another.
  - Combine and chill briefly.
  - Pour into cups and freeze.
notes:
  sr: Mera je velika — može se praviti i pola. Sa 200-400 g šećera nije preslatko.
  en: This is a large batch — halving works. With 200–400 g sugar it is not too sweet.
