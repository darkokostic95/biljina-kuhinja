  sr:
  - 150 g šećera

  - 1 šoljica mleka
  - 100 g čokolade
  - 100 g mlevenih i seckanih oraha
  - 150 g mlevenog keksa
  en:
  - 150 g sugar
  - 150 g margarine
  - 1 small cup milk
  - 100 g chocolate
  - 100 g ground and chopped walnuts
  - 150 g ground biscuits
steps:
  sr:
  - Šećer, margarin i mleko mešati na vatri dok se ne istopi.
  - Dodati čokoladu, orahe i keks.
  - Staviti na celofan i oblikovati salamu.
  en:
  - Stir the sugar, margarine and milk over the heat until melted.
  - Add the chocolate, walnuts and biscuits.
  - Shape into a log on cling film.
