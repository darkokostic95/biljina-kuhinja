---
slug: slani-stapici
title:
  sr: Slani štapići
  en: Paprika Sesame Sticks
category: peciva
tags:
- susam
- posno
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 200 g mlake vode
  - 200 g ulja
  - 1 kašičica soli
  - oko 800 g brašna
  - 1 žumance
  - 1 kašičica aleve paprike
  - susam
  en:
  - 200 g warm water
  - 200 g oil
  - 1 tsp salt
  - about 800 g flour
  - 1 egg yolk
  - 1 tsp paprika
  - sesame
steps:
  sr:
  - Zamesiti testo od vode ulja soli i brašna.
  - Razvući koru i premazati žumancetom umućenim sa alevom paprikom.
  - Jednu stranu premazati smesom a drugu posuti susamom.
  - Iseći na štapiće uviti i ispeći.
  en:
  - Knead a dough from the water oil salt and flour.
  - Roll out and brush with yolk beaten with paprika.
  - Brush one side with the mixture and sprinkle the other with sesame.
  - Cut into sticks twist and bake.
notes:
  sr: U svesci piše „Ukusno!“
  en: Marked "Delicious!" in the notebook.
