slug: beze-torta
title:
  sr: Beze torta
  en: Meringue Hazelnut Cream Cake
category: torte
tags:
- lesnik
sources:
- IMG_5685
- IMG_5724
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Fil
    en: Filling
  sr:
  - 700 g mleka
  - 5 žumanaca
  - 8 kašika šećera
  - 1 kašika brašna
  - 2 kašike griza
  - 1 puding
  - 1 margarin
  - šećer u prahu
  - 100 g mlevenog lešnika
  - kore od bezea
  en:
  - 700 g milk
  - 5 egg yolks
  - 8 tbsp sugar
  - 1 tbsp flour
  - 2 tbsp semolina
  - 1 pudding mix
  - 1 margarine block
  - powdered sugar
  - 100 g ground hazelnuts
  - meringue layers
steps:
  sr:
  - Od mleka odvojiti malo i u njemu razmutiti žumanca šećer brašno griz i puding.
  - Sipati u vrelo mleko i skuvati.
  - Kad se prohladi dodati margarin umućen sa šećerom u prahu i lešnik.
  - Filovati kore od bezea.
  en:
  - Mix the yolks sugar flour semolina and pudding with a little of the milk.
  - Pour into the hot milk and cook.
  - When cool add the margarine creamed with powdered sugar and the hazelnuts.
  - Fill the meringue layers.
