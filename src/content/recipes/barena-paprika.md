title:
  sr: Barena paprika
  en: Blanched Pickled Peppers
category: zimnica
tags:
- povrce
sources:
- IMG_5634
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - paprike
  - 1/2 l ulja
  - 1/2 l sirćeta
  - 1/2 kg soli
  - 400 g šećera
  - malo konzervansa
  - malo salicila
  - šargarepa
  - beli luk
  - peršun
  - celer
  - ren
  en:
  - peppers
  - 1/2 l oil
  - 1/2 l vinegar
  - 1/2 kg salt
  - 400 g sugar
  - a little preservative
  - a little salicylic
  - carrot
  - garlic
  - parsley
  - celery
  - horseradish
steps:
  sr:
  - Paprike očistiti od drške.
  - Ulje sirće so šećer konzervans i salicil prokuvati.
  - Ubacivati po 7-8 paprika promešati 2-3 puta i vaditi u poklopljen sud.
  - Vruće ređati u tegle sa šargarepom belim lukom peršunom celerom i renom.
  - Na dno i vrh tegle staviti po kašičicu konzervansa i soli.
  en:
  - Remove the stems from the peppers.
  - Boil the oil vinegar salt sugar preservative and salicylic.
  - Add 7–8 peppers at a time stir 2–3 times and lift into a covered pot.
  - Pack hot into jars with carrot garlic parsley celery and horseradish.
  - Put a teaspoon of preservative and salt at the bottom and top of each jar.
