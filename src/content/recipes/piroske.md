---
slug: piroske
title:
  sr: Piroške
  en: Fried Piroshki
category: peciva
tags:
- kvasac
- meso
- sir
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Testo
    en: Dough
  sr:
  - 2 žumanca
  - 2 kašike ulja
  - kvasac
  - 250 g mleka
  - 400 g brašna
  en:
  - 2 egg yolks
  - 2 tbsp oil
  - yeast
  - 250 g milk
  - 400 g flour
- group:
    sr: Fil
    en: Filling
  sr:
  - kečap
  - salama
  - origano
  - sir (po želji)
  - ulje za prženje
  en:
  - ketchup
  - salami
  - oregano
  - cheese (optional)
  - oil for frying
steps:
  sr:
  - Umutiti žumanca i ulje; kvasac rastopiti u mleku i zamesiti sa brašnom.
  - Ostaviti 30 minuta.
  - Razviti koru i vaditi krugove čašom.
  - Staviti kečap salamu origano (i sir) preklopiti i pržiti.
  en:
  - Beat the yolks and oil; dissolve the yeast in the milk and knead with the flour.
  - Rest 30 minutes.
  - Roll out and cut circles with a glass.
  - Add ketchup salami oregano (and cheese) fold over and fry.
notes:
  sr: 'Veća mera (IMG_5678): 1 kg brašna, 500 ml vode, svež kvasac, 2 jaja, 3 kašičice šećera, 2 kašičice soli, 200 ml ulja; fil po želji (npr. pire).'
  en: 'Larger batch (IMG_5678): 1 kg flour, 500 ml water, fresh yeast, 2 eggs, 3 tsp sugar, 2 tsp salt, 200 ml oil; any filling (e.g. mashed potato).'
