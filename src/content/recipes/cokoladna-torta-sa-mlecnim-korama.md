  sr: Čokoladna torta sa mlečnim korama
  en: Chocolate Cake with Milk-Soaked Layers

tags:

sources:

ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Kore (×3)
    en: Sponges (×3)
  sr:
  - 3 jaja
  - 3 kašike šećera
  - 1 i po kašika brašna
  - 1 kašičica kakaa
  - mleko za natapanje
  en:
  - 3 eggs
  - 3 tbsp sugar
  - 1.5 tbsp flour
  - 1 tsp cocoa
  - milk for soaking
- group:
    sr: Fil
    en: Filling
  sr:
  - 1 i po margarin
  - 200 g šećera u prahu
  - 5 žumanaca
  - 150 g rastopljene čokolade
  - 1 kašika kakaa
  - 5 belanaca
  - 5 kašika šećera
  en:
  - 1.5 margarine blocks
  - 200 g powdered sugar
  - 5 egg yolks
  - 150 g melted chocolate
  - 1 tbsp cocoa
  - 5 egg whites
  - 5 tbsp sugar
- group:
    sr: Šlag
    en: Cream
  sr:
  - 300 g šlaga
  - kisela voda
  en:
  - 300 g whipped topping
  - sparkling water
steps:
  sr:
  - Ispeći 3 kore i natopiti ih mlekom.
  - Umutiti margarin sa šećerom u prahu pa dodati žumanca, čokoladu i kakao.
  - Belanca umutiti sa šećerom i sjediniti sa filom.
  - Šlag umutiti sa kiselom vodom.
  - 'Filovati: kora, fil, šlag — tri puta.'
  en:
  - Bake 3 sponges and soak them with milk.
  - Cream the margarine with the powdered sugar then add the yolks, chocolate and cocoa.
  - Whisk the whites with the sugar and fold into the filling.
  - Whip the topping with sparkling water.
  - 'Layer: sponge, filling, cream — three times.'
