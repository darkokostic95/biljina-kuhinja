---
slug: maturski-rad-torta
title:
  sr: Maturski rad torta
  en: Graduation Cake
category: torte
tags:
- cokolada
- orasi
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Kora (×3)
    en: Sponge (×3)
  sr:
  - 3 jaja i 3 belanca
  - 5 kašika šećera
  - 8 kašika mlevenih oraha
  - 1 kašika brašna
  - 1/2 šoljice zejtina
  - 50 g rendane čokolade
  en:
  - 3 eggs and 3 egg whites
  - 5 tbsp sugar
  - 8 tbsp ground walnuts
  - 1 tbsp flour
  - 1/2 small cup oil
  - 50 g grated chocolate
- group:
    sr: Preliv za kore
    en: Soak for the sponges
  sr:
  - 30 kašika vode
  - 6 kašika šećera
  - 1 kocka čokolade
  en:
  - 30 tbsp water
  - 6 tbsp sugar
  - 1 square of chocolate
- group:
    sr: Fil
    en: Filling
  sr:
  - 1 l mleka
  - 14 kašika šećera
  - 9 žumanaca
  - 2 pudinga od vanile
  - 5 kašika gustina
  - 2 margarina
  - 200 g čokolade
  - 2 kesice šlag pene
  en:
  - 1 l milk
  - 14 tbsp sugar
  - 9 egg yolks
  - 2 vanilla pudding mixes
  - 5 tbsp cornstarch
  - 2 margarine blocks
  - 200 g chocolate
  - 2 sachets whipped topping
- group:
    sr: Glazura
    en: Glaze
  sr:
  - 70 g šećera u prahu
  - 1 šoljica mleka
  - 125 g čokolade
  - 1/4 margarina
  en:
  - 70 g powdered sugar
  - 1 small cup milk
  - 125 g chocolate
  - 1/4 margarine block
steps:
  sr:
  - Od sastojaka za koru ispeći 3 kore.
  - Za preliv sve rastopiti i preliti kore.
  - Skuvati fil i podeliti ga na dva dela; u svaki dodati po 1 umućen margarin.
  - U prvi deo dodati čokoladu, a u drugi neumućene šlag pene.
  - 'Filovati: kora, preliv, beli fil, crni fil — ponoviti — i završiti korom sa prelivom.'
  - Glazuru izmešati na pari i preliti tortu (ili staviti šlag).
  en:
  - Bake 3 sponge layers from the sponge ingredients.
  - Melt the soak ingredients together and pour over the sponges.
  - Cook the filling and divide it in two; beat 1 margarine block into each half.
  - Add the chocolate to one half and the dry whipped-topping powder to the other.
  - 'Layer: sponge, soak, white filling, dark filling — repeat — and finish with a soaked sponge.'
  - Mix the glaze over steam and pour it over the cake (or top with whipped cream).
