  en: Homemade Mayonnaise

ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 2 jaja
  - 1 kašičica sirćeta
  - 1 kašika senfa
  - pola kašičice soli
  - pola kašičice šećera
  - 350 g zejtina
  en:
  - 2 eggs
  - 1 tsp vinegar
  - 1 tbsp mustard
  - half a tsp salt
  - half a tsp sugar
  - 350 g oil
steps:
  sr:
  - Umutiti jaja sa sirćetom senfom solju i šećerom.
  - Mutiti uz postepeno dodavanje zejtina.
  en:
  - Whisk the eggs with the vinegar mustard salt and sugar.
  - Keep whisking while adding the oil gradually.
