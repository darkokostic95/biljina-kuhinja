---
slug: rolat-od-spanaca
title:
  sr: Rolat od spanaća
  en: Spinach Roulade
category: salate-predjela
tags:
- povrce
- sir
- meso
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Kora
    en: Sponge
  sr:
  - 150 g margarina
  - 1 šolja od bele kafe brašna
  - 2 šolje od bele kafe mleka
  - 1/2 kg spanaća
  - 4 jaja
  - so
  - malo šećera
  - biber
  en:
  - 150 g margarine
  - 1 coffee cup flour
  - 2 coffee cups milk
  - 1/2 kg spinach
  - 4 eggs
  - salt
  - a little sugar
  - pepper
- group:
    sr: Fil
    en: Filling
  sr:
  - 300 g sira
  - pavlaka
  - 200 g šunke
  - biber
  - rendani kačkavalj
  en:
  - 300 g white cheese
  - sour cream
  - 200 g ham
  - pepper
  - grated kashkaval cheese
steps:
  sr:
  - Istopiti margarin i propržiti brašno.
  - Dodati obaren samleven spanać i propržiti pa dodati mleko i prokuvati; ohladiti.
  - Umutiti jaja sa malo šećera i soli (prvo belanca) i umešati u masu.
  - Ispeći na laganoj vatri.
  - Sir izgnječiti sa pavlakom šunkom i biberom; filovati iznutra i spolja i narendati kačkavalj.
  en:
  - Melt the margarine and toast the flour.
  - Add the blanched minced spinach fry then add the milk and simmer; cool.
  - Whisk the eggs with a pinch of sugar and salt (whites first) and fold in.
  - Bake gently.
  - Mash the cheese with sour cream ham and pepper; fill and coat the roll and grate cheese on top.
---
