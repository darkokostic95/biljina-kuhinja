---
slug: pekarske-kiflice
title:
  sr: Pekarske kiflice
  en: Bakery-Style Crescents
category: peciva
tags:
- kvasac
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 500 ml mleka
  - kvasac
  - 3 žumanca
  - 1 čaša jogurta
  - 1 jogurtska čaša ulja
  - pola jogurtske čaše šećera
  - 2 vanilin šećera
  - prstohvat soli
  - brašno po potrebi
  - džem
  - belance
  en:
  - 500 ml milk
  - yeast
  - 3 egg yolks
  - 1 glass yogurt
  - 1 yogurt glass oil
  - half a yogurt glass sugar
  - 2 vanilla sugar sachets
  - a pinch of salt
  - flour as needed
  - jam
  - egg white
steps:
  sr:
  - U mleku rastopiti kvasac da nadođe.
  - Dodati žumanca jogurt ulje šećer vanilu i so pa brašno i zamesiti.
  - Kad nadođe odvajati loptice i razvući svaku.
  - Praviti kiflice sa džemom i premazati belancetom pa ispeći.
  en:
  - Dissolve the yeast in the milk to froth.
  - Add the yolks yogurt oil sugar vanilla and salt then flour and knead.
  - When risen divide into balls and roll each out.
  - Shape crescents with jam brush with egg white and bake.
notes:
  sr: 'Slana verzija (IMG_5677): 1 čaša mlake vode, 1 čaša mleka, kvasac, pola kašičice soli, 1 šoljica ulja, 6 čaša brašna; 12 lopti, rendani margarin, 32 kiflice, odmaraju 1 sat.'
  en: 'Savoury version (IMG_5677): 1 glass warm water, 1 glass milk, yeast, half a tsp salt, 1 small cup oil, 6 glasses flour; 12 balls, grated margarine, 32 crescents, rest 1 hour.'
