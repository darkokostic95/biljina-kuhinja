---
slug: stangle-s-mirisom-limuna
title:
  sr: Štangle s mirisom limuna
  en: Lemon Meringue Bars
category: kolaci
tags: []
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Sastojci
    en: Ingredients
  sr:
  - 100 g margarina
  - 100 g šećera
  - 5 kašika mleka
  - 3 žumanca
  - pola kesice praška za pecivo
  - 1 ceo limun (kora i sok)
  - 200 g brašna
  - mast za pleh
  - džem (pekmez)
  - 3 belanca
  - 150 g šećera
  en:
  - 100 g margarine
  - 100 g sugar
  - 5 tbsp milk
  - 3 egg yolks
  - half a sachet baking powder
  - 1 whole lemon (zest and juice)
  - 200 g flour
  - lard for the tin
  - jam
  - 3 egg whites
  - 150 g sugar
steps:
  sr:
  - Umutiti margarin pa dodati šećer mleko žumanca prašak i limun.
  - Na kraju dodati brašno i zamesiti.
  - Pleh premazati mašću i peći dok ne bude polupečeno.
  - Premazati džemom.
  - Belanca umutiti sa šećerom i staviti preko džema pa dopeći.
  - Iseći na štangle.
  en:
  - Beat the margarine then add the sugar milk yolks baking powder and lemon.
  - Finally add the flour and knead.
  - Grease the tin with lard and bake until half done.
  - Spread with jam.
  - Whisk the whites with the sugar spread over the jam and finish baking.
  - Cut into bars.
