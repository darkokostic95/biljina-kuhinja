  sr: Posne korpice sa kremom od limuna
  en: Lenten Tartlets with Lemon Cream

tags:

ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Korice
    en: Pastry
  sr:
  - 300 g brašna
  - pola praška za pecivo
  - 125 g margarina
  - 100 g šećera u prahu
  - pola šoljice sojinog (ili običnog) mleka
  en:
  - 300 g flour
  - half a baking powder
  - 125 g margarine
  - 100 g powdered sugar
  - half a small cup soy (or regular) milk
- group:
    sr: Fil
    en: Filling
  sr:
  - 500 g mleka
  - 1 margarin
  - 5 kašika oštrog brašna
  - 10 kašika šećera
  - pola limuna (rendana kora i sok)
  en:
  - 500 g milk
  - 1 margarine block
  - 5 tbsp strong flour
  - 10 tbsp sugar
  - half a lemon (zest and juice)
steps:
  sr:
  - Zamesiti testo i obložiti kalupiće za korpice; ispeći.
  - Skuvati mleko brašno i šećer u gust krem pa dodati margarin i limun.
  - Napuniti korpice.
  en:
  - Knead the pastry and line tartlet moulds; bake.
  - Cook the milk flour and sugar into a thick cream then add the margarine and lemon.
  - Fill the tartlets.
