---
slug: interkontinental-torta
title:
  sr: Interkontinental torta
  en: Intercontinental Cake
category: torte
tags:
- orasi
- cokolada
- kokos
- lesnik
ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Kore (×3)
    en: Sponges (×3)
  sr:
  - 5 belanaca
  - 5 kašika šećera
  - 2 kašike brašna
  - 7 kašika mlevenih oraha
  en:
  - 5 egg whites
  - 5 tbsp sugar
  - 2 tbsp flour
  - 7 tbsp ground walnuts
- group:
    sr: Fil
    en: Filling
  sr:
  - 1 l mleka
  - 15 žumanaca
  - 10 kašika šećera
  - 2 pudinga od vanile
  - 5 kašika gustina
  - 1 margarin
  - 250 g šećera u prahu
  - 100 g čokolade sa lešnikom
  - 100 g mlevenih oraha (ili suvog grožđa)
  - 100 g kokosovog brašna
  en:
  - 1 l milk
  - 15 egg yolks
  - 10 tbsp sugar
  - 2 vanilla pudding mixes
  - 5 tbsp cornstarch
  - 1 margarine block
  - 250 g powdered sugar
  - 100 g hazelnut chocolate
  - 100 g ground walnuts (or raisins)
  - 100 g desiccated coconut
steps:
  sr:
  - Od sastojaka za kore ispeći 3 kore.
  - U mleku skuvati krem od žumanaca, šećera, pudinga i gustina.
  - Margarin umutiti sa šećerom u prahu i sjediniti sa ohlađenim kremom.
  - Fil podeliti na 3 dela — u prvi dodati čokoladu, u drugi orahe, u treći kokos.
  - 'Filovati: kora, I fil, kora, II fil, kora, III fil i odozgo šlag.'
  en:
  - Bake 3 sponge layers.
  - Cook a custard in the milk from the yolks, sugar, pudding mix and cornstarch.
  - Beat the margarine with the powdered sugar and combine with the cooled custard.
  - Divide into 3 — chocolate into the first, walnuts into the second, coconut into the third.
  - 'Layer: sponge, filling I, sponge, filling II, sponge, filling III, then whipped cream on top.'
