  - 8 kašika šećera
  - 8 kašika mlevenih oraha
  - 2 kašike brašna
  en:
  - 8 eggs
  - 8 tbsp sugar
  - 8 tbsp ground walnuts
  - 2 tbsp flour
- group:
    sr: Fil
    en: Filling
  sr:
  - 1 i po l mleka
  - 6 pudinga od čokolade
  - šećer po ukusu
  - 1 margarin
  - 400 g čokolade
  - 500 g šlag kreme
  - 500 g slatke pavlake
  - 2-3 šake mlevenih oraha
  en:
  - 1.5 l milk
  - 6 chocolate pudding mixes
  - sugar to taste
  - 1 margarine block
  - 400 g chocolate
  - 500 g whipped topping
  - 500 g whipping cream
  - 2-3 handfuls ground walnuts
steps:
  sr:
  - Ispeći patišpanj.
  - U mleku sa šećerom skuvati pudinge; kad se ohladi dodati margarin i otopljenu čokoladu.
  - Posebno umutiti šlag kremu i slatku pavlaku pa ih sjediniti.
  - Dodati mlevene orahe i filovati.
  en:
  - Bake the sponge.
  - Cook the puddings in the sweetened milk; when cool add the margarine and melted chocolate.
  - Separately whip the topping and the cream then combine them.
  - Add the ground walnuts and assemble.
