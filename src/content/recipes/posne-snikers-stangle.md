  sr:

  - 250 g šećera u prahu
  - 300 g mlevenog kikirikija
  - 300 g mlevenog keksa
  - 1 šoljica soka

  en:
  - 250 g vegan margarine
  - 250 g powdered sugar
  - 300 g ground peanuts
  - 300 g ground biscuits
  - 1 small cup juice
  - honey
  - chopped peanuts
  - 200 g chocolate for glazing
steps:
  sr:
  - Umutiti margarin sa šećerom u prahu pa dodati kikiriki i keks i zamesiti sa sokom.
  - Oblikovati koru na tacni.
  - Premazati medom i posuti seckanim kikirikijem.
  - Preliti glazurom od čokolade i opet posuti kikirikijem.
  en:
  - Cream the margarine with the powdered sugar then add the peanuts and biscuits and bind with the juice.
  - Press into a layer on a tray.
  - Brush with honey and sprinkle with chopped peanuts.
  - Pour over the chocolate glaze and sprinkle with more peanuts.
