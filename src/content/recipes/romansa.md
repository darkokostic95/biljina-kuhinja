  sr: Romansa

category: kolaci
tags:

ingredients:
- group:
    sr: Testo
    en: Batter
  sr:
  - 2 jaja
  - 4 šoljice za kafu šećera
  - 2 šoljice zejtina
  - 2 šoljice mleka
  - 6 šoljica brašna
  - 1 prašak za pecivo
  - 1 kašičica kakaa
  en:
  - 2 eggs
  - 4 coffee cups sugar
  - 2 coffee cups oil
  - 2 coffee cups milk
  - 6 coffee cups flour
  - 1 baking powder
  - 1 tsp cocoa
- group:
    sr: Fil
    en: Topping
  sr:
  - 10 kašika mleka
  - 10 kašika šećera
  - pola margarina
  - 3-4 kašike kakaa
  - 100 g čokolade
  - 100 g kokosa
  en:
  - 10 tbsp milk
  - 10 tbsp sugar
  - half a margarine block
  - 3-4 tbsp cocoa
  - 100 g chocolate
  - 100 g coconut
steps:
  sr:
  - Umutiti sastojke za testo i ispeći.
  - Za fil zagrejati mleko, šećer, margarin, kakao i čokoladu pa dodati kokos (malo ostaviti za posipanje).
  - Premazati kolač i posuti kokosom.
  en:
  - Mix the batter and bake.
  - For the topping warm the milk, sugar, margarine, cocoa and chocolate then add the coconut (keep a little for sprinkling).
  - Spread over the cake and sprinkle with coconut.
